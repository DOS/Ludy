# Changelog

What changed in each release that reaches the pilot. A release is a `v*` tag of `main`, deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`; how to cut one is in [docs/product-owner-config.md](docs/product-owner-config.md) ("Branches, environments and gates"). Pull request numbers link the detail.

## v0.3.4 (2026-11-17)

From this release on, a deploy makes the context token key when the host's env file has none (#3284; `docs/runbooks/deploy.md`, "Production steps in the deploy"); a host that already has one logs `context token key present` and keeps it. After any deploy whose log says `context token key generated` (a failed deploy can make it, and the retry then says `present`), whoever holds the secret store copies the key from the host's env file into it. The deploy also prints the count of plain idempotency rows that #1882 waits for.

- **Per-org app switches** (#3317, #3357, #3376, #3395, #3399, #3400, #3414, #3458, #3497, #3505; ADR-0053): an owner turns an app on or off in Settings, "Ứng dụng". An app that is off answers not found, its worker stays quiet, its events wait and flow again when it is back on within the outbox retention window (a held event lives at most 30 days), and Vy, Home and MCP leave it out. Switching Meetings or Ads off is refused while their work is open. A Social post more than an hour late waits for a person.
- **Ludy eSign (Ký kết)** (#3410, #3432, #3500, #3516, #3520, #3528, #3533, #3565, #3568, #3351, #3331, #3380, #3515): envelopes, public signing links with email OTP, an approver step, reusable templates with signer, approver and CC roles and direct links, Vy's field placement and risky-clause summary, Engage quotes handed off for signing, and a Vietnam CA adapter package. The page is ludy.vn/esign. On the pilot the module runs only once it is enabled (`docs/runbooks/pilot-enable-sign.md`, #3537).
- **Projects** (#3358, #3386, #3390, #3392, #3396, #3402, #3438, #3446, #3455, #3498, #3504): a projects module with board and list, links to notes and to-dos, a Home feed of late and stuck work, Vy's weekly status shared as a note or email, tasks proposed from a note, meeting or conversation, checklists, recurring tasks, project templates, manual column order and saved views.
- **Notes** (#3509, #3517, #3521): a note line becomes a private to-do; notes link to meetings; an AI summary of a note with suggested to-dos.
- **Meetings** (#3280, #3287, #3303, #3279, #3340, #3448, #3449, #3454, #3466, #3484, #3501, #3524, #3538, #3545, #3546, #3570): minutes posted to Slack and Teams; recordings imported from meeting platforms; "Gửi qua Zalo"; the audio kept from an uploaded video; a recording that survives screen changes; names from the meeting sent with the audio so transcripts spell them right; speaker names suggested from the attendees; open items carried into the next meeting of a series with a nudge 30 minutes before it; Vy's brief before a meeting.
- **HR** (#3327, #3365, #3371, #3374, #3375, #3377, #3384, #3391, #3394, #3397, #3398, #3412, #3413, #3415, #3426, #3430, #3435, #3437, #3441, #3443): labor contracts and probation checks, severance and 13th-month calculators, timekeeping import with the 40-hour overtime cap, the annual leave ledger, departments and job titles, the labor register and 01/PLI and D02-LT exports with Ly's check, and a light recruiting pipeline with Ly's screening brief.
- **Finance** (#3316, #3328, #3342, #3347, #3353, #3360, #3364, #3424, #3487, #3539, #3544): marketplace settlements reworked (one voucher per delivery month, fees to 331, withheld tax credited, month-end fee accrual, reversal warnings); the MISA meInvoice adapter, built and off; a late sales invoice declared in the period of its sale, named in the coworker's summary.
- **CRM and Engage** (#3292, #3345, #3378, #3418, #3420, #3422, #3464, #3470, #3488, #3293): a contact inside the deal form, duplicate parties followed on deals, the quote PDF attached to its email, moving the deal after a quote, live updates of deals and contacts, links from the won and lost columns.
- **Social, Ads and Hộp thư** (#3298, #3469, #3296, #3346, #3301, #3383, #3382, #3419, #3467, #3465, #3468, #3482, #3295, #3302, #3421, #3461, #3299, #3473): unanswered comment counts on posts; the whole launch plan on the ad approval card, platform object states, committed against actual spend, months of spend without an invoice, the bank lines that paid the platform, a warning before a Meta token ends; a task from a conversation linked back to it; a disconnected channel's conversations deleted with it.
- **Platform** (#3277, #3305, #3242, #3475, #3201, #3368, #3367, #3379, #3387, #3389, #3273, #3343, #3282, #3284, #2704, #3496): `AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS` also binds extension tokens and MCP grants; the egress guard checks the first host and the origin on redirects; AI prices are compared as decimals; the owner export carries every module's tables under a coverage check; the deploy can write the identity provider settings for DOS sign-in once the pilot opts in (not set yet).

## v0.3.3 (2026-11-15)

- **Five apps in the app shell** (#3237, #3238): the apps are regrouped as Cộng tác (Việc nhóm, Ghi chú, Việc của tôi, Cuộc họp), Tài chính, Engage, Nhân sự and Ludy AI (Đội ngũ AI, Ngân sách AI, Kết nối AI), each shown only where its module runs.
- **Home: "Since yesterday", "Tuần này" and the coworker on each item** (#3260, Vy V3; #3261, Vy V6; #3276, Vy V2): each Home item says which AI coworker raised it ("Từ Ly" for finance, "Từ Dy" for CRM and conversations, "Từ Uy" for Social and Ads, and "Từ Vy" on the summary), with the name an owner gave the coworker. Also one line per AI coworker that did something for you in the last 24 hours, linking to the AI inbox, with counts only and no model call. A "Tuần này" card gives the last 7 days in figures computed by code, and Vy's sentences about them are checked against those figures.
- **Sign in with an outside identity provider** (#3222, #3226, #3231; Refs #2704): `AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS` limits sign-in to listed addresses, and provider buttons come from `AUTH_OAUTH_PROVIDERS`. A hosted login page (PKCE redirect, one-time code, the standard session) has a contract for an identity provider such as DOS ID. Wiring the pilot to DOS ID is an owner step in `docs/runbooks/pilot-sign-in-remote-idp.md`, not done yet.
- **Meetings: recording notice and consent** (#3195, A1; #3198, A2): a recording notice kit (Vietnamese and English, a draft pending the lawyer's reading, #2585) and an indicator that stays visible while recording. An org setting decides whether consent is required before recording (standard or strict).
- **Meetings: minutes and notes** (#3206, A4; #3220, A5; #3189): Vietnamese minutes templates and the biên bản page; rough notes that the recorder's Vy expands with "Viết lại đầy đủ"; and a recording rebuilt from its live pieces when the final upload never arrives.
- **Meetings: ask and translate** (#3239, A3; #3200, E1; #3208, E2): ask across meetings, with a citation to a transcript line on every sentence. A member can translate the summary (Vietnamese and English) and the transcript, privately, for themselves.
- **Meetings: calendars and the extension** (#3245, B1; #3268, B3; #3258, B2): a member can let Ludy read the names and times of the meetings in their own Google Calendar or Outlook calendar (off by default), so it can suggest "record this meeting". The extension's popup shows the next meetings ("Sắp họp") behind a new opt-in `agenda` permission.
- **Meetings: a member's signed webhook** (#3269, A6): when a member publishes minutes, Ludy can send them to the member's own HTTPS address, signed. The org owner turns this on (off by default), and the secret is shown once.
- **Browser extension** (#3274, D1; #3278, D2): reviewed against the Chrome Web Store 2026 policy. The extension now says where a recording goes, and its calendar and live-notes statements are corrected. Publishing on Microsoft Edge Add-ons is documented; the store steps are person steps.
- **Finance: marketplace settlements (Sàn)** (#3098, #3213, #3248, #3254, #3218, #3255; #2516): a stored marketplace settlement, its payout and the clearing of a platform's waiting fees on 1388 by its fee invoice are each posted as one journal voucher. The Sàn screen gets a work list (ask to post a settlement, record its payout, clear fees by an invoice) and one line from the finance coworker. Voucher tags are written only by their own command, and a bank line is held by one writer.
- **Finance: household business books** (#3154, #3155; #2517): the household business profile and the law as dated data, and the household sales book S1a from the ledger, with its screen and Excel export.
- **Hộp thư: WhatsApp, YouTube and Telegram bots** (#3146, #3142, #3141, #3232): WhatsApp Business (Cloud API) conversations; YouTube comments, under a daily quota budget; Telegram bot private chats; and a Telegram Business connection that Ludy missed is read back.
- **Vy flags a customer who waits for an answer on an open deal** (#3124, the fourth cross-app alert of Vy): on Home, a customer who wrote in Hộp thư 24 hours ago or more, has had no answer and still has an open deal gets one flag, with the hours waited and a link to the conversation. The count starts from the oldest message after your last answer. It shows only conversations the member may open (a private message or a channel they are limited away from gives them nothing), carries no word of the message, calls no model and writes nothing. The 24 hours is fixed, not a setting. `ludy_signals` lists the rule as not available to an AI app: a connected AI app cannot read conversations.
- **MCP: an AI app can write a Social draft for you to review** (#3227, step 3, the first MCP write; owner decision 2026-11-14): a company connection can now be given one extra permission, `ludy.social.draft`, a choice on the consent page that is off until you tick it and is shown only if the app asked for it. With it the app has one more tool, `ludy_social_draft_create`: it makes ONE draft post for the channels it names, marked as written by an AI and untrusted, that waits for a person to send it to review and approve it in Ludy (nobody is notified, nothing is scheduled or published; no media, no per-channel settings). A burst of six drafts, then one every ten seconds, for you (more for the organization); over that the app is told how long to wait. A connection that did not get the permission has no such tool, and a member's own connection can never hold it. The database lets the draft's own context insert that one draft and its channels and nothing else. No idempotency key: a retry makes a second draft, which the tool says.
- **MCP: Social posts and the review queue for an AI app** (#3227, step 2): two new read tools, `ludy_social_posts_list` (the org's posts with their channels, status, who wrote them, `tainted`, filters by status, channel and time) and `ludy_social_review_queue` (channels waiting for a decision and AI drafts not yet sent to review), under the existing read-only permission. The permission screen now says that the app reads the org's Social posts. Nothing writes: no tool drafts, approves, schedules, publishes or deletes yet.
- **Telegram photos as images in Hộp thư** (#3192, third slice of #3132): a customer's photo sent to the shop's bot, or in a business chat of a connected Telegram account, appears in the conversation as an image under the caption (or the "[Khách gửi ảnh]" line when there is none), instead of only a line. Ludy takes the largest size under 5 MiB and a background job fetches it from Telegram with the bot's token, which the job opens and lets go: the download address holds the token, so it is never stored, logged or put in an event or an audit row, and a failure is a short code. The type is read from the bytes; a document, sticker, video or voice message stays a line; a photo the owner sent from their own Telegram is not fetched. Not run against real Telegram yet: rows 31 to 34 of `docs/runbooks/telegram-conversations-live-check.md` (an owner action; row 32 needs a Telegram Premium account).
- **Telegram Business mode in Hộp thư** (#3191, second slice of #3132): an owner with Telegram Premium connects the shop's bot to their own account in Telegram (Settings, Business, Chatbots), and that account's customer chats come into Hộp thư as threads of their own, answered through the same bot as the owner's own message, only when a person presses Send, for 24 hours after the customer's last message. Dy drafts as for any chat; a business connection that is off, or that Ludy was never told of, stores nothing; what the owner typed in Telegram shows as already answered; a customer's edit and deletion change the stored words. The Channels card names the connected accounts and says how to connect in the order that works. Any Premium user can connect a bot that has Business Mode on, so an owner or admin allows each account (by its @username) before any of its chats is stored, and can stop it at once (a stopped, disconnected or read-only account is not answered, whatever was queued). No new switch: a connection whose chats were turned on before this version asks for the business updates after chats are turned off and on once. Also fixes a latent bug of the bot chats (#3141): a Telegram message id is unique inside one chat only, so the stored id now names the chat. Live check rows 18 to 30 in `docs/runbooks/telegram-conversations-live-check.md` need an owner with Telegram Premium.

## v0.3.2 (2026-11-14)

Before the first deploy of this release: link the GHCR package `ghcr.io/dos/ludy-media` to the repository for the deploy token (one time), or the deploy stops at pull and changes nothing. JOY linked it on 2026-11-14.

- **Telegram bot chats in Hộp thư** (#3132): an owner or admin turns on "receive customers' messages" on a Telegram connection; what customers write to the bot in a private chat appears in Hộp thư, Dy drafts a reply and a person presses Send (no auto-send), saved replies and `{tên}` work. The webhook is set with the bot token only the worker opens and is checked by a per-connection secret before anything is stored. Photos are a placeholder line for now, and Telegram Business mode follows; live check: [docs/runbooks/telegram-conversations-live-check.md](docs/runbooks/telegram-conversations-live-check.md).
- **Meetings: live notes** (L4a, ADR-0052, off by default, ticked per meeting at the consent step as "Ghi chú trực tiếp"): while a meeting is recorded, the extension or the app sends the audio in pieces of about 15 seconds, each sealed under its own key and kept only until the final recording arrives (#3109, #3117, #3118); the recorder's own Vy turns them into a live transcript on the meeting page, about 30 to 45 seconds behind the speech (not yet measured), and refreshes running notes (summary, decisions, action items as "Đề xuất" drafts) every 5 minutes or on a click; only the recorder sees them, nothing becomes a task without the recorder's click, each piece is admitted on its cost against the AI budget, a spent budget or the kill switch stops the notes and never the recording, it runs only while the meeting page is open, and the live text is replaced by the batch transcript and deleted with the meeting (#3110, #3119, #3121, #3123).
- **Bookings linked to deals** (#3103): a deal lists its upcoming and past bookings, "Đặt lịch" on a deal gives the client a booking link that carries the deal, and on the Lịch hẹn screen Dy suggests the guest's open deal by email or phone (no AI model) for a person to confirm; link and unlink are audited commands.
- **CRM, Dy's help**: Dy drafts the follow-up for a quiet deal or an unanswered quote for a person to send (#3101, #3107), and writes a meeting brief before a booking linked to a deal (#3122).
- **Hộp thư and Social**: Threads replies in Hộp thư (#3133, #3137); a contact name placeholder `{tên}` in saved replies (#3102, #3104).
- **Meetings, live notes** (opt-in per meeting): audio pieces are sent while the meeting is recorded (#3118), and the recorder's Vy shows a live transcript preview and running notes (L4a-2, #3123).
- **Accounting**: the marketplace connection kind in Tích hợp, the `MarketplaceProvider` port, an offline provider and polling into the staged path (#2519, #2838).
- **Docs**: the runbook for pilot channels beyond Meta and Zalo (#3111); the plan for WhatsApp and the popular messaging channels in Hộp thư (#3128, #3130); the Meetings line status of 2026-11-13 (#3127).

- **Landing page and README** (ludy.vn deploys from `main` already): below the hero, a five-step invoice story, app cards, a numbers band counted from the repository and a "your data stays yours" map, in plain words (#3018); the README gets the same pictures, the numbers with how each is counted, and an architecture diagram (#3014); social media covers for LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube and link previews (#3094).
- **Meetings**: link a meeting to a contact, a deal or a calendar event, linked meetings on deals and contacts, Vy's link suggestions read from the transcript without a model (L1, #3002 to #3005); a follow-up email after a meeting (L3, #3015, #3019); the length of a recording is measured by the media sandbox, never taken from the browser (#2987, #2992); the media sandbox image is built, published and pinned with each release (#2991); video playback hardening (#2982, #2983, #2986, #2993); a Vietnamese transcription accuracy eval (#2981).
- **MCP endpoint** (off unless `OAUTH_SERVER=on`): customer and invoice read tools (#2980, #2995), a daily row budget per connection (#2996), the Claude and ChatGPT connection guide (#2997), a receiver and a secret for the wake-up webhook of a connection (#3024, setup: #3030), and its delivery: a sweep every minute stores what is due (a schedule, a new signal) and a job posts it signed through the egress guard, retried with a bounded backoff (#3024, step b); owners and admins set it up, rotate its secret and send a test message from Settings under each connected AI app (#3040, #3041), and the receiver guide with a verification snippet and test vectors is [docs/runbooks/mcp-wake-webhook.md](docs/runbooks/mcp-wake-webhook.md) (#3038).
- **Your own AI app (member-bound connection)**: a member or viewer can connect their own AI app, with the org's owner switching it on (off by default, Settings, "Connected AI apps"), at most five connections each; it reads only what that member's role reads and never anything private to another member, and an owner or admin sees it in the list marked "Personal connection" (#3043, #3044, #3045 to #3049). The member can give it a wake-up message of its own (schedule and test, never signals) under the connection; an owner or admin sees only the receiver's host and stops it by disconnecting (#3051, #3053, #3054). When an owner or admin is demoted, every connection they made ends, their own included. No private tool exists yet.
- **MCP: company connections read nothing private** (#3069, #3071): a connection made by an owner or admin now runs in a sealed context with no user, like a member's own connection, so no private note or Vy table can reach an AI app; the tools return the same org data. Private tools for member connections are on hold by the owner's decision (2026-11-13).
- **MCP: "what needs attention" for an AI app** (#2967, #2969): a new read tool `ludy_signals` lists the cross-app checks (a won deal a week old with no sales invoice, a customer with an overdue invoice and an open deal) as ids, days and amounts, and says which checks it could not make (for example when the connection sees only one branch's books), so an empty list never reads as "all clear". Home shows the same two checks.
- **Signal wake-ups** (#2967, #2969): a company connection's wake-up webhook now sends `signal.fired` when a won deal a week old has no sales invoice or an overdue customer has an open deal (ids, a day and an amount only, checked every five minutes). A member's own connection still gets a schedule and a ping only. "A supplier's bank account changed" stays quiet until a supplier bank account record and a supplier-payment flow exist; the guide says so ([mcp-wake-webhook.md](docs/runbooks/mcp-wake-webhook.md)).
- **Pilot layout**: the API refuses the admin database login and a worker LISTEN url; the API container drops the worker-only settings and the archive volume, the worker drops the MCP server settings (#2999, #3001).
- **Browser extension**: Ludy icons, an English popup, a release zip from an `ext-v*` tag, the Chrome Web Store runbook (#3006, #3011, #3012); for the store listing, a localized manifest (Vietnamese by default, English too) (#3082, #3084), the store's extension id accepted by default (#3010, #3083), the store images with the script that regenerates them (#3087) and a public privacy page at `/privacy` (#3091).
- The usage page names every AI feature (#3021).
- **Meetings, more**: search across the meetings a member reads (L2, #3050); stop a transcription, and a member's transcription starts are serialized (#3033, #3057); each transcription chunk is admitted on its projected cost against the hard-stop budgets (#3067); one live member Vy run per feature, so a transcription is not cancelled by other Vy work (#3035); a run whose result is already ready is closed as succeeded, never cancelled beside it (#3060); the transcription eval with a Vietnamese WER and CER scorer (#2981); media handling hardened: a global video stream cap, one spelling per video ticket and a reserve-then-refund failure budget, stored media named by the sniffed container, an audit row for each live run a meeting delete cancels (#2982, #2983, #2986, #2993).
- **Erasure of personal data**: it also drops a meeting's follow-up mail copy and the follow-up mails whose own text mentions the value; a short digit value in meeting text is listed and kept instead of redacted (#3025, #3062, #3063, #3065, #3066, #3020, #3026).
- **Accounting and HR**: the Sàn screen and `finance.marketplace.balance`, the platform's money read from the ledger (#2516, slice D, #3096); a run whose posting was reversed can be posted again (#2931, #2938); the issue card says when the sales order of the draft was cancelled (#2849, #3099); the bank salary file can follow a saved template chosen on the export card (#2893, #2928).
- **Docs**: the runbook to turn on CRM and Social (with the inbox) on the pilot (#3100); ADR-0052, the meeting bot and live notes, proposed (#3068); the status of the Meetings line (#3022).
