# Changelog

What changed in each release that reaches the pilot. A release is a `v*` tag of `main`, deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`; how to cut one is in [docs/product-owner-config.md](docs/product-owner-config.md) ("Branches, environments and gates"). Pull request numbers link the detail.

## v0.3.4 (2026-11-17)

- **Ludy eSign: self-contained PDF signing, public ceremony and `ludy.vn/esign`** (#3321, #3380, #3410, #3429, #3432, #3500, #3515, #3533, #3568; Refs #3318, #3536): `Ludy eSign` (`eSign` in the app switcher in Vietnamese and English, with a public product page at `ludy.vn/esign` and `/en/esign`) joins as the sixth app, running locally on `docker compose up` with no outside provider account. `crove-extract` checks uploaded PDFs in a sandbox (`pdf_inspect`, up to 25 MB and 200 pages, refusing encrypted files and active scripts) and seals completed envelopes with SHA-256 (`pdf_seal`), an appended audit certificate page and an append-only hash-chained event trail; `/sign/verify` checks a sealed PDF locally in the browser without uploading it. Vy proposes signature, initial, date, text and checkbox fields and summarizes risky clauses with verbatim quote checks through the AI budget meter, marked `"Đề xuất"` for a person to apply; core signing works the same with AI off, and a person triggers every send. Recipients sign or approve on a mobile-friendly public page (`/-/sign#<token>`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, single-use replay guard, parallel or sequential routing, optional 6-digit email OTP locked after 5 wrong attempts, and no third-party CAPTCHA), with a dedicated Approver ceremony that confirms or declines without drawing a signature (#3533). Completion emails attach the sealed PDF and JSON audit certificate up to 18 MB (falling back to the certificate and SHA-256 notice when larger or unreadable, and alerting ops on any blob hash mismatch). Daily cleanup keeps draft envelopes intact while expiring OTP and token secrets with per-org error isolation, and personal-data erasure removes signers on unsealed envelopes (`draft`, `voided`, `expired`, `declined`) while keeping executed and sealed statutory records (#3515).
- **Ludy eSign: reusable templates and direct link setup** (#3565; Refs #3526): create reusable PDF templates at `/sign/templates` with `Signer`, `Approver` and `CC` role presets, normalized field layouts, one-click envelope creation that inherits the validated PDF metadata, and 1-signer direct link management (the public `/-/sign/d` signing ceremony follows in #3551).
- **Ludy eSign: Engage quote handoff and pluggable Vietnam CA engine** (#3516, #3520; Refs #3512, #3511): from a deal's quotes tab in Ludy Engage, a sent or draft quote (`crm_quote`) hands off to Ludy eSign with the customer's signer details prefilled, tracks live envelope status on the quote (warning when a draft quote changed after handoff) and links to the sealed PDF once signed (#3516). Behind the scenes, `@crove/sign` adds a pluggable Vietnam public CA adapter (`sandbox`, `VNPT SmartCA`, `Viettel MySign`, `MISA WeSign` over SSRF-pinned transport with async `reconcile_only` status checks) and `crove-extract` adds `pdf_embed_cms` for PAdES-LTV CMS signatures, RFC 3161 timestamps and `DSS`/`VRI` validation data (#3520); the live organization CA connection screen waits on provider test credentials and the lawyer's review (#62).
- **Per-org app switches (`Ứng dụng`)** (#3308, #3317, #3357, #3376, #3395, #3399, #3400, #3414, #3458, #3497, #3505; ADR-0053, Refs #3307): an owner or admin can turn optional apps on or off for their organization in Settings under `"Ứng dụng"` (read-only for members), with a confirmation naming what stays and what pauses (#3505). Turning an app off hides it from menus, Home, Vy wake-ups, morning mail, the AI inbox (which shows one count of hidden proposals) and MCP (`MODULE_OFF`), stops its streams, uploads, extension features and public pages (404), parks its outbox events until it is turned back on (#3414), and refuses to turn Meetings or Ads off while a recording, import, campaign or activation is open (#3400). Stop, revoke, erasure and Social takedown commands keep running while an app is off, and a scheduled Social post more than an hour past its time waits for a person instead of publishing on switch-on (#3458).
- **Deploy, identity provider sync and owner export** (#3277, #3284, #3305, #3368, #3379, #3387, #3389, #3475, #3496, #3537, #3567; Refs #2704, #3531, #3547, #3367): `deploy.yml` and `deploy.sh` gain two opt-in environment syncs that validate inputs with shell builtins, take a dated mode-600 `.env` backup, verify `/api/config` in smoke checks and restore the backup on rollback: `DEPLOY_MODULES_ENVIRONMENT` with `DEPLOY_CROVE_MODULES` to update `CROVE_MODULES` on the pilot host (enabling `sign` or `hr` on the pilot follows `docs/runbooks/pilot-enable-sign.md` and `docs/runbooks/pilot-enable-hr.md`, #3537, #3567), and `DEPLOY_IDP_*` with `AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS` to write remote identity provider settings (#3496). On deploy the pilot host also generates `CROVE_CONTEXT_TOKEN_KEY` if absent and logs the count of plain-hash idempotency rows (#3284). `AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS` now binds browser extension tokens and MCP grants on every use, code exchange and refresh without consuming a refused refresh token (#3277). - **Finance: marketplace settlements (Sàn)** (#3098, #3213, #3248, #3254, #3218, #3255; #2516): a stored marketplace settlement, its payout and the clearing of a platform's waiting fees on 1388 by its fee invoice are each posted as one journal voucher. - **Finance: household business books** (#3154, #3155; #2517): the household business profile and the law as dated data, and the household sales book S1a from the ledger, with its screen and Excel export.
- **Hộp thư: WhatsApp, YouTube and Telegram bots** (#3146, #3142, #3141, #3232): WhatsApp Business (Cloud API) conversations; YouTube comments, under a daily quota budget; Telegram bot private chats; and a Telegram Business connection that Ludy missed is read back.
- **Vy flags a customer who waits for an answer on an open deal** (#3124, the fourth cross-app alert of Vy): on Home, a customer who wrote in Hộp thư 24 hours ago or more, has had no answer and still has an open deal gets one flag, with the hours waited and a link to the conversation. The count starts from the oldest message after your last answer. It shows only conversations the member may open (a private message or a channel they are limited away from gives them nothing), carries no word of the message, calls no model and writes nothing. The 24 hours is fixed, not a setting. `ludy_signals` lists the rule as not available to an AI app: a connected AI app cannot read conversations.
- **MCP: an AI app can write a Social draft for you to review** (#3227, step 3, the first MCP write; owner decision 2026-11-14): a company connection can now be given one extra permission, `ludy.social.draft`, a choice on the consent page that is off until you tick it and is shown only if the app asked for it. With it the app has one more tool, `ludy_social_draft_create`: it makes ONE draft post for the channels it names, marked as written by an AI and untrusted, that waits for a person to send it to review and approve it in Ludy (nobody is notified, nothing is scheduled or published; no media, no per-channel settings). A burst of six drafts, then one every ten seconds, for you (more for the organization); over that the app is told how long to wait. A connection that did not get the permission has no such tool, and a member's own connection can never hold it. The database lets the draft's own context insert that one draft and its channels and nothing else. No idempotency key: a retry makes a second draft, which the tool says.
- **MCP: Social posts and the review queue for an AI app** (#3227, step 2): two new read tools, `ludy_social_posts_list` (the org's posts with their channels, status, who wrote them, `tainted`, filters by status, channel and time) and `ludy_social_review_queue` (channels waiting for a decision and AI drafts not yet sent to review), under the existing read-only permission. The permission screen now says that the app reads the org's Social posts. Nothing writes: no tool drafts, approves, schedules, publishes or deletes yet.
- **Telegram photos as images in Hộp thư** (#3192, third slice of #3132): a customer's photo sent to the shop's bot, or in a business chat of a connected Telegram account, appears in the conversation as an image under the caption (or the "[Khách gửi ảnh]" line when there is none), instead of only a line. Ludy takes the largest size under 5 MiB and a background job fetches it from Telegram with the bot's token, which the job opens and lets go: the download address holds the token, so it is never stored, logged or put in an event or an audit row, and a failure is a short code. The type is read from the bytes; a document, sticker, video or voice message stays a line; a photo the owner sent from their own Telegram is not fetched. Not run against real Telegram yet: rows 31 to 34 of `docs/runbooks/telegram-conversations-live-check.md` (an owner action; row 32 needs a Telegram Premium account).
- **Telegram Business mode in Hộp thư** (#3191, second slice of #3132): an owner with Telegram Premium connects the shop's bot to their own account in Telegram (Settings, Business, Chatbots), and that account's customer chats come into Hộp thư as threads of their own, answered through the same bot as the owner's own message, only when a person presses Send, for 24 hours after the customer's last message. Dy drafts as for any chat; a business connection that is off, or that Ludy was never told of, stores nothing; what the owner typed in Telegram shows as already answered; a customer's edit and deletion change the stored words. The Channels card names the connected accounts and says how to connect in the order that works. Any Premium user can connect a bot that has Business Mode on, so an owner or admin allows each account (by its @username) before any of its chats is stored, and can stop it at once (a stopped, disconnected or read-only account is not answered, whatever was queued). No new switch: a connection whose chats were turned on before this version asks for the business updates after chats are turned off and on once. Also fixes a latent bug of the bot chats (#3141): a Telegram message id is unique inside one chat only, so the stored id now names the chat. Live check rows 18 to 30 in `docs/runbooks/telegram-conversations-live-check.md` need an owner with Telegram Premium.
