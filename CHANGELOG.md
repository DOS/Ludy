# Changelog

What changed in each release that reaches the pilot. A release is a `v*` tag of `main`, deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`; how to cut one is in [docs/product-owner-config.md](docs/product-owner-config.md) ("Branches, environments and gates"). Pull request numbers link the detail.

## v0.3.2 (2026-11-14)

Before the first deploy of this release: link the GHCR package `ghcr.io/dos/ludy-media` to the repository for the deploy token (one time), or the deploy stops at pull and changes nothing. JOY linked it on 2026-11-14.

- **Meetings: live notes** (L4a, ADR-0052, off by default, ticked per meeting at the consent step as "Ghi chú trực tiếp"): while a meeting is recorded, the extension or the app sends the audio in pieces of about 15 seconds, each sealed under its own key and kept only until the final recording arrives (#3109, #3117, #3118); the recorder's own Vy turns them into a live transcript on the meeting page, about 30 to 45 seconds behind the speech (not yet measured), and refreshes running notes (summary, decisions, action items as "Đề xuất" drafts) every 5 minutes or on a click; only the recorder sees them, nothing becomes a task without the recorder's click, each piece is admitted on its cost against the AI budget, a spent budget or the kill switch stops the notes and never the recording, it runs only while the meeting page is open, and the live text is replaced by the batch transcript and deleted with the meeting (#3110, #3119, #3121, #3123).
- **Bookings linked to deals** (#3103): a deal lists its upcoming and past bookings, "Đặt lịch" on a deal gives the client a booking link that carries the deal, and on the Lịch hẹn screen Dy suggests the guest's open deal by email or phone (no AI model) for a person to confirm; link and unlink are audited commands.
- **CRM, Dy's help**: Dy drafts the follow-up for a quiet deal or an unanswered quote for a person to send (#3101, #3107), and writes a meeting brief before a booking linked to a deal (#3122).
- **Hộp thư and Social**: Threads replies in Hộp thư (#3133, #3137); a contact name placeholder `{tên}` in saved replies (#3102, #3104).
- **Meetings, live notes** (opt-in per meeting): audio pieces are sent while the meeting is recorded (#3118), and the recorder's Vy shows a live transcript preview and running notes (L4a-2, #3123).
- **Accounting**: the marketplace connection kind in Tích hợp, the `MarketplaceProvider` port, an offline provider and polling into the staged path (#2519, #2838).
- **Docs**: the runbook for pilot channels beyond Meta and Zalo (#3111); the plan for WhatsApp and the popular messaging channels in Hộp thư (#3128, #3130); the Meetings line status of 2026-11-13 (#3127).

- **Landing page and README** (ludy.vn deploys from `main` already): below the hero, a five-step invoice story, app cards, a numbers band counted from the repository and a "your data stays yours" map, in plain words (#3018); the README gets the same pictures, the numbers with how each is counted, and an architecture diagram (#3014); social media covers for LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube and link previews (#3094).
- **Meetings**: link a meeting to a contact, a deal or a calendar event, linked meetings on deals and contacts, Vy's link suggestions read from the transcript without a model (L1, #3002 to #3005); a follow-up email after a meeting (L3, #3015, #3019); the length of a recording is measured by the media sandbox, never taken from the browser (#2987, #2992); the media sandbox image is built, published and pinned with each release (#2991); video playback hardening (#2982, #2983, #2986, #2993); a Vietnamese transcription accuracy eval (#2981).
- **MCP endpoint** (off unless `OAUTH_SERVER=on`): customer and invoice read tools (#2980, #2995), a daily row budget per connection (#2996), the Claude and ChatGPT connection guide (#2997), a receiver and a secret for the wake-up webhook of a connection (#3024, setup: #3030), and its delivery: a sweep every minute stores what is due (a schedule, a new signal) and a job posts it signed through the egress guard, retried with a bounded backoff (#3024, step b); owners and admins set it up, rotate its secret and send a test message from Settings under each connected AI app (#3040, #3041), and the receiver guide with a verification snippet and test vectors is [docs/runbooks/mcp-wake-webhook.md](docs/runbooks/mcp-wake-webhook.md) (#3038).
- **Your own AI app (member-bound connection)**: a member or viewer can connect their own AI app, with the org's owner switching it on (off by default, Settings, "Connected AI apps"), at most five connections each; it reads only what that member's role reads and never anything private to another member, and an owner or admin sees it in the list marked "Personal connection" (#3043, #3044, #3045 to #3049). The member can give it a wake-up message of its own (schedule and test, never signals) under the connection; an owner or admin sees only the receiver's host and stops it by disconnecting (#3051, #3053, #3054). When an owner or admin is demoted, every connection they made ends, their own included. No private tool exists yet.
- **MCP: company connections read nothing private** (#3069, #3071): a connection made by an owner or admin now runs in a sealed context with no user, like a member's own connection, so no private note or Vy table can reach an AI app; the tools return the same org data. Private tools for member connections are on hold by the owner's decision (2026-11-13).
- **MCP: "what needs attention" for an AI app** (#2967, #2969): a new read tool `ludy_signals` lists the cross-app checks (a won deal a week old with no sales invoice, a customer with an overdue invoice and an open deal) as ids, days and amounts, and says which checks it could not make (for example when the connection sees only one branch's books), so an empty list never reads as "all clear". Home shows the same two checks.
- **Signal wake-ups** (#2967, #2969): a company connection's wake-up webhook now sends `signal.fired` when a won deal a week old has no sales invoice or an overdue customer has an open deal (ids, a day and an amount only, checked every five minutes). A member's own connection still gets a schedule and a ping only. "A supplier's bank account changed" stays quiet until a supplier bank account record and a supplier-payment flow exist; the guide says so ([mcp-wake-webhook.md](docs/runbooks/mcp-wake-webhook.md)).
- **Pilot layout**: the API refuses the admin database login and a worker LISTEN url; the API container drops the worker-only settings and the archive volume, the worker drops the MCP server settings (#2999, #3001).
- **Browser extension**: Ludy icons, an English popup, a release zip from an `ext-v*` tag, the Chrome Web Store runbook (#3006, #3011, #3012); for the store listing, a localized manifest (Vietnamese by default, English too) (#3082, #3084), the store's extension id accepted by default (#3010, #3083), the store images with the script that regenerates them (#3087) and a public privacy page at `/privacy` (#3091).
- The usage page names every AI feature (#3021).
- **Meetings, more**: search across the meetings a member reads (L2, #3050); stop a transcription, and a member's transcription starts are serialized (#3033, #3057); each transcription chunk is admitted on its projected cost against the hard-stop budgets (#3067); one live member Vy run per feature, so a transcription is not cancelled by other Vy work (#3035); a run whose result is already ready is closed as succeeded, never cancelled beside it (#3060); the transcription eval with a Vietnamese WER and CER scorer (#2981); media handling hardened: a global video stream cap, one spelling per video ticket and a reserve-then-refund failure budget, stored media named by the sniffed container, an audit row for each live run a meeting delete cancels (#2982, #2983, #2986, #2993).
- **Erasure of personal data**: it also drops a meeting's follow-up mail copy and the follow-up mails whose own text mentions the value; a short digit value in meeting text is listed and kept instead of redacted (#3025, #3062, #3063, #3065, #3066, #3020, #3026).
- **Accounting and HR**: the Sàn screen and `finance.marketplace.balance`, the platform's money read from the ledger (#2516, slice D, #3096); a run whose posting was reversed can be posted again (#2931, #2938); the issue card says when the sales order of the draft was cancelled (#2849, #3099); the bank salary file can follow a saved template chosen on the export card (#2893, #2928).
- **Docs**: the runbook to turn on CRM and Social (with the inbox) on the pilot (#3100); ADR-0052, the meeting bot and live notes, proposed (#3068); the status of the Meetings line (#3022).

## v0.3.1 (2026-11-11)

The first release on the pilot since v0.2.0 (v0.3.0 was rolled back on 2026-11-08: the API container turned unhealthy at start; the cause read from the code, compose defaulting the e-invoice and ads sandboxes on, is fixed in #2974 and v0.3.1 boots).

- **Vy**: the plan and ADR-0051 (Vy's brain is a DOS Claw agent, Ludy is the gate; Cloud includes one Vy, other coworkers are add-ons) (#2944, #2948, #2971); the kernel half of the first cross-app alerts, V4 and V5 (#2968, #2970); member-scoped Vy (ADR-0049) finished: audit allowlist, run bounds, sinks ratchet, daily sweeps of private data (#2822 and its clean-up PRs).
- **MCP endpoint 1a** (off by default): OAuth 2.1 authorization server, consent screen, `agent_grant`, read-only `/mcp` with `ludy_about`, audit "via <app> (MCP)" (#2976, #2977, #2978).
- **Meetings**: summary, decisions and action items by hand and from the recorder's Vy (M6, #2876 to #2909), personal-data finder and erasure in meeting text (L5), video by signed ticket (#2964), transcription with Gemini through `chat_input_audio` (#2973), the switch-on runbook (#2958).
- **Payroll and Accounting**: post and pay a month that carries corrections, the bank salary file per bank and by saved template, payroll payments offered on bank lines (#2532 and its PRs, #2893, #2897); marketplace income import and settlement proposals (#2516 slices).
- **Storage**: a retention delete tags the object version so a lifecycle rule expires it (#2959).
- **Fix**: the API boots on a public deployment with the compose defaults (#2974).
