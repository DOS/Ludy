## The texts in force

- **E-invoices:** Decree 254/2026/ND-CP (30/06/2026, in force 01/07/2026) replaces Decree 123/2020, Art. 1 of Decree 41/2022 and Decree 70/2025 (its Art. 43.2). Circular 91/2026/TT-BTC (in force 01/07/2026) replaces Circular 32/2025 (its Art. 25.2). Read from the signed Decree 254 scan; Circular 91 from a full-text copy. Any spec citing Decree 123 or Circular 78 article numbers is outdated.
- **Platform withholding:** Decree 252/2026/ND-CP replaces Decree 117/2025 from 01/07/2026 (Chapter III, Art. 40 to 46). Secondary sources only.
- **Household businesses:** Decree 68/2026/ND-CP (05/03/2026) as amended by Decree 141/2026/ND-CP; books under Circular 152/2025/TT-BTC; returns under Circular 50/2026/TT-BTC and Circular 89/2026/TT-BTC. Read from full-text reproductions.
- **VAT reduction:** Decree 174/2025/ND-CP, from 01/07/2025 to 31/12/2026 (Art. 2.1, signed scan). No extension into 2027 was found; re-check in December 2026.
- **PIT reduction:** Decree 398/2026/ND-CP (in force 29/10/2026, tax years 2026 and 2027), a 30% cut of PIT payable for resident business individuals with revenue up to 10 billion VND. Secondary sources only.
- **Enterprise accounting:** Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC; SMEs may keep Circular 133/2016; micro enterprises: Circular 58/2026/TT-BTC (in force 01/07/2026, replaces Circular 132/2018). Accounting Law 88/2015/QH13. VAT: Decree 181/2025/ND-CP as amended by Decrees 359/2025 and 144/2026.

| 1. Invoices sent in a summary table are corrected in the next table instead (not Ludy's case: Ludy issues coded invoices). | Circular 91 Art. 10.1.a, b, đ, 10.3, 10.4; Appendix III. | 6. Buyer CCCD or passport | The buyer field is name, address, tax code **or personal identification number** (Decree 254 Art. 10.1.d). When a consumer gives a name, address or ID, the invoice **must show it**; when the buyer gives nothing, the invoice says "Bán cho người tiêu dùng". A foreign buyer's address and ID may be replaced by **passport or entry-exit document number and nationality**. Only cash-register invoices use "if the buyer asks". | Decree 254 Art. 10.1.d, Appendix point 4.b, 9.c, 9.l, 9.n. | 7. An 8% invoice corrected in 2027 | Decree 174 has **no clause** for this. The precedent for the 2022 to 2023 changeover (Official Letter 2121/TCT-CS, 29/05/2023): a correction that does not change the goods value or the VAT, a return, or a trade discount set later **keeps 8%**; an error in **quantity** that changes the amount and the VAT uses **the rate in force when the adjusting or replacing invoice is made** (10% in 2027). No equivalent letter for 2026 to 2027 yet. | Decree 174 Art. 1.5 and 2.1 (signed scan, high); Official Letter 2121/TCT-CS (reproductions, medium) | Correction (#916, #2504): the rate follows the original only for corrections that keep the amount or for returns and discounts; a quantity error uses the rate on the correction date. The correction screen names the precedent while no 2027 guidance exists. Issue #3709 |
| 8. Art. 15 and Art. 19 | **Art. 15**: duties of the seller using coded invoices (keep the tax-authority account credentials, be responsible for the invoice's accuracy, send the coded invoice to the buyer as soon as the code returns). **Art. 19**: duties of an **authorized issuer** (ủy nhiệm) and, in cl. 2, of **e-commerce and digital platform operators** (give the seller the buyer and order data). Neither binds an app that calls the seller's own provider account. The line to stay behind is Art. 3.9 and 20.1 with Circular 91 Art. 12.1: a "solution provider" that creates, signs, transmits or stores the legal XML has duties of its own. | Decree 254 Art. 3.9, 15, 19, 20.1; Circular 91 Art. 9, 12.1. ## Selling through platforms (#2520 part A and D)

| # | Answer | Source and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Payout: Dr 112 (net), Dr 331 platform (fees withheld) / Cr 131 platform. Fee invoice: Dr 641 (TT99, sub-account 6417) or 6421 (TT133), Dr 1331 / Cr 331. Month end without an invoice: Dr 641 or 6421 / Cr 335, released when the invoice comes. Not 1388. Platforms may invoice their fees after reconciling with the customer (Decree 254), so the accrual is common. | Standard practice, TT99 Appendix II chart (secondary). High on the entries |
| A2 | Fees carry VAT 10%, 8% until 31/12/2026 (Decree 174 Art. 1), except excluded groups (finance, insurance): take the rate printed on the invoice, never compute it. For input VAT on a purchase of 5 million VND or more, an offset counts as non-cash payment only when the contract provides for it and both sides confirm the reconciliation (Decree 181 Art. 26.2.a); a remainder of 5 million VND or more paid in money must be paid non-cash (26.2.đ). Keep the platform's terms of service and the confirmed settlement statements. | Decree 181 Art. 26 (full text, high); that platform netting qualifies: medium-low, no ruling found |
| A3 | Never refuse a valid fee invoice. Book it in full to 331; a larger invoice (ads top-ups, other periods) leaves a 331 balance offset in the next settlement, or paid by bank transfer when no netting will come (keeps the VAT deductible). Flag a balance older than one cycle. | Decree 181 Art. 26.2.a and đ. Medium (practice) |
| A4 | Platforms withhold VAT and PIT **for households and individuals only**; a company is never withheld and declares itself. From 01/07/2026: Decree 252 Art. 43 to 46 (withholding when the platform confirms the order successful and accepts payment; the highest rate when goods and services cannot be told apart). The withheld revenue **still counts** toward the 1 billion VND threshold (Decree 68 Art. 8.1.b as amended) and the annual PIT finalization (Art. 11.3), with the withheld PIT credited; below the threshold the withheld tax is a refund (Art. 12.1, form 01/TKN-CNKD). Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada revenue is not re-declared on the quarterly 01/CNKD. A household has no chart of accounts (Circular 152): VAT and PIT are separate lines of its book; household PIT is the owner's tax, **not a business expense** (Decree 68 Art. 6), so an internal ledger books it to owner's equity or drawings. | Decree 68 and 141 (full text, high); Decree 252 article numbers (secondary, medium) |
| A5 | Revenue when control passes, normally successful delivery; not the settlement or payout date. Where returns cannot be estimated, at the end of the return window ("order completed"). Invoice timing: Decree 254 Art. 9; household revenue: Decree 68 Art. 5.3.a. | VAS 14 (paragraphs from memory, medium); principle high |
| A6 | Never edit or delete a posted entry: correct it with an adjustment voucher linked to the original (Accounting Law 88/2015 **Art. 27**; 27.4 for electronic books). Errors found before the annual statements are filed are fixed in that year; later, in the year found, with disclosure. A closed period is corrected in the open one; a change to tax already declared flags a supplementary declaration. | Accounting Law Art. 26.6, 27 (full text, high) |
| A7 | Per period: the settlement and income report matched order by order, and the fee invoices. Yearly: the electronic withholding certificate (Decree 117 Art. 7.2 form 01/CTKT-TMĐT until 30/06/2026; then Decree 252, digital signature under Decree 254). Lazada's WHT field still waits for a real Vietnamese seller export (#2521). | Decree 117 (high); the Decree 252 article (low-medium) |
| D1 | Split a single withheld amount into VAT and PIT **only as a flagged estimate**: in 2026 the ratio is not fixed (VAT cut by 20%, PIT payable cut by 30%, the highest rate when unclassifiable). Better: compute each tax as revenue times the rate the platform applied for the category and period, carry the remainder as a reconciliation difference, and replace the estimate with the annual certificate, which shows both. | Decree 117 Art. 5.2, Decree 174, Decree 398. Medium |
| D2 | An enterprise on the direct VAT method: Dr 511 / Cr 3331 at period end, then pays it itself. A household: no accounts; VAT and PIT lines in S2a (or S2b and S2c), PIT against owner's equity in an internal ledger, never an expense. | Medium |
| D3 | Not the ship date by default. Fallback order: successful delivery date; the platform's "order completed / confirmed successful" date (also the withholding point, so revenue and withheld tax line up); the ship date only as a flagged proxy with a month-end cut-off for orders in transit. | Judgment, low-medium |

## What changes, and the build issues

1. **Household tax (#2517, #2518):** add the digital-content trade (PIT 5%); model the 2026 VAT cut (20% of the percentage, until 31/12/2026) and the Decree 398 PIT cut (30% of PIT payable, 2026 and 2027, revenue up to 10 billion VND) as dated rows applied to the tax, never constants; S2a and 01/CNKD show the reduction on its own line. Rows backed by a high-confidence primary text move from `commentary` to `confirmed` with this page as the source; the rest keep the provisional label. Issue #3710.
2. **Marketplace posting (#2519 and the Sàn screen):** the A1 to A5 and D1 to D3 answers: fees through 331 and 335, not 1388; household withholding split into VAT and PIT lines with PIT never an expense; revenue on delivery with the "order completed" fallback; a combined withholding split only as a flagged estimate. Posting stays off until this lands. Issue #3711.
3. **E-invoice issue (#2504, #3681):** nationality with a passport; "Bán cho người tiêu dùng" when the buyer gives nothing; the 2027 correction rate per Official Letter 2121; the 04/SS step says "before the VAT return" instead of a day count. Issue #3709.
4. **Stock (#2512, #2513):** sales returns reduce revenue and output VAT (521 under Circular 99, 511 under Circular 133), not only cost; stocktake surplus to 3381 then 711; under Circular 99, a 1381 balance without evidence expensed at the reporting date. Issue #3712.

Still open for a person (after the cross-check below): the 2027 rate of a correction to an 8% invoice (#2506 question 7: no 2027 guidance, and the earlier AI reading said the original rate is always kept); D1 to D3 (one research pass only, not in the questions put to ChatGPT and Gemini); any extension of the 8% VAT rate decided in December 2026.
