# Ludy Meetings integrations: plan (2026-11-14)

> Plan for the integrations around [Ludy Meetings](meetings.md) (epic [#932](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/932)). Owner decision of 2026-11-14 in the Meetings PO chat: the owner approved all four groups the research report recommended (A to D below). The same day the owner added a fifth, **group E, translation of the transcript and the summary** ("Tính năng dịch transcript và summary nữa chứ nhỉ", relayed by the Meetings PO). The research behind it is [meetings-research.md](meetings-research.md) (English notes) and [meetings-research.vi.md](meetings-research.vi.md) (the Vietnamese report for the owner). A plain Vietnamese summary for the owner is [meetings-integrations.vi.md](meetings-integrations.vi.md). No code is written by this plan; it derives the build issues of section 11.
>
> **Caveat from the research, which holds here too:** no vendor page could be opened when the research ran; every fact about Google, Zoom, Microsoft, Slack, Zalo, Chrome and Edge comes from search-result summaries, and every price is unverified. Each build issue below starts by re-checking its facts on the vendor's own page and writes what it found on the issue ("Facts to re-check" in each group). A fact marked **[recheck]** is one the build must not rely on before that. The Vietnamese legal points stay commentary until a lawyer confirms them (meetings.md section 7, #2585).

## 2. The scope gate, applied once to the whole plan

[AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and [scope.md](../product/scope.md) set the gate. For every group the plan says the four things, and each build issue repeats them:

- **Fit.** The user is an owner or salesperson of a small business who meets several times a month and wants the result of a meeting to reach the people who need it. Groups A and D are used by every recorder. Group B and C are used only by those who already live in Google, Microsoft, Zoom, Slack or Zalo, so each is **optional and off by default**; Ludy Meetings stays whole with none of them (extension, microphone, upload).
- **Smallest slice.** One person, the defaults, no setup beyond a connection or a pasted address, one screen per integration. No settings framework; an org setting only where the law or money needs a choice (the consent policy; the webhook switch).
- **AI part, named.** Each group says what the AI coworker does, or "none, on purpose". Meetings is Vy's tab in Ludy Notes, so the coworker is Vy (a member's own Vy, ADR-0049). Every model call goes through `metered()` under `withAiScope` with a key in `AI_FEATURES` counted against the `meetings` module budget; the core works with AI off; AI output is tainted and code decides.
- **A person approves money, messages and spend.** Nothing in this plan starts a recording, joins a call, imports a recording, posts a message or spends money without a person's click. A calendar event only **suggests**; a platform webhook only **suggests** an import; a post to Slack, Teams or Zalo is the recorder's own click.

**One tension with the gate, flagged for the PO.** scope.md names "calendar providers few small businesses use (Microsoft 365, CalDAV)" as Icebox-level fit. The owner's 2026-11-14 approval names Outlook explicitly, so it is planned, but as its own step (B3) after Google (B1, B2), so it could be moved to P3 without touching the rest. **Decided by the Meetings PO on 2026-11-14: B3 stays P2** (the owner approved group B as a whole).

## 4. Group A: learn from competitors (M3 Paid pilot, P2)

No outside account, no cost beyond the AI budget the org already has. Six steps (A1 to A6).

### 4.1 A1 and A2: the notice, the policy and the recording indicator

- **What a user sees.** In the consent step (app and extension popup) a button "Sao chép thông báo ghi âm" puts a short Vietnamese text on the clipboard (English one click away, the meeting's language is the default), to paste in the call's chat: "Cuộc họp này đang được ghi âm để lập biên bản. Nếu ai không đồng ý, xin báo ngay để chúng tôi dừng ghi." The recorder then ticks how people were told; "đã dán thông báo vào khung chat cuộc họp" is a new, written, method. The recording indicator: the extension badge shows "REC" while recording (built) and the app header shows "Đang ghi 12:31" on every screen while a recording of this member runs, from the app or from the extension (the extension's status is already readable, `meetings.meeting.extension_status`); a recording that stops unexpectedly (tab closed, permission lost, mic lost) turns into a visible "Ghi âm đã dừng lúc 12:44" on the meeting. The research's lesson: tools that fail silently lose the meeting and its trust.
- **Org setting (A2), "Cần xác nhận trước khi ghi".** Two values for the owner or admin. *Standard* is today (any method the meeting offers). *Strict*: the recorder must pick a written method (chat notice, the invite, agreed in writing); "said at the start" alone is refused by the server; and the extension popup shows the notice on every start with no remembered choice. **Today the server already refuses audio without a consent record** (meetings.md section 7), so the setting only tightens what counts; the Meetings PO decided on 2026-11-14 that nothing more is meant (section 10). A change applies to meetings recorded afterwards, like the retention settings.
- **Smallest slice.** One button, one new method, one chip, one setting with two values.
- **The AI part: none, on purpose.** A notice is a legal act, not a draft. The text is fixed, versioned and shown the same to everyone.
- **What a person approves.** The recorder's own confirmation, as today; the owner or admin sets the policy. Nothing is sent to the call by Ludy: the person pastes the text.
- **Data and consent rules.** The notice text has a version (`consent_notice_version`); the version of the text copied is stored on the meeting. The audit row records codes only. The new text and the strict policy are **commentary until a lawyer reads them** (#2585); the issue ships the text marked as pending that review and links #2585. No voiceprint, no per-participant tracking.
- **Owner actions.** None to build. The lawyer's reading of the notice text rides on #2585.
- **Facts to re-check.** The wording of Vietnamese notice sentences against Law 91/2025 and Decree 356/2025 (lawyer). Whether Zoom, Meet and Teams show their own banners to people when Ludy records a tab (they do not: Ludy is not the platform's recorder), which is exactly why the paste step exists.
- **Protected paths.** A1: none (app and extension source; if the manifest permission list changes, the CI check of the permission list fails, which it must not). A2: a migration and the settings command (`supabase/migrations/`, `packages/kernel-runtime` only if the consent check lives there).

### 4.2 A3: ask across meetings, with citations

- **What a user sees.** A box above the meetings list: "Hỏi về các cuộc họp". The member types "Khách A đã đồng ý chiết khấu bao nhiêu?"; Vy answers in two to six sentences, and every sentence carries a chip "Họp giao ban, 14/11, 12:31" that opens the transcript at that line.
- **Smallest slice.** One box, one answer, no history kept. It uses the existing folded search (L2) to pick the lines and sends only those lines (at most 40 lines from at most 8 meetings, the meeting titles and dates) to one model call. The card shows how much of the AI budget is left before the member asks (research: credit walls are the top complaint; Ludy never buys more).
- **The AI part.** Vy reads the retrieved lines and writes the answer with citations. Feature key `meetings.ask` (module `meetings`). It runs as the asker's own Vy (foreground, ADR-0049 stage A), so it reads exactly what the asker can read and nothing else. Checks in code, before anything shows: every citation names a line that was in the retrieved set and that the asker can read; each quoted phrase is a substring of its line; the number check of #1237 (a figure in the answer must appear in a cited line); an answer with no valid citation is replaced by "Không tìm thấy trong các cuộc họp bạn đọc được" and the hit list of the search. Transcript text is untrusted: an instruction inside a line stays data (red-team transcripts in CI with a fake model, like M6).
- **What a person approves.** Nothing leaves the screen: the answer is not stored, not sent and creates no task. A "Tạo việc" from an answer is not offered in this slice.
- **Data and consent rules.** Only meetings the asker reads (row-level security on every row). Text goes to the model provider like the summary does; the deployment's provider list already names it. The audit row hashes the question and holds counts and ids.
- **Owner actions and facts to re-check.** None. Re-check only how the model's context limit holds 40 lines.
- **Protected paths.** None: no migration is needed (nothing is stored), and `packages/agent/**`, `modules/meetings/**`, `packages/kernel-contract` and `apps/app` are not on the protected list.

### 4.3 A4: Vietnamese minutes templates and the biên bản page

- **What a user sees.** When creating or opening a meeting the recorder picks a type: *Tóm tắt chung* (today), *Họp giao ban*, *Họp khách hàng*, *Họp với nhà cung cấp*, *Họp 1:1*. After the result is reviewed, "Xem biên bản" shows a page in the layout Vietnamese offices use: header with the company name, "BIÊN BẢN CUỘC HỌP", time, place, attendees, content, conclusions, action items with owner and date, and a signature block; the browser prints it to PDF.
- **Smallest slice.** Five templates shipped in code, no editor, no org-made templates (the report's Meet AI+ sells "your company's own template"; that is later and stays in the Icebox until a pilot asks). The page is HTML with a print style; no new document library.
- **The AI part.** The template chooses the section list and gives Vy section hints; the **same** `meetings.summary` run fills the sections from the transcript (no new feature key), with the checks of meetings.md section 5.3. Date, place, attendee names, owner and due date come from the meeting row and code, never from the model. The layout is deterministic code.
- **What a person approves.** The recorder reviews and publishes as today (`meetings.result.publish`); the biên bản page shows only what is published and says "Bản nháp" until then. The signature block is blank for people to sign on paper or by their own e-signature tool; Ludy does not sign for anyone.
- **Data and consent rules.** No new data leaves; the template name is a code on the meeting. The page is readable by the meeting's readers only.
- **Owner actions and facts to re-check.** One person to read the five layouts for Vietnamese office custom (an accountant or an office manager at the pilot); the issue asks for it in a comment, it is not a blocker. Re-check: no legal form is required for an internal biên bản of a small business; a lawyer can say so at #2585.
- **Protected paths.** A migration for the template code on the meeting is the only protected piece (`supabase/migrations/`), done first in a small PR or together if the reviewer agrees.
- **A4 as built (#3162).** The only column is `crove_meetings.meeting.summary_template` (a code: `general`, `briefing`, `customer`, `supplier`, `one_on_one`; a CHECK and the default `general`). The five templates, their section keys, headings and hints for Vy are code in `packages/kernel-contract/src/meeting-templates.ts`. The sections' text is **not** a new column: `sectionsToText` writes the filled sections into the existing `meeting_result.summary` as the template's headings and paragraphs (and `textToSections` reads them back by the headings), so the translation, the follow-up mail, the search, the personal-data finders and the erasure keep covering it with no change. The recorder picks the template at create (`meetings.meeting.create`, default general) or later with `meetings.minutes.set_template` (recorder only; the column UPDATE grant is for that command); a result already saved keeps its text, and the page shows text that no longer matches the template's headings whole. The same `meetings.summary` run asks for `{sections, decisions, items}` when the meeting has a template; the checks of `checkSummary` apply to each section line by line (a figure the transcript lacks or a link, email or phone removes the sentence; a key the template does not have is dropped; at most 1200 characters a section). `meetings.summary.copy_result` writes the draft through `sectionsToText` and refuses a result made for another template than the meeting's now (`SUMMARY_TEMPLATE_CHANGED`). With AI off, `meetings.summary.save` takes the sections typed by hand (`TEMPLATE_MISMATCH` for a mix of a summary and sections). "Xem biên bản" is the screen `print/meeting-minutes/$id`, outside the org's frame like the content plan's print page, reading `meetings.get` and `meetings.result.get` (so only the meeting's readers read it, the recorder a draft beside the published result); it shows only the published result and says "Bản nháp" until then. The date and time (Vietnam time), the attendees (the recorder, then the readers, by their names), each owner (the attendee code matched) and each due date (the date code parsed) come from the meeting and code: an owner or date code did not find prints as a dotted line, never as the words the model heard. The place is a field on the page, typed before printing and **not saved** (the meeting row has no place, and a stored place would be new personal data for the erasure to cover; a saved place is an Icebox follow-up if a pilot asks). The signature block is blank. The translation of a templated summary translates it as one text, headings included. Known limits, by design of this slice: the live notes (L4a) do not use the template, so their draft is one free text that the editor puts into the first section and the page prints as one block; changing the template after a result was shared leaves that text as it was and the page shows it whole; a run that fills no section on a template stores a bare dash as its summary and its decisions stay the conclusions.

### 4.4 A5: enhance my notes

- **What a user sees.** During or after the meeting the recorder types rough lines in a "Ghi chú của tôi" box (live notes already run beside it). "Viết lại đầy đủ" asks Vy to expand them. The result shows each of the member's own lines unchanged in one style and each added line in another, "Vy thêm từ bản ghi", with a chip to its transcript line.
- **Smallest slice.** One box, one button, one draft in the recorder's own Vy result. The member's rough notes are stored on the meeting (a recorder-only column).
- **The AI part.** Vy merges the member's notes with the transcript into the chosen template's sections (the Granola loop). Feature key `meetings.summary` (same run family as A4, so one budget line). Checks: every added statement links to a real transcript line; numbers must appear in the transcript or in the member's own notes; a member's note is never reworded away (kept verbatim). The draft is tainted until the recorder edits and publishes.
- **What a person approves.** The recorder reads the draft and publishes with the same click as every result.
- **Data and consent rules.** The rough notes are the recorder's private text, deleted with the meeting and with the transcript retention, a personal-data finder column (L5 reaches them).
- **Owner actions and facts to re-check.** None.
- **Protected paths.** A migration (a column for the notes) and the personal-data registry entry, both protected paths.
- **A5 as built (#3163).** The notes are a **table**, `crove_meetings.meeting_note` (one row a meeting, up to 8000 characters in the table and 6000 in the box), not a column: the meeting row is read by every reader of the meeting, and hiding a column from them would need a definer that returns meeting text. Row security lets the recorder alone read, write and delete it (an owner, an admin, an attendee, any agent context and the system context read none); it cascades with the meeting (the recorder's delete, an owner's delete, the retention shred at the text date) and the org purge deletes it (`crove_kernel.org_purge` restated with the table added). Commands: `meetings.notes.save` (recorder only, an empty text deletes) and the sensitive read `meetings.notes.get`; the audit rows carry a length, never the text. "Viết lại đầy đủ" is the recorder's own Vy run of the **same family** as the summary (`meetings.summary.start`, the same feature key, meter, budget line and rate limit; `runNotesEnhance` in `apps/kernel`): `enhanceNotes` in `packages/agent` numbers the note lines and the model answers with `{sections: [{key, lines: [{note: n} | {text, seconds}]}]}`. **Code inserts the recorder's own line from its number, so it is verbatim by construction**; a note line the model did not name is appended at the end of the last section with lines, never dropped. A line Vy adds needs a `seconds` inside a real transcript segment, a figure that the transcript or the notes hold, and no link, email or phone number, or **that line is removed and counted** (the A4 and summary rule; an expansion that adds no line at all is refused, `SUMMARY_EMPTY`). The result is the recorder's private Vy result with an `enhanced` part; `meetings.notes.preview` (a sensitive read, in `summary.ts` with the other readers of private results) gives it to the recorder with own and added lines told apart, and the existing `meetings.summary.copy_result` copies it into the draft as one text under the template's headings (the A4 text form), tainted until the recorder has read it, with no change to the sinks list and no new feature key. A copy of an expansion leaves the items a summary run suggested. Personal data (the Meetings PO asked that a data subject request find the notes): `crove_meetings.meeting_note.body` is in the registry as `erase`. `crove_meetings.personal_row_ids` is restated (one `UNION ALL` arm, kind 5, stage `draft` so only the recorder passes) so the finder lists the notes for the recorder acting as owner; the new `crove_meetings.personal_note_counts` counts the notes of other recorders per recorder for the owner, never listing them (the finder's `meetingText` entries gain an optional `notes`, present only when there are some, and the personal-data screen shows it); the new `crove_meetings.personal_note_erase` redacts the value in the notes of **every recorder of the org** and returns the count per recorder (the org is the data controller, and the owner's erasure already deletes every member's translations of a touched meeting; the erasure answer gains an optional `meetingNotes`, counts only, shown on the screen). `personal_matches`, `personal_erase` and the notice functions are **not** restated: their result columns are a contract (a changed one needs a DROP and an ADR), and the sibling functions do the same job for the notes. A short number (fewer than 10 digits) is left as for the other meeting text. The box saves with AI off and the button appears only when the deployment lets Vy summarize.

### 4.5 A6: a webhook when minutes are approved

- **What a user sees.** In a member's meetings settings: "Gửi biên bản đã duyệt tới một địa chỉ", an https address (for Zapier, Make, n8n or the member's own system), the fields to send, a "Gửi thử" button. Each time the recorder publishes a result (the moment of approval), one signed POST goes to the address.
- **Smallest slice.** **A member's own webhook**, not an org-wide one: a meeting is readable only by its recorder and attendees (meetings.md section 8), and an org-wide address set by an owner would send an attendees-only meeting to a place the readers did not choose. This copies the rule of the member-bound MCP grants (ADR-0051 slices 3a to 3c): it works only while an org switch (off by default, the owner's) allows member webhooks, at most one address per member, and it stops when the member leaves.
- **The payload.** Always: event id, type `meeting.result_published`, meeting id, publish time, the meeting's date and length. The member ticks what else goes: title, summary, decisions, action items (text, owner as said, due date). **Never** the transcript, the audio, the video or attendee contact details. The signing is the ADR-0051 format (`Ludy-Event-Id`, `Ludy-Timestamp`, `Ludy-Signature: v1=<hex>`), delivered at least once from the outbox with bounded retries through the egress guard, so a receiver deduplicates on the event id.
- **The AI part: none, on purpose.** What leaves is what the person approved; no model touches it.
- **What a person approves.** The publish click is the trigger; the member set the address and fields; the owner allowed the feature for the org. An unpublished or unpublished-again result sends nothing (an unpublish sends no event in this slice).
- **Data and consent rules.** The receiver is outside the org; the settings screen says the text of the summary and action items will go there. The secret is sealed like a channel credential and shown once. Erasure: the webhook cannot recall a delivered body; the dialog says so (same sentence as L3's mail).
- **Owner actions and facts to re-check.** None to build. Re-check how the existing wake-up webhook's code is shaped so this reuses it and does not copy it.
- **Protected paths.** A migration (address, secret, switch), the outbox event and the delivery code in `apps/kernel/src/**`; if it touches the egress guard or `http.ts` it is protected, and an invariant (no payload column of a transcript) is added in `test/invariant/` first.

## 5. Group B: calendar and output channels (M3 Paid pilot; Zalo and ZNS lower)

### 5.1 B1 to B3: read the calendar to suggest "record this meeting"

- **What a user sees.** In Settings, under the connected Google account, a switch "Cho Ludy đọc tên và giờ cuộc họp trong lịch" (off by default; the connection today reads only busy times, never titles, and stays that way until the member turns this on and approves Google's new consent). When on: in the extension popup, a card "Sắp họp: Họp giao ban 9:00 (Google Meet)" with "Ghi cuộc họp này" which fills the title and starts the normal consent step; in the Meetings tab, the same list for today; and after any recording or upload ends, "Gắn vào sự kiện lịch này?" when exactly one event overlaps the recording's time (within 15 minutes) and the recording's start. The meeting then links to that calendar event (the L1 link).
- **Smallest slice.** Google first (B1 server and app, B2 extension), reading **only the connecting member's own primary calendar**. Outlook is the same shape through Microsoft Graph and is B3.
- **The AI part: none, on purpose.** Matching is by time overlap and, where the event carries a join link, that link; code decides, a person confirms. Using AI to guess a meeting from a title would be a model call on calendar text for no gain.
- **What a person approves.** Switching the read on, and each "Ghi cuộc họp này" or "Gắn vào" click. **Ludy never joins a call and never starts a recording from the calendar**: the research's top complaint about the market is auto-join, and the consent step must run before any recording.
- **Data and consent rules.**
  - A new, narrower scope on top of the existing connection: the read of events on calendars the member owns (`calendar.events.owned.readonly`, or `calendar.events.readonly` if the first does not exist). **[recheck]** its name, its category (sensitive needs verification, restricted needs the CASA security assessment) and what it returns. The research found that sensitive scopes need a verification with a demo video and a privacy policy, about three to five working days, and no CASA; a restricted one would add an annual assessment of roughly 540 USD (one community source, unverified). If it turns out restricted, B1 stops and the owner decides.
  - What is stored: for events from 1 day ago to 7 days ahead, per member: title, start, end, time zone, join address (only a Meet, Zoom or Teams address, as the adapter parses it), and the number of invitees. **Never** the invitees' addresses, the description, or attachments. Refreshed by the existing 15-minute sweep; rows older than the window are deleted; disconnecting or switching off deletes the cache at once; the cache is readable only by its member (row-level security), and the owner, admin and system read nothing. The extension reads only the next events through a new, narrow extension-token scope (B2); a stolen token could at most list a member's next event titles, which the plan accepts and the security review confirms.
  - Calendar titles are personal data of the member and of third parties; they are never sent to a model in this group.
- **Owner actions.** B-OA1: add the read scope to the Google OAuth consent screen of the Ludy calendar client and complete Google's verification if it is a sensitive scope (video, privacy policy page, justification); the runbook `calendar-oauth.md` gets the steps. B-OA2: for Outlook, register a multi-tenant Microsoft Entra app with delegated `Calendars.Read`, add the redirect URI, set a publisher domain; check whether tenant admins must consent ([recheck]).
- **Facts to re-check on the vendor page before building.** Google: the exact scope names and categories (Google's scopes list and the sensitive-scope verification page), the unverified-app user cap (100) and the 7-day test-mode token expiry, whether an event's `conferenceData` is returned for the narrow scope. Microsoft: `Calendars.Read` consent requirements (the report found one third-party document claiming admin consent became the default for every external app from November 2025), `onlineMeeting.joinUrl` on events, the delta query.
- **Protected paths.** A migration (the events cache); the extension-token scope is a change to the extension token (M8's `apps/kernel/src/auth*.ts`, `http.ts`, the route enumeration test #693, an invariant), so B2 is protected; the vault and OAuth route code for Outlook (B3) is protected like the Google one was.

### 5.2 B4: send approved minutes to Slack and Teams (post only)

- **What a user sees.** An owner or admin pastes an address under Settings, "Kênh nhận biên bản": a Slack Incoming Webhook URL or a Teams Workflows webhook URL, with a name ("Kênh #hop-giao-ban"). Slack: the customer makes a small Slack app with an Incoming Webhook (a few clicks) and pastes its URL, so Ludy needs **no Slack app, no Marketplace listing and no Slack review**, and self-hosters have no extra setup. An "Add to Slack" button with a Ludy Slack app is later and only if customers find the webhook step too hard.

- **What a person approves.** The recorder's click on the exact text, once per meeting per channel (a repeat needs a tick, like L3). Posting a message is outward-facing, so it is an R2 command from the click. **Only a published result can be posted**, and the dialog says everyone in the channel will read it even if they cannot open the meeting.
- **Data and consent rules.** The webhook URL is a secret (it lets anyone post): stored sealed as a channel connection (`slack_webhook`, `teams_webhook`) by the owner or admin, never shown again, never logged. Only the summary, decisions and action items leave; never the transcript, audio or attendee contact details. The post is a job through the egress guard with the adapter's host allowlist (`hooks.slack.com` for Slack; the Teams Workflows hosts **[recheck]**), recorded in `external_call`, retried only when safe, with the idempotency key of the post. The audit row holds ids and counts.
- **Owner actions.** None for Ludy. The customer makes the webhook in their own Slack or Teams.
- **Facts to re-check.** Slack: that Incoming Webhooks are still offered for new apps and their limits (about one message a second per webhook, unverified). Teams: the current Workflows template name and whether it needs a Power Automate licence beyond the one included with Microsoft 365 (the report says the template needs no premium licence), the message size limit, the card format, and the hosts a Workflows webhook URL uses.
- **Protected paths.** A migration (channel provider values and the post log), the channel adapters in `packages/channel/**` (not on the list) and the command in `modules/meetings/**`; the vault use is the existing one. The migration is protected.

### 5.3 B5: send approved minutes to a customer by Zalo OA (inside the free window)

## 6. Group C: import after the meeting (GA gate, P2)

The three platforms close or tax the live door (Google's Media API ends on 2027-01-15 and Zoom bots must leave when their person leaves, per the research) but keep an after-meeting door. Ludy uses only that door and prefers **the audio file**: if a platform's own transcript is poor in Vietnamese, Ludy's own Vietnamese transcription of the same audio is the point of the module. All of C is optional and needs the owner's platform apps (Ludy Cloud holds one approved app per platform in the private Cloud layer; a self-hoster registers their own, which is a short, documented step for a small company).

### 6.1 C1: the import core

- **What a user sees.** In Settings, "Kết nối nền tảng họp": connect Google, Zoom or Microsoft Teams. On the Meetings tab a card "Bản ghi có sẵn trên nền tảng": a list of recordings the platform has finished and the member could import ("Họp giao ban, 12/11, 41 phút, Zoom"). The member presses "Nhập", the consent step runs (a new method: "the platform showed its own recording notice to everyone, and I confirm", plus the Ludy notice text), and the meeting appears in the normal flow: transcript, summary, review, publish.
- **Smallest slice.** A manual import of a finished recording the member asks for, with the list built from the platform's notice (webhook) or the member's refresh. **Nothing is downloaded until the member presses "Nhập"**: the platform's webhook creates only a suggestion row. That answers the research's consent lesson (no silent import) and the cost lesson (Microsoft meters per minute).
- **The AI part.** None new: after the import the file is a normal recording; transcription (`meetings.transcribe`) and the summary (`meetings.summary`) run as for an upload, as the importer's own Vy, through the AI budget meter. A platform transcript, when there is no audio (only a transcript is available) is imported as typed text marked "từ nền tảng, không phải Ludy chép" and a person may then ask Ludy to re-transcribe only if audio exists.
- **What a person approves.** The import click and the consent confirmation; the summary, as always, the recorder publishes.
- **Data and consent rules.** The file goes through the same path as an upload (staged, encrypted at claim with the meeting's key, `crove-media` normalizes and drops the video unless "Ghi cả video" was ticked at import, retention as set). The platform's own copy is never deleted by Ludy. Platform tokens are sealed in the vault; only the OAuth routes and jobs do network I/O; every adapter has its host allowlist. A recording belongs to the member who imported it (the recorder), with the same readers rule. Platform participant lists are not copied; the member names attendees as for any meeting. Importing a recording does not remove the duty to tell participants: the confirmation records the platform's notice and Ludy's, and the lawyer question of meetings.md section 7 applies.
- **Order of formats.** Audio-only file if the platform offers one, else the video file with its audio extracted by `crove-media`, else the platform transcript. The first import on each platform compares the platform's transcript with Ludy's on the same Vietnamese audio using the eval scorer of `docs/runbooks/meetings-transcribe-eval.md` and writes the numbers on the issue; this is the research's suggested test, and it needs two of the consented scripted recordings of #2585.
- **Owner actions.** The platform apps (C-OA1 to C-OA3) and, for the Microsoft billing decision, a spend approval.
- **Protected paths.** A migration (connections' import state, suggestions, the import log), the route for connecting and, for Zoom, a public webhook route (the route-coverage invariant's reasoned public list changes in two steps, as CAL-1 did), the worker job. Protected.

## 7a. Group E: translate the transcript and the summary (M3 Paid pilot, P2)

Added by the owner on 2026-11-14 ("Tính năng dịch transcript và summary nữa chứ nhỉ"), after the other groups. No outside account is needed; the only cost is the AI budget the org already has. Three steps: E1 (the summary), E2 (the transcript), E3 (Chinese, Japanese and Korean, later).

- **What a user sees.** A meeting held in English by a Vietnamese team (or the reverse: a Vietnamese meeting a foreign partner must follow) is the common case. On the summary and on the transcript there is a button "Dịch sang English" (or "Dịch sang tiếng Việt"): the target is the other language of the pair, because a meeting is recorded in Vietnamese or English (`MEETING_LANGUAGES` has `vi` and `en` only). The translation appears **next to the original**, labelled "Bản dịch máy", never in place of it: the summary as a second block, the transcript as a second line under each segment. A long transcript is translated in a background run and fills in part by part ("Đã dịch 12/20 phần"). Whoever reads the meeting can ask for a translation of what they read; the recorder can also ask for one of their unpublished draft.
- **Smallest slice.** The pair vi to en and en to vi, the summary first (E1, a foreground run of one short call, like the summary), then the transcript (E2, chunked, a background run like the transcription). Decisions and action items are part of the summary translation. No target language picker, no automatic translation, no translation of the live preview (later, below).
- **The AI part.** Vy, the clicking member's own Vy (ADR-0049: it reads exactly what that member reads), translates the text. One metered model call per click for the summary, one per chunk for the transcript, feature key `meetings.translate` (module `meetings`, a new key in `AI_FEATURES`; a key of its own so a translation never cuts off a transcription, a summary or live notes, which the one-run-per-feature rule of #3029 would otherwise do). Each chunk is admitted on its projected cost before the call, as the audio chunks are (#2863): a chunk that would reach a hard-stop budget is refused, the translation stops with "Chờ ngân sách AI" and resumes on the member's next click or when the budget returns; chunks already done are kept. The card shows the estimated cost before the member clicks. The kill switch for the org, the `meetings` module or the deployment stops new translation calls; calls in flight finish.
- **Checks in code, before anything shows.** The translation is tainted text and code decides, with the rules of meetings.md section 5.3 adapted to two languages: (1) a digit run (separators ignored, so `1.250.000` and `1,250,000` are one) that the translation holds and the original does not **refuses that segment's translation** (the original shows with "không dịch được dòng này"); (2) a digit run of the original that the translation drops shows a warning mark on that segment ("Số liệu có thể khác bản gốc"), not a refusal, because a date written `14/11` may become "November 14"; (3) a capitalised word mid-sentence that neither the original nor a small fixed list in code (days, months, countries) holds shows the same warning, since names must come through untouched; (4) an empty result, a result over its length, a link or address the original does not hold, or a result that is the original unchanged is refused. The transcript text is untrusted: an instruction inside a line is translated as text and obeyed by nobody (red-team transcripts in CI with a fake model, as for M6).
- **What a person approves.** The click starts it and spends the budget. **A translation is never a source of anything**: it creates no task (tasks are made from the original items, as today), is not sent by the follow-up mail (L3 sends the original, published result), is not posted to Slack, Teams or Zalo (B4, B5), is not in the approved-minutes webhook (A6), is not searched (L2 and A3 read the original lines) and is not exported. Using a translated text anywhere else is a later step in which a person reads and approves it.
- **Storage, decided: store it, privately, as a derived row.** On demand rendering would spend the budget again on every reload and every attendee, and a one-hour transcript is about twenty calls, so the translation is kept in a new tenant table `crove_meetings.translation` (one row per member, meeting, kind `summary` or `transcript segment`, source stage and target language). The rules: (a) **owned by the member who asked and readable by that member alone** (restrictive per-member row policies like `live_line`), so no Vy run writes a row another member reads, which keeps ADR-0049's "no Vy output lands in a row an attendee reads" and the meetings.md section 8 reader rule intact; an owner or admin who is not a reader reads nothing; (b) it carries a hash of the source text it translated, and an edited or re-published source makes it "Bản dịch cũ, dịch lại" instead of showing it as current; (c) it is deleted with the meeting (foreign key and the org purge), when a published result it translated is unpublished, and with the source draft's own expiry (30 days, ADR-0049); a translation of published text is deleted at the text retention, and it is part of the shredding of text; (d) it is `tainted` for as long as it exists; (e) the personal-data finder reaches it and an erasure **drops** the translations of a meeting it touched instead of redacting them, since a redacted source would leave the translated name behind and a name cannot be located reliably in another language; (f) it is in no export, no WORM batch, no outbox payload and no audit fact (the audit row holds ids, language codes and counts). Publishing a translation to all readers, so one click serves every attendee, is a later step with the recorder's click (`meetings.result.publish` style) and is not in this plan.
- **Data and consent rules.** The text goes to the model provider exactly as the summary does (the deployment's provider list already names it; a provider outside Vietnam is the same cross-border question as in meetings.md section 7). Translation adds no new data subject: it only re-expresses text the member already reads. The screen says the text is sent to the AI provider, as the summary screen does.
- **With AI off.** The button stays and says why in the words used elsewhere ("Chờ ngân sách AI", "AI đang tạm dừng", or that the deployment has no model); nothing is called. The meeting works as before. The core works with AI off.
- **How it meets the other pieces.** *Live notes (L4a):* not translated in the first slice (a preview that changes every few seconds would spend a call per refresh); the finished transcript and notes can be translated after the meeting. *Follow-up email (L3):* sends the published original; a translated follow-up mail is a later step in which the recorder reads and approves the translated text. *A3, ask across meetings:* answers in the language of the question, citing the original lines, whatever language the meeting used, so it needs no translation store. *A4, the biên bản page:* Vietnamese layout, not translated here.
- **E3, Chinese, Japanese and Korean (later, Icebox, P3).** For trading partners. A target-only language (a meeting is still recorded in vi or en), a picker of target languages, and a quality check by a person who reads that language, since the research found no measure of any language pair. It waits until a pilot customer asks, because the Vietnamese SME case is vi and en.
- **Owner actions.** None to build. One bilingual person (the owner or a pilot customer's staff) reads about 20 translated segments and the translated summary of the scripted eval meetings and writes the verdict on the E1 issue; it is not a blocker for merge, it is a blocker for calling it done.
- **Facts to re-check.** Whether the deployment's text model translates Vietnamese to English and back well enough on the six scripted meetings of `docs/runbooks/meetings-eval-scripts` (no measurement of a Vietnamese pair was found in the research; none is assumed); the model's context size against a chunk; the meter's token counts for a chunk.
- **Protected paths.** E1: a migration (the translation table, its policies, the member-Vy catalog entry) and the personal-data registry (`packages/kernel/src/personal-data.ts`). E2: none of its own if E1's table covers segments (a migration only if the segment kind needs a column). E3: none.
- **E1 as built (#3184).** Table `crove_meetings.translation` with the unique key (org, meeting, member, kind, target language, source stage): the source stage (`draft` or `published`) is in the key so a recorder's draft translation and published translation never overwrite each other, and a published translation of a new publish is dropped by trigger, not shown as stale. One row translates the summary, the decisions and the item titles (who and when stay as said). Routes: `meetings.translation.status` (the button: language pair, expected cost from the text size and `AI_PRICE_*`, or why not), `.translate` (the click, one `meetings.translate` call under the member's meter) and `.get` (the member's own rows, with `stale`). The sink is `meetings.translation.copy_result`, on `MEMBER_VY_SINKS`, held to the hash of the text the member reads now. The same switch as the summary (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) turns it on. Deletion paths, each tested: meeting delete, owner delete, text retention and org purge (foreign key and the purge list), draft expiry, a replaced draft, a new publish and any removal of a published row (triggers on `meeting_result` and `action_item`), and the personal-data erasure (`personal_erase` drops the translations of every meeting it touches). No command unpublishes a result today; the triggers cover one added later. Not covered by E1: a former member's translations stay unreadable (row security needs an active member) until the meeting's text date; the 30-day purge of a removed member's Vy rows does not reach them (P3 follow-up).

## 10. Risks and open questions

- **The facts are unverified.** Mitigation: each issue re-checks first and records the result; nothing in a build depends on a price.
- **Consent.** The strongest risk is a recording the others did not know about. A1 and A2 make telling people a one-click act and the policy tightenable; none of it replaces the lawyer's reading.
- **A webhook or channel post leaks a private meeting.** A6 is member-owned and field-limited; B4 and B5 send only a published result a person reads and clicks; the dialogs say who will read it. Every outward post is an R2 command.
- **Google CASA cost.** Decided by C-OA1 before C2 is built; the plan does not assume a figure.
- **Microsoft billing.** The owner decides the Azure account; a one-person company never needs it under 600 minutes a month **[recheck]**.
- **The platform apps are one per platform.** Ludy Cloud holds approved apps; a self-hoster registers their own; the core never needs them.
- **Translation quality and cost (group E).** Nobody measured Vietnamese and English translation of meeting talk; a wrong figure in a translated summary could mislead a foreign partner. Mitigation: the label "Bản dịch máy", the original always beside it, the code checks on figures and names, the warnings, a bilingual person's spot check on E1, the cost shown before the click and the admission before each chunk.

- **Still open, for the Meetings PO:** (5) a translation (group E) is private to the member who asked in the first slice, instead of one shared by all readers; this costs one translation per member but never lets a Vy run write text another member reads.
