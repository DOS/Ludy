# Cash-flow forecast, 30/60/90 days (#4391)

Owner decision 2026-11-22 (popup to the Accounting PO): a 30/60/90-day cash-flow view per legal entity, computed by code, with the Accounting coworker (Ly) explaining the low point. Listed in [accounting-coworker.md](accounting-coworker.md). Plan line for the issue; the design and the PO's four decisions are on [#4391](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4391).

## What it is

A card on the Kế toán Tổng quan (`finance.cash_forecast`, `modules/finance/src/cash-forecast.ts`, pure rules in `domain/cash-forecast.ts`). One legal entity at a time, VND only, day 0 to day 90, every amount a decimal string. The forecast itself posts and pays nothing; the only thing stored is the owner's own list of expected items (below, [#5326](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/5326)).

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Opening cash | The booked cash by **the rule of B01 line 110** (the overview's cash card): the summed balance of each account of 111 and 112, counted only when it is a debit balance. So an account overdrawn on its own adds nothing, as it adds nothing to the card, and the opening cash equals `finance.overview`'s cash for the same day (a DB test pins that). A bank line not yet booked is not in it. |
| Receipts | The open sales invoices of the reminders' loader (`loadCustomers`: the 131 balance laid over the invoices, newest first), each on its due date (invoice date + the customer's or the org's terms). **An overdue invoice is not counted**; it is shown apart. The part of 131 no invoice covers is not counted either. |
| Payments | The 331 balance per supplier laid over its purchase invoices, newest first, each on invoice date + 30 days (**an estimate, Uncertain**: a received invoice has no due date); an invoice dated after day 0 is not laid under day 0's balance. An overdue one counts on day 0. The part of 331 no invoice covers counts on day 0. **331 lines that name no party** (a manual journal) are netted: a credit counts on day 0 like any debt no invoice covers; a debit is a payment already made (the cash has left), so it lowers what is owed, the largest supplier first (the books do not say which one it paid). |
| Approved supplier payments (#3113) | A dated outflow on the first day the bank file may go (the end of the 24-hour hold, else today). Its amount is still inside 331 until a bank line is matched, so it is taken out of that supplier's pool first: **what it pays comes off its own invoice** (the whole invoice leaves the allocation only when the payment covers all of it, a partial payment leaves the rest). What it counts for is capped by the supplier's 331 balance, so a payment already booked by hand leaves once; **an approved payment to a supplier with no positive 331 balance is not counted at all**. A payment in another currency than the books' is ignored and its invoice stays in the pool. |
| Expected items the owner entered (#5326) | The costs and receipts the books cannot show, one row per item of one legal entity (`ludy_finance.cash_forecast_item`, at most 100 an entity, commands `finance.cash_forecast_item_set` and `finance.cash_forecast_item_remove`, list `finance.cash_forecast_items`). Category (payroll, insurance, tax, rent, loan, other), direction (out or in), a whole-dong amount above zero, a first date and a repeat: once, monthly or quarterly. A repeating item falls on the day of its first date each month (a short month on its last day, counted from the first date so it never drifts), up to an inclusive end date; an occurrence before day 0 is ignored (no catch-up of a missed one-off) and the window is day 0 to day 90. Each occurrence is an `entered_in` or `entered_out` item, so it moves the series, the checkpoints, the low point and the top items like any other. **Entering one takes its category off the left-out line** (loan drops loans; other drops recurring costs only when it repeats); the books' figures (opening cash, receipts, payables, overdue, unlinked) are unchanged. Owners and admins write, users only (an agent, Vy or an MCP client writes none); every role reads. Hard delete. The label is the owner's own words, shown on the card and **never** in an audit row, the forecast answer, the coworker's facts or the answer of a save (which names the item and its entity only, since a command's answer may be kept for an idempotency key where an erasure does not reach it, #5333) (the facts name an item by its kind only: expected receipt or cost entered by the owner). The form warns that an item already booked in a purchase invoice would be counted twice. |
| Left out, always said | Payroll, social insurance, tax payments, rent, loans and any other recurring cost, **each until the owner enters an item of that category**. Finance cannot read payroll (ADR-0046: `hr` depends on `finance`; no pay date or amount is stored), and no tax amount is computed anywhere. The tax due dates are shown as markers with no amount, **of the chosen entity and only for the tax calendar's own window, the next 30 days** (not the whole 90). |
| Low point | The lowest balance of the 91 days and its day (the earliest on a tie), shown with or without AI. The five largest items up to that day drive the note; no party names. |

Asymmetry on purpose: an overdue receivable is not expected, an overdue payable is. Every date is derived (terms, the first day a file may go); none is stated by a document.

## The coworker's note

`finance.cash_forecast_explain` in `AI_FEATURES` (the finance budget line), the same flow as the overview sentence: the model is told the figures and dates to reason on, no name, and answers up to 3 sentences **in words only**. The note states **no figure and no date**: the card beside it shows every one, and a model allowed to write a figure can put a real one on the wrong label (the balance of day 90 as "the lowest balance") and still pass a check that only asks whether the number is among the given ones (review of #4389, finding B3). So a text with a digit (even one the facts hold), a number in words, a scaled number, a month or day name, a link, **advice about money** (move, transfer, borrow, delay, chase, cut) or **a promise or certainty** (will, sẽ, guaranteed) is refused and the card shows the figures alone. The advice and promise words are checked in code (`packages/agent/src/cash-forecast.ts`), not only asked for in the prompt. No model call when nothing is expected in the 90 days; the core works with AI off.

## Bounds (performance)

At most 500 customers and 500 suppliers (largest balances first), 10,000 sales invoices and 10,000 purchase invoices (newest first) and 1,000 approved payments are read; past a bound the forecast says it is partial (`truncated`). The invoices of the customers are grouped once by customer, not filtered per customer. Reads are aggregates on `journal_line` by org, entity, account prefix and entry date, the predicates the overview and the customers list already run; invoice reads are bounded by invoice date up to day 0. The as-of day, when a caller names one, lies between 2000-01-01 and 2100-12-31 (a day outside it is refused as invalid): past the year 9999 a date has no four-digit text. One small table of the owner's items (#5326), read by the entity index and capped at 100 rows an entity, so the extra read is bounded; no change to the journal or its indexes. The coworker's note is a separate call (`explain`) that reads the forecast again; the same aggregates run twice, which is cheap at these bounds and keeps the note tied to the figures it is asked for; a refused or failed note is kept by its facts (30 minutes, 5 minutes after a failed call) so the card's 60-second reads do not pay for it again.
