# Issuing sales e-invoices through the seller's own provider: plan

> Plan for [#2497](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2497), 2026-11-03. Owner direction of 2026-11-03 in the Accounting PO chat: Ludy does not become an e-invoice provider; it integrates with the providers that have an API, set up by the org itself in a "Tích hợp" (Integrations) section of the accounting settings. Design only: nothing here is built, no money is spent, no credential was used, and every fact about a provider comes from public pages read on 2026-11-03. The decision record for the adapter boundary is [ADR-0044](../adr/0044-einvoice-provider-adapters.md).

## 1. Goal

Ludy records the sales e-invoices of the org (#313, a sale is a document with direction `sale`) and the corrections that arrive as XML (#916), but it cannot **issue** one. Every business that sells issues invoices every month, so without issuing Ludy cannot be the only tool of a small company: the owner would open the provider's own web page for each sale and then bring the XML back.

After this plan an owner or member of an SME can, in Kế toán:

1. connect the org's own e-invoice provider account once (Tích hợp), test the connection and see the invoice templates and series the provider holds;
2. make an invoice draft (typed, copied from an earlier invoice, or drafted by the finance coworker from a deal, a booking or a chat line), check it, and press **Phát hành**;
3. see the invoice come back numbered, signed and carrying the tax authority code, with the signed XML stored as the source file and the sale posted through the existing proposal path;
4. issue an adjustment or a replacement from an issued invoice, never by editing it.

The first slices need no provider at all: they run against an offline **sandbox provider** inside Ludy (as `channels.connectSandbox` does for channels), so the whole flow is built and tested before the owner creates a single provider account.

## 2. What the owner decided

- **Ludy is not a provider.** It never numbers, signs or sends an invoice to the tax authority itself; the seller's provider does, with the seller's certificate. The seller stays the issuer and is liable for each invoice (rule 1 of section 3).
- **Bring your own account.** The org enters its own provider account or API credentials. They are stored sealed as channel credentials ([ADR-0025](../adr/0025-channel-layer.md)), read only by the worker, never shown back, removable by owners and admins. Where the provider lets its own customers call the API, no partner contract between the provider and Ludy is needed; the survey records what each provider says (section 4).
- **One adapter per provider behind one interface.** The Tích hợp page lists the providers, a "Kiểm tra kết nối" test, the templates and series pulled from the provider, and the status.
- **Providers an SME can start with on its own come first.** Section 5 says which that is, and what the survey could not settle.

## 3. The rules a design must encode

**The rules changed this year.** Decree 254/2026/NĐ-CP on e-invoices and e-documents (issued 2026-06-30, in force from 2026-07-01, implementing the Tax Administration Law 108/2025/QH15) repeals Decree 123/2020, Decree 70/2025 and Article 1 of Decree 41/2022 (its Article 43); Circular 91/2026/TT-BTC replaces Circular 32/2025 and guides the Law and the Decree; Circular 94/2026/TT-BTC covers tax risk rating, and high-risk taxpayers must use coded invoices. The text of #2497 says "Decree 123/2020 as amended": that is the previous regime. **Caveat on the sources:** the official decree PDF ([chinhphu.vn](https://xdcs.cdnchinhphu.vn/446259493575335936/2026/7/13/254-ndcp-signed-hoa-don-dien-tu-1783935351029278986043.pdf)) is a scanned image that could not be read, and the legal database refused the fetch. The article numbers below come from the [government's policy site](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-so-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu-119260713164251972.htm), an [EY note](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu) and accountancy commentary ([ketoananpha](https://ketoananpha.vn/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp), [ihoadon](https://ihoadon.vn/hddt/hoa-don-dien-tu-tai-thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc.html)); Circular 91's article numbers rest on commentary only. **A person who can read the primary text (the accountant of the pilot partner, #61) checks this table before the first build issue merges** (section 10).

**What the survey supports, and what it does not.**

- **MISA is the only provider with a complete, first-party, public specification** (field lists, error codes, limits). Every other provider's API is known only from unofficial copies, old forum posts, a community client or resellers, or not at all. An adapter written from a 2018 forum table is a guess; none is written until the provider's own document, or a sandbox call by the owner, confirms it (section 10).
- **No provider shows both things the owner asked about**: the customer's own credentials with no contract for Ludy, and a sandbox an SME starts itself. Viettel's access model (own login, own IP list) and SePay's (own client id and secret) come closest on the first; the second is not stated anywhere.
- **None offers webhooks.** Every API is polled; some answers stay `Pending` for minutes to two hours. The status job is built for that.
- **Unattended signing exists only through the provider's HSM or remote certificate** (MISA, Viettel, BKAV, SePay's HSM option). A USB token needs a person and a machine, so it is not a server path: the Tích hợp page says which signing a connection uses and refuses a connection that needs a token held on a PC.
- **A cancel call is documented by MISA (a page of its specification) and appears in the older BKAV and VNPT sources, and not in SePay's or Pay2S's.** Ludy does not use it: the rules appear to have dropped the cancellation of an e-invoice (section 3, rule 7), so an API that still has the call proves nothing about what the seller may do. If the primary text keeps cancellation, the port gets it as a capability (section 6).
- **Prices.** Per-invoice packages in the hundreds of thousands of VND a year for a small business; the API is a separate quote at the providers that mention it and unstated at the rest. The owner learns the real cost only by asking (section 10).
- **Series and templates are made by the seller in the provider's own web page** and registered with the tax authority first; the APIs only list them. So Ludy cannot make a series, and a seller with none cannot issue.

## 5. Which provider first, and why

Criteria, in order: (1) a first-party public specification, so the adapter is built from the provider's own words; (2) the operations the flows need (issue, status, files, adjust, replace); (3) unattended signing through the provider's certificate; (4) the customer's own credentials; (5) a sandbox; (6) price known. No candidate meets all six on public evidence.

**Recommendation: MISA meInvoice first, after one owner-only check.** It is the only provider that meets (1), (2) and (3) on first-party pages, and a seller who already uses MISA's accounting products is the likeliest early user. (4) and (5) are open: the developer portal reads as self-service (sign in, create an app, request integration) but no page confirms that the request is approved without a Ludy agreement or how a sandbox account is made. The check is cheap and decides the order: the owner (or the accountant of the pilot partner) signs in with a MISA ID, creates a test app, sends the integration request and writes down what happens (section 10, issue [#2507](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2507)). Until then the build runs on the offline sandbox provider, so nothing waits.

**Second: Viettel S-Invoice**, the only one whose access reads as customer-controlled, but its specification was seen only as an unofficial copy: its adapter waits for the owner's check of the same kind. **Then BKAV and VNPT** (credentials per customer, a paid integration extra, no open document). **EasyInvoice and FPT** only when they publish a document or the owner holds an API specification under a sales contact. **SePay and Pay2S** are not first: each adds a company between Ludy and the provider and neither covers cancel or (Pay2S) adjust and replace; they are reconsidered if a seller's provider has no API of its own.

The order is cheap to change because the interface is one ([ADR-0044](../adr/0044-einvoice-provider-adapters.md)) and the offline provider tests every flow.

## 6. The adapter boundary

Decided in [ADR-0044](../adr/0044-einvoice-provider-adapters.md). In short:

- A port `EInvoiceProvider` in a new package `@crove/einvoice`, as `@crove/channel` holds the channel adapters. Capabilities, each declared by the adapter: `testConnection`, `listTemplates` (templates and series, with the symbol), `issue` (one call that numbers, signs and submits for the code, or a draft call and an issue call), `status` (by the provider's reference and by Ludy's own key), `files` (signed XML, PDF), `adjust`, `replace`, and flags for what the provider cannot do (no cancel, no draft, one call or two for signing, batch size, sequential per series).
- **Ludy's key.** Each issue carries a key made by Ludy for the draft (a UUID kept on the draft), sent as the provider's own idempotency field where one exists (MISA's `RefID`, Viettel's `transactionUuid`, BKAV's partner-side ID). A call whose answer was lost is never sent again blindly: the job asks the provider for that key first (`external_call` states `started`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `ambiguous`, [ADR-0025](../adr/0025-channel-layer.md) point 5).
- **Where the truth is.** The provider holds the legal invoice; Ludy keeps the draft, the state and the signed XML it fetched. A state is one of `draft`, `submitting`, `pending` (accepted by the provider, waiting for the tax authority), `issued`, `rejected`, `failed` and, on a later invoice, `adjusts` or `replaces` a reference. An invoice becomes `issued` only when the provider's answer carries a number, a signing time and, for coded invoices, the tax authority code, and the signed XML has been fetched and **read by Ludy's own XML reader and signature check (#674)**: the totals of the XML must equal the draft's, or the invoice goes to a person as a mismatch, never posted quietly.
- **The sale is posted through the existing path.** The fetched XML enters the normal intake (`kernel.file.ingest`, the `crove-extract` sandbox, the signature check) as a sale of the org (the seller's tax code is the org's), so the proposal, the checks, the duplicate key (direction, seller tax code, series, number) and the posting are the ones used today for a received sales XML. What is new is who triggers it and how it is approved (point 3 of the ADR: the person's Phát hành click is the approval of the posting when the XML matches the draft; otherwise the proposal waits for a person).
- **Egress** only to the adapter's own hosts, through `guardedRequest`, every call in `external_call`, off unless the deployment enables the provider, with the kill switch of the channel layer. Credentials never leave the worker's decrypt step; logs and audit rows carry codes, never the credentials, the buyer's data or a provider's message.

## 7. The Tích hợp page

Where: Kế toán settings, a section "Tích hợp" (owners and admins write, every member of the entity sees the status). The section is general: a connection has a **kind** (`einvoice` here; `marketplace` for Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada in #2499, later others such as a bank feed) and a provider within the kind, and each kind is one card in the section with the same parts as below (list, connect, test, status, disconnect). The section, the sealed credential, the status line and the kill switches are built once in #2501 and know nothing about e-invoices; only the template and series step (item 4) belongs to the `einvoice` kind. A kind is added by registering its adapter list and its card, never by a new settings page.

1. **Providers listed** with a short line each: what it needs from the seller (its own account and API access enabled, a certificate on the provider's HSM), and a link to the provider's page. A provider appears only when its adapter is built and enabled for the deployment.
2. **Connect:** the fields the adapter names (for MISA: the seller's tax code, user and password, and the app credentials the org created at MISA; for Viettel: user, password and a note to allow Ludy's server IP). Sent once, sealed at the edge (the envelope, never the secret, reaches the command), stored in `crove_kernel.credential`, never shown back. **Disconnect** drops the credential.
3. **Kiểm tra kết nối:** `testConnection` plus a read of the templates and series. The result is a state (works, wrong credentials, API not enabled, IP not allowed, provider unreachable, signing needs a person), never the provider's text.
4. **Templates and series** pulled from the provider and shown with the symbol decoded in words (coded or not, the year, the type); the org links each series to a legal entity (a company with several entities issues from the series of the right one) and may mark one as the default. Ludy never creates a series: a seller with none sees "tạo ký hiệu hóa đơn trên trang của nhà cung cấp trước" with the provider's link.
5. **Status:** last successful call, last failure kind, the number of invoices in `pending`.
6. Nothing here issues an invoice, and the page works for an org with no provider connected (the offline sandbox provider can be switched on on a self-hosted install for trying the flow, as `CHANNEL_SANDBOX` does).

## 8. The flows

**Issue.**

1. *Draft.* A member makes a draft in a new screen "Hóa đơn bán ra": the buyer (a customer of the org or typed: tax code, name, address, email; a buyer with no details is "bán cho người tiêu dùng"), the lines (description, unit, quantity, unit price, VAT rate), the series chosen from the provider's, the date. Totals are computed by code in decimal ([`decimal.js`](../../AGENTS.md#rules)), never typed. The draft is stored in a tenant table of its own (build issue 1) with the key of section 6, scoped to the legal entity.
2. *Checks, deterministic.* Required fields by Decree 254 Art. 10; the buyer's tax code checksum (`@crove/l10n-vn`) and, when the tax-code lookup is configured, the name and address it returns shown beside the typed ones; the VAT rate in the set the provider accepts (0, 5, 8, 10, "không chịu thuế", "không kê khai", other); one currency; the line sum equal to the total; a series that belongs to the entity; a date that the provider accepts. A failed check is shown on the field; nothing is sent.
3. *Phát hành.* A command `finance.einvoice.issue` (R3: a legal document to the tax authority that only a replacement or adjustment corrects, so a fresh second factor as for other irreversible acts; owners, admins and members who may approve sales, never an agent) moves the draft to `submitting` and queues the job. The person is told it is final.
4. *Job.* It calls the adapter's `issue` under the key, then polls `status` (every few seconds at first, then minutes, up to a day) until `issued` or `rejected`. A timeout or a lost answer asks the provider for the key before anything is sent again.
5. *Back.* On `issued`, the job fetches the signed XML and PDF, stores them as evidence files, runs the intake and the posting of section 6, and tells the person. On `rejected`, the draft returns to `draft` with the provider's reason shown as a code and a short Vietnamese line and the invoice **keeps its key**, so a fixed draft is a new key and a new invoice: the rejected number is never reused by Ludy, and what the provider does with its number is the provider's.
6. *A "pending" invoice* is shown as such on Home ("Hôm nay cần bạn") past a threshold so a stuck one is noticed.

**Adjustment** (điều chỉnh tăng, giảm, or of information): from an issued invoice a person chooses "Điều chỉnh", enters the difference per line (plus or minus) or the corrected information, and the same issue flow runs with the original's reference (series, number, date) in the request. The result is a **new** invoice whose XML references the original; the intake already links it (#916: `correction_kind = adjustment`, `corrects_document_id`), the original stays in force, and the posting is the difference.

**Replacement** (thay thế): from an issued invoice a person chooses "Thay thế", edits a copy, and issues it with the original's reference. On success the intake voids the original (#916: the original is voided in the same transaction, the entry reversed) and posts the new one. A rejected replacement leaves the original untouched.

**Cancellation:** no action (section 3, rule 7). If the primary text shows that a cancel still exists, it is one more build issue behind the same port; the capability flag `cancel` is there for it.

**Errors only in non-core content** (buyer name or address, amount in words): the screen offers "Gửi thông báo cho người mua" and says the notice to the tax authority is the seller's step until the form is confirmed (section 10); no new invoice is made.

**Not covered here:** cash-register invoices (rule 3), non-coded invoices, issuing for another org from one login (the firm case: one org per client company, each with its own connection), foreign-currency invoices beyond showing the VND amount, and batch invoices (01/BK-ĐCTT).

## 9. The coworker's part

The finance coworker (`{finance}` in strings) drafts; a person issues. Where it helps a one-person company:

1. **Lines from a source.** From a deal or booking of the CRM, a previous invoice to the same buyer, or a line a person typed or pasted from a chat ("bán 10 hộp A giá 120k cho anh Nam"), it proposes the description, quantity, unit and unit price, and the VAT rate it has seen on the earlier invoices to that buyer or for that item.
2. **The buyer's tax details.** It proposes the party and tax code from the org's customers. The name and address come from the party record or the tax-code lookup, never from the model.
3. **Tainted, and checked.** The model's output is a proposal. Every number it states (quantity, price) must appear in the source it was given (the deal value, the earlier invoice, the typed line), by the same rule as the reminder wording: a figure the source does not hold is refused and the lines stay empty. The VAT rate must be one of the provider's. Totals are computed by code. The tax code passes the checksum. A rejected proposal is no proposal: the person types the lines.
4. **Metered** under a new `AI_FEATURES` key (`finance.einvoice_draft`), the core works with AI off, and **it never presses Phát hành**: nothing a model wrote is sent before a person reads the whole draft and issues it.
5. What the model sees: the source text and the buyer's name; never the org's credentials, the provider's data or a bank account.

## 11. Risks and open points

- **Wrong legal text.** The rules in section 3 may differ from the primary text. Mitigation: the check issue gates the issue command (build issue 3) and the correction flows (issue 5).
- **A provider changes its API.** Each adapter has a recorded-response test and a "Kiểm tra kết nối" that fails visibly; an adapter breaks on the provider's schedule.
- **Duplicate or lost invoices** on a timeout: the key, the `external_call` states and the ask-before-retry rule; a person sees an `ambiguous` invoice and is told to check the provider's page before issuing again.
- **Signing needs a person.** A connection whose signing is a USB token is refused with an explanation; the seller moves to the provider's HSM or remote signing.
- **A rejected invoice after the sale was posted.** The posting happens only on `issued` with the XML read; a later change of status at the provider (a revoked code) is a state change that raises a task, never an edit.
- **Cost for the seller.** The API may cost extra at the provider; Ludy cannot know it, and says so on the page.
- **Posting approval.** Point 3 of the ADR lets a person's issue click approve the posting when the XML equals the draft. That touches the ledger rules (protected): it needs the Platform PO's security review before the build issue that adds it.
