## Rollout invariants & verification gates

1. **Zero edits to applied migrations:** Every migration already on `main` (`2026*`..`2027*`) keeps its exact filename (`*_crove_*.sql`) and `sha256` checksum.
2. **Pilot equivalence proof:** `#3969` + `#3970` + `#3971` must prove in CI (`migrate.db.test.ts`, `supabase-stack`, `shared-database`, `pgtap`) that upgrading a pre-cutover database yields the exact same schemas, roles, tables, functions, triggers, grants, and pinned `search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp` hardening as a clean bootstrap.
3. **Immutable data-at-rest decoders:** Sealed rows, WORM manifests, backup archives, and historical idempotency/hash records continue decoding legacy domain tags per [ADR-0054](../adr/0054-rebrand-internal-crove-identifiers-to-ludy.md) section 2.
4. **Package vs DB concurrency:** S3 (`#3966`) and S4 (`#3967`) can proceed in parallel or right after S2 (`#3965`), while S6 (`#3970`) waits on S5 (`#3969`) and S7 (`#3971`) waits on S6 (`#3970`) so `Invariant suite (from base)` and `hardeningViolations` remain green on every PR head.
