# App UI/UX audit, 2026-11-17

The owner found the web app messy and asked on 2026-11-17 for all of it to be audited. This file covers everything outside Ludy Engage (the Inbox, CRM, Social and Ads screens are audited by the Engage audit session): the app shell, sign-up and sign-in, Home (Overview), Notifications, Tasks, Settings, Chart and AI (the Ludy AI app), and the Accounting app (Ludy Kế toán). Not covered: Notes, Projects, Meetings, Nhân sự, eSign (other lines, not in the request), the public landing page.

## How it was done

- Code: `origin/main` at `1c1b3e71e` (2026-11-17). The pilot was not used; it serves a bundle from 2026-11-03 ([#3580](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/3580)).
- Stack: `docker compose` in an own project on its own port, auth e-mail confirmation off (local default).
- Demo org: "Công ty TNHH Thương mại Hoa Mai", an owner who signed up through the form, created the org on the onboarding screen, uploaded seven Vietnamese purchase e-invoices (Oct and Nov 2026) to the Document inbox, approved four (so October has a VAT period with figures), turned on two-factor sign-in as the first approval forces, and added five tasks.
- Walked as a new owner at 1440 and at 390 wide, in Vietnamese and in English: sign-up to the first useful screen, the rail and the sidebar, the phone drawer, the org switcher, the account menu, search (Ctrl K), every Settings card, every Accounting screen. English strings were swept by script for leftover Vietnamese on 35 routes. axe-core (WCAG 2.2 AA rules) ran on 20 routes at both widths. Tap target sizes were measured on the phone.
- Judged against [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) (principles, 3.4 layout, section 4 components, 5.3 states, 5.1 approval, section 7 accessibility, section 8 i18n, 1.1 names) and [docs/product/scope.md](../product/scope.md).
- Screenshots are not in git. They are in the audit session's shared notes folder `app-ux-2026-11-17-screens/`, named `<language>-<device>/<screen>.png`, and each finding below cites its file.

Severity: **P1** blocks or misleads; **P2** clearly wrong or ugly; **P3** polish.

## What is already good

- axe-core found no WCAG 2.2 AA violation on any of the 20 routes at either width.
- No horizontal page scroll at 390 on any route walked.
- The proposal screen (`inbox-detail`) follows DESIGN 5.1: the entry in Nợ/Có form, the checks with results, a dashed "Đề xuất, chưa ghi sổ" outline, an approve button that names the outcome.
- Money is right-aligned, tabular and exact on the VAT, ledger and journal screens. Empty states exist everywhere and most name the next action.
- The 2FA field has `autocomplete="one-time-code"` and a numeric keypad.

## The five worst problems

1. **Settings is one page of about 25 cards**, 5000 px tall on a laptop and 10000 px on a phone, with no sections, no tabs, no links to a card. Accounting, calendar, integration and personal-security settings sit side by side (section 5).
2. **The sidebar menu does not match the screen.** Overview, Notifications, Settings, the activity log and Ludy AI's cards show the Cộng tác menu with nothing lit, from the first sign-in (section 1).
3. **Home counts the same seven invoices four times** ("14 việc cần bạn" for 7 invoices) and buries the tax deadlines at the bottom of a 2100 px page (section 3).
4. **The first approval is a wall.** The owner who presses "Duyệt và ghi sổ" for the first time must set up an authenticator app in the middle of the proposal screen, and every sign-in after that asks for a code. Nothing earlier says so (section 9).
5. **No create or edit flow uses a panel or dialog** (section 12): Tasks, Vouchers, Fixed assets, Journal, Settings and Ludy AI all push an inline form above the list. Also: **names and words disagree across the product:** Settings says "Social", "Quảng cáo", "CRM" where the shell says Engage; the menu says "Việc nhóm", the page says "Công việc"; Ludy AI shows no AI team while every Accounting screen says Ly proposes the entries; the activity log shows `kernel.notification.dispatch` and UUIDs to an owner (sections 5, 6, 7, 8).

## 12. Create and edit flows and misplaced settings, against the top work apps

Rules from the owner on 2026-11-17, who judges Ludy against HubSpot, Twenty, Attio and Clarify: create and edit in a right-side panel or a small dialog, never an inline form pushed above a list; the first field is what people already know; duplicates are flagged as the person types; no field asks for data the business does not need; settings live in Settings, not inside working screens.

Reference screens used (from the Engage PO): HubSpot's "Create contact" panel checks the e-mail while typing and shows the existing contact before a second can be made; Twenty's "Create person" panel opens over the list with a Create button and a keyboard shortcut (and shows what not to do: it lists every field of the object); Attio's "Add person" is a small dialog with one field ("Email, URL, or name"), matches under it and an "Add 'Anh Le'" row.

Code fact behind most rows: no create or edit screen in the app uses a dialog or a panel. `tasks.tsx`, `vouchers.tsx`, `fixed-assets.tsx`, `journal-voucher.tsx`, `settings.tsx` and `agents.tsx` all draw an inline `<form>`, and `packages/ui` has `Dialog` but no side panel (DESIGN 4.5 names a Sheet).
