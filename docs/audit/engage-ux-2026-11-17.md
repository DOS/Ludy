# Ludy Engage UI/UX audit, 2026-11-17

The owner found the Engage Inbox messy on the pilot and asked on 2026-11-17 for every Engage screen to be audited. This file covers Ludy Engage: the Inbox, Contacts, Companies, Deals and quotes, Bookings and the public quote and booking pages, Posts, the composer and the post page, Post updates, Reports, Ads, Channels and the Engage settings places. Everything outside Engage (shell, Home, Settings outside Engage, Accounting) is in [app-ux-2026-11-17.md](app-ux-2026-11-17.md); the few things this walk saw there are under "Other lines" below.

## How it was done, and what is not covered

- Code: `origin/main` at `9cded40c7` (2026-11-17), after [#3616](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/3616) (one current sidebar item, retention in Settings, availability control, two-pane Inbox) and [#3610](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/3610) (contacts and companies created in a `Sheet`, no personal tax code, duplicates flagged). Those fixes are not repeated. The pilot was not used: it serves a bundle from 2026-11-03 ([#3580](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/3580)) and its sign-in was not available to this session.
- **Rendered walk, partial.** A stack ran in an own compose project on its own port with an org "Cửa hàng Hoa Mai" (six contacts and a company by CSV import, five deals, a quote sent by link, two sandbox channels, posts in each state, sandbox comments, three emailed conversations). Its screenshots at 1440 wide in Vietnamese were looked at for the Inbox, Contacts, Companies, Deals, Bookings, Posts with its tabs, the composer, a published post's page and Số liệu. The host then ran out of capacity (load above 100, the Docker engine restarted twice) and the Engage PO then relayed the owner's load order of 2026-11-17: no local stacks, so the phone and English renders and the other screens were not captured. **No screenshots are kept**, so no finding has a screenshot path; each cites code (file and line, or catalog key) instead.
- **Code audit for the rest.** Three read-only reviews of the code, one for CRM, one for Social and Reports, one for Ads and the Inbox thread, simulated a new owner of a Vietnamese shop and judged against DESIGN.md (principles, 3.4 layout, section 4 components, 5.1 approval, 5.3 states, section 7 accessibility, section 8 i18n, 1.1 names) and docs/product/scope.md. Findings marked "code only, not rendered" are read from the code: the class names say what a phone does, the catalogs say what the owner reads. Phone layout, axe-core and tap-target sizes were **not measured**. A fix session should confirm each by rendering first.
- Not covered: the public booking page and quote page as rendered, Channels connect and disconnect on a real platform, English strings (vi and en parity is enforced by the type check; en was spot-checked), dark theme.

Severity: **P1** blocks or misleads a user; **P2** clearly wrong or ugly; **P3** polish.

## What is already good

- The server role gating matches the controls: erase, connect, disconnect, review policy, quote settings and the pipeline are hidden from members and viewers.
- Destructive deletes of labels, snippets, feeds, saved views, deals, stages, draft quotes, bookings and ad objects use `ConfirmDialog` with a named confirm button.
- The reply flow of the Inbox has a stale-reply guard ("Có tin mới ... Hãy đọc trước khi trả lời"), Dy's draft goes into the box and sends nothing until a person presses send, and unclear sends are never retried by a button.
- AI output is marked as a proposal on the composer rewrite (dashed, "Dùng bản này" and "Bỏ"), on the campaign fields ("Do Uy soạn") and on the approval card of Ads, which puts the largest possible spend first.
- Money goes through decimal strings and `formatMoney`; no em dashes in the vi or en copy of the audited areas; Dy and Uy are never called assistants.

## The five worst problems

1. **A new owner cannot post.** The composer has no "when" field and its one-step button sends the post to the channel's queue, which a fresh channel does not have, so the first attempt ends in a red "hàng đợi" error (SO-4, to confirm live). Behind it the channel connect card is the last of about twelve cards on a phone (SO-1), and env var names such as `AI_BASE_URL` and `CHANNEL_GOOGLE_BUSINESS` show in the owner's own screens (SO-2).
2. **The Ads approval says nothing is sent while the build sends the campaign, paused, and holds the cap** (AD-1). It is the screen where a person approves money.
3. **The erase controls sit next to Resolve** (IN-1): a 28 px ghost "Xóa khách này" beside the primary button, a second one in every comment bubble, and a confirmation that names nobody and gives no counts.
4. **Booking and quotes dead-end the customer and the owner.** The owner cannot find the public booking link anywhere (CR-1); the public quote page, opened from Zalo on a phone, is an eight-column table with no phone layout (CR-2); "Gửi và tạo liên kết" says "Đã gửi" when nothing was sent (CR-3).
5. **The Posts screen is built around the tools, not the work.** The page is named "Engage", the default tab is empty while posts wait for review, seven equal buttons and ten tabs surround one primary action, and the same figures live in Posts and in Reports (SO-5, SO-6, SO-7, SO-16, SO-18). Deals have the same shape: five columns of 288 px in a 1088 px area hide Thắng and Thua (CR-7).
