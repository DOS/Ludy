# WhatsApp Business conversations: live check

Settles, on one spare WhatsApp number, the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) facts the conversation core (issue #3131, [plan](../plan/conversations-channels.md) section 4.1, ADR-0041) was built on without ever seeing a live event. **No official platform page could be read** when this channel was built: `developers.facebook.com` did not resolve from the build session's network (the host is outside its allowlist), so nothing below is [docs]. Every fact is tagged **[S]** (a secondary source: a vendor's or an SDK's documentation, several that agree), **[M]** (recalled from earlier knowledge of the platform and not seen in any source of the pass: field names, call shapes and error codes) or **[U]** (not found, or sources conflict). The code never treats one as truth: each is read in one place, listed here with the file to change if the live answer differs. The first thing a person with access to the pages should do is read them and write what they say in the Result column.

**Scope.** Only the owner's own business portfolio, a **spare number** and test accounts (the owner's own WhatsApp accounts). A number that runs in the WhatsApp Business app cannot be moved to the API without coexistence [S], and a test must not disturb a real shop. **No real customer's messages** go through a live channel until the table below is settled. A customer's message holds their phone number and words, which are personal data of a third party: kept here only until stored, then under the org's retention (CV6, built).

**Nothing here costs Ludy money.** The business pays Meta on its own account. Ludy sends no template and no business-initiated message: only a free-form reply inside the 24 hours after the customer's last message. If a step below shows a charge on Ludy's own account, stop and tell the PO.

Markers: **[PERSON]** only a person can do it (the owner: Meta business portfolio, the Meta app dashboard, phones). **[AGENT]** a Ludy session can do it with the owner's go-ahead. Never paste a secret, a token, a phone number or a customer's message into chat, an issue or a commit: write what each step showed ("matched", "no match", the error code), not its content.

## 0. What the owner sets up

**[PERSON]**

1. A **Meta business portfolio** and, in it, a **WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)** with a **spare phone number** registered to the Cloud API (the number cannot also run in the WhatsApp Business app, unless onboarded with coexistence, row 12). Note the **phone number id** and the **WABA id** (the Meta app's WhatsApp > API Setup page shows both). They are not secrets, but write them in the Ludy connect form only, never in chat.
2. A **Meta app** with the WhatsApp product. It may be the same app as Facebook Pages or another; Ludy's two variables for it are separate (below). The business verification and App Review that Ludy Cloud needs to serve other businesses are a later step (plan section 6); a developer-mode app is enough for a test with the owner's own numbers.
3. A **system user** in the business portfolio, with the app and the WhatsApp Business Account assigned, and a **system user access token** with the `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management` permissions [M]. It is a credential: it goes into the connect form and is sealed.
4. In the app dashboard, WhatsApp > Configuration: the **callback URL** `<PUBLIC_URL>/api/channels/whatsapp/webhook` (https, nothing in it names an org) and a **verify token** of the owner's choosing, and subscribe the field **`messages`**. **[PERSON]** note whether the dashboard also needs `POST /{waba-id}/subscribed_apps` to be called for the webhook to flow (row 4); Ludy does not call it.
5. Add the owner's own WhatsApp accounts as **test recipients** while the app is in development mode [S].

### The settings: where they live, how they are held, how they are rotated

- **Variables:** `CHANNEL_WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET` (the Meta app's secret), `CHANNEL_WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN` (the token of step 4), optional `CHANNEL_WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET_PREVIOUS` (a rotation) and `CHANNEL_WHATSAPP_MEDIA_HOSTS` (row 14). With the app secret **and** the verify token set, `GET` and `POST /api/channels/whatsapp/webhook` exist and a business may connect its number on the Channels screen (a number connected without a webhook would never receive a customer's message); without both, the route does not exist and the screen offers no WhatsApp form. The worker sends replies with the app secret alone, so a number already connected keeps replying if the verify token is later removed. The server refuses to start with a verify token and no secret, with a previous secret and no secret, or with a value of fewer than 8 or more than 200 characters or with spaces.
- **How they are held:** read from the process environment only. Never written to the database, a file, a log line, an error, an alert, an audit row or an answer. The system user token is **not** one of them: it is each connection's own credential, sealed by `CREDENTIAL_KEY` as for every channel and opened only inside the worker's job. The pilot check refuses a sample value (`change-me`) for each secret and lists a left-over `_PREVIOUS` as an open item.
- **Planned rotation** (it protects only the check of Meta's notices, so a rotation loses nothing stored). **[PERSON]** reset the app secret in the Meta dashboard, and in **one** restart set `CHANNEL_WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET` to the new secret and `CHANNEL_WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET_PREVIOUS` to the old one: the server refuses to start when the two are equal, so never restart with the old secret in both. A notice Meta signed with the new secret between the reset and the restart is refused, and Meta retries a failed delivery [U]; a notice still signed with the old secret (a retry, a slow delivery) is accepted after the restart. Check with step 2 below; then remove `_PREVIOUS` and restart, **within the hour**. A leaked secret never goes into `_PREVIOUS`: reset it and restart at once with the new secret only, and accept that the notices signed with the leaked one from then on are refused. A forged notice signed with a leaked secret is accepted: it could add messages to the threads of connected numbers, never read or send anything.
- **Where a value is never to appear:** chat, issues, pull requests, runbook results. Write "secret set", "token set" and "rotated on <date>".

## 2. Steps

All with the number connected in Ludy (**Social > channels > WhatsApp**: the system user token, the phone number id and the WABA id) by an owner, and the variables of section 0 in place.

1. **Handshake (row 1).** **[PERSON]** in the app dashboard, save the callback URL with the verify token. It saves cleanly. **[AGENT]** read the API's log for the route: a `200` for the `GET`, a `403` for a wrong token (try one).
2. **Signature and inbound (rows 2, 3, 4, 5, 17).** **[PERSON]** from a test WhatsApp account that is a test recipient, send "kiểm tra 1" to the number. The message appears in **Hộp thư** with the account's name and its number as the customer (record whether the number is digits only, row 17). **[AGENT]** read the log: a `200` means the signature matched, a `401` means it did not. On a `401`, with the owner's go-ahead, capture one raw request in a scratch environment (never in the repo) and check the header against the HMAC of its bytes with the app secret. Then, in the contact panel, check that a contact whose phone is the same number is offered.
3. **Reply (rows 6, 7).** Reply from Ludy to that thread. The test phone receives it; the message ends `sent`. Then **[PERSON]** read the business's billing page (Meta Business Suite > Billing, or WhatsApp Manager > Insights) and record whether the reply was charged (row 7). Dy's draft appears for a new thread when the org's switch is on and a model is configured; the person presses Send, and nothing is sent by Dy.
4. **Window (rows 8, 9, 20).** **[PERSON]** after more than 24 hours without a message from the test account, open the thread in Ludy: the reply box is replaced by the window message and Send is not offered. **[AGENT]**, with the owner's go-ahead and a scratch call with the token (never from the repo), send a free-form text to that account and record the code Meta returns (expect 131047). If Meta allows more than 24 hours, change `windowHours` and the `whatsapp` rule.
5. **Status failed and errors (rows 10, 11).** Provoke each one that can be provoked and record the code Meta returns and the wording Ludy shows: a reply to a number that is not a test recipient (131030), a number with no WhatsApp (131026), a revoked token (190: the connection becomes expired and asks to be reconnected). A code Meta gives that the mapping does not know shows as `WA_<code>`: add it to `sendError` and the messages. For row 10, send a reply just before the window closes, or use any case where Meta reports a failure after accepting, and check that the message shows `failed` with its code and that a second delivery of the same status changes nothing.
6. **Image (rows 13, 14, 15).** Send an image, then an audio message, a document, a sticker and a location. Each keeps its placeholder line in the thread ("[Khách gửi ảnh]" and so on); the image also appears as an image within a few seconds. Check by the row only: `state` `stored`, `source_url` and `source_ref` empty, the type and size filled, one object under `conversation/<org_id>/` in the store. If it stays a placeholder, the worker's log says `attachment <id>: dropped` and the audit row's `code` says why (`WA_MEDIA_HOST`: the host is not allowed, row 14; `WA_MEDIA_GONE`; `NOT_AN_IMAGE`; `HTTP_<status>`). Then **[PERSON]** erase that customer from the thread's header and check that the rows and the object are gone within two minutes (`crove_kernel.conversation_blob_doomed` empty).
7. **Redelivery (row 16).** Stop the API for two minutes, send two messages, start it: each message is in the thread once. Record when the retries came.
8. **Unclear send.** Kill the worker while a reply is being sent (or stop it right after the call): the message shows "Chưa rõ đã gửi hay chưa", and the only button is "Đã gửi rồi" (a person looks on the phone and marks it). There is no button that sends it again: WhatsApp has no history to read back.
9. **Coexistence (row 12), later.** Only if the owner decides to offer Embedded Signup: connect a number that stays in the WhatsApp Business app, send a message from the app, and record the event the API receives.
10. **Clean up.** **[PERSON]** erase the test customers from their threads (an owner or admin, with a fresh second factor), remove the test recipients if they are no longer needed, and rotate the system user token and the app secret if either was ever shown on a screen that was shared.

## 3. What is not built (follow-up issues, same plan)

- **Templates and any business-initiated message.** Out of this channel on purpose (plan section 8): a template is billed per delivered message by category and country, so a mistaken send costs the business money. A later slice has its own approval flow and a person's confirmation of the cost.
- **Embedded Signup** (a business signs in with Meta and picks its number) and Meta business verification and App Review for Ludy Cloud: an owner decision and owner actions, time and not money.
- **History and a gap marker:** there is no endpoint to read back (row 21). The one safety net is Meta's own retry.
- **Marking messages read, typing indicators, outbound images and documents, WhatsApp groups, calling:** not built.
- **A longer window for ads** (row 20) and **coexistence echoes** (row 12): kept to what the live check shows.
