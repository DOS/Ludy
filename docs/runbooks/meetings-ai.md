# Runbook: switch on the meetings AI (summary and transcription)

How an instance turns on the recorder's Vy for meeting notes: the summary with its decisions and action items (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY`, #2580), and speech to text (`MEETINGS_TRANSCRIBE`, #2578). Both are off by default, and the meetings module works without them: a person types or pastes the transcript and writes the summary by hand.

This is a production step. No team lead or worker runs it. The coordinator shows the owner the step list below, the owner approves it in the coordinator's chat, and the coordinator runs exactly that list, stopping at the first failure ([contributing](../contributing.md#production-secret-and-credential-steps)). Secrets are named here by reference, never by value.

## What switching on does

- **Summary.** The recorder clicks "Nhờ Vy tóm tắt" on a meeting, then "Chép vào bản nháp của tôi". Their own Vy, a member-scoped run (ADR-0049), reads the transcript and writes a draft summary, decisions and action items. Every copied draft row is tainted: the screen shows the review note, and the draft cannot be shared until the recorder clicks "Tôi đã đọc". Each call goes through the AI budget meter, and the kill switch stops it.
- **Transcription.** The recorder clicks "Nhờ Vy ghi lời". Their Vy sends the meeting's audio, chunk by chunk, to the speech-to-text provider. It reads the audio only through the audited door (`ludy_meetings.audio_for_member_vy`) and the recorder's "Chép vào bản nháp của tôi" copies the text into a draft transcript, also tainted. It is metered by audio minutes.
- **Translation (E1, #3184).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) also lets any reader click "Dịch sang English" (or "Dịch sang tiếng Việt") on the summary they read, and the recorder on their own draft. Their own Vy translates the summary, the decisions and the item titles in one call under its own AI budget line (`meetings.translate`, module `meetings`), the card shows the expected cost before the click, and the answer stays private to that member, beside the original as "Bản dịch máy". It is never shared, mailed, posted or made into a task, and it is deleted with the meeting, with the text it translated and by the personal-data erasure. The model's raw answer also sits in the member's own Vy result for up to 30 days (sealed to that member, like every Vy result, and counted by the erasure report); "deleted with the text" covers the translation row, not that result copy. Quality of the Vietnamese and English pair is unmeasured: a bilingual person's spot check of the six scripted eval meetings is recorded on #3184.
- **Transcript translation (E2, #3185).** The same switch also lets a reader click "Dịch sang ..." on the transcript they read (the recorder on their draft). Their own Vy translates one chunk of about 4500 bytes a request, in the member's own request (no worker job, needs the deployment's text model, the one the summary uses), and the page asks again while it is open; each chunk is admitted on its projected cost before the call, a hard-stop budget refuses a chunk, the parts earlier requests kept stay, and the card says "Chờ ngân sách AI" until the next click. The parts are kept in the member's own private row and are never shared, mailed, posted, searched or exported. A one-hour Vietnamese transcript is about twenty calls (14 in the test); the card shows the expected calls and cost before the click. The raw answer of each request sits in the member's own Vy result for up to 30 days, as for the summary.
- **Bilingual minutes (E4, #4777).** The recorder asks Vy to translate the shared minutes (the E1 button, same meter line `meetings.translate`), then opens "Duyệt và đăng bản dịch", reads and edits each segment (an empty one is left out) and clicks "Đăng bản dịch". Only that click makes the version readable by the meeting's readers (a switch beside the original); "Gỡ bản dịch" takes it down. Publishing calls no model and works with AI off for a recorder who types it. The version is dropped when the shared summary, a decision or an item title changes, is replaced or redacted, and goes with the meeting, the retention job and the org purge. Nothing to configure and no new AI budget line.
- **Translation into Chinese, Japanese and Korean (E3, #3186).** Off by default and a switch per language: `MEETINGS_TRANSLATION_LANGUAGES` is a comma list from `zh` (Simplified Chinese), `ja` and `ko`, empty when unset (a typo stops the boot). A meeting is still held in Vietnamese or English; the three are targets only, offered beside the other language of the pair where the list names them and `MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`. **Turn a language on only after a native reader has checked it:** follow [the reader's check](meetings-translation-reader.md) (the scripted meetings of `docs/runbooks/meetings-eval-scripts` run into that language, a reader of it rates about 20 translated lines and the translated summary) and put the verdict on issue #3186; until then leave it out of the list (the code ships nothing by itself). The checks in code are the same as for the pair (an invented figure or a link is refused, a dropped figure warns) plus one: a translation that is mostly not in the language's script shows as untranslated. The model is told to keep numbers in Arabic digits and names and acronyms as written, so a figure written with 万 or 만 is refused rather than guessed at. The capitalised-name warning reads Latin script, so for these three it is turned around: a foreign Latin name or acronym of the source that the translation does not carry as written (transliterated or dropped) shows the same mark, "a name may differ from the original". It is kept narrow so the mark means something: a Vietnamese word, a Vietnamese place or title, a country, a month, a city, a unit and common acronyms (OK, CEO, VAT, VND, IT, HR, TNHH) are translated, not asked for as written, and a name is matched only when no Latin letter or digit stands next to it. A translation may be at most 1.5 characters per original character plus 60 (the pair keeps 3 plus 100), since these languages write the same thing in far fewer characters; the bound is loose on purpose until a reader's check gives a measured one. The estimate before the click leans higher for them (more tokens per character).
- **Ask across meetings (A3, #3161).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets any member who may work with Vy ask a question in the box above the meetings list. Their own Vy reads at most 40 transcript lines from at most 8 meetings that the member's own search found (nothing a member cannot read), sent to the AI provider of this system, and writes two to six sentences, each with a chip that opens the transcript at its line. Code drops a sentence that cites no sent line, quotes a phrase its line does not hold, states a figure its lines do not write, or holds a link. It runs under its own AI budget line (`meetings.ask`, module `meetings`); the box shows the expected cost and the percent of the AI budget used. The answer is shown to the asker only and is not stored: the member's Vy result row for the run holds counts only (lines, sentences, sources, dropped) for up to 30 days. A question with no matching line makes no run and no model call. With AI off the box searches the transcripts for the question's main words.
- **Ask about this meeting (#4815).** The meeting page of anyone who reads a meeting with a transcript has a box "Hỏi về cuộc họp này": the same question as the box above the list (same switch, meter line `meetings.ask`, budget and checks), but Vy is given only the lines of THIS meeting that the person reads (up to 40, those holding a word of the question), and every sentence shows the time of the line it rests on. Nothing is stored; with AI off or the budget spent the box lists the lines of this meeting that hold the question's words. Nothing to configure.
- **Vy's brief before a meeting (L7, #3543).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets any member who may work with Vy click "Nhờ Vy viết vài câu tóm tắt" on the brief of a meeting of a series (on the meeting's page or on the card of an upcoming calendar event). The facts are read by code as the member (the previous meeting's decisions only if the member read it, the series' open items, the linked customer's open deals through the CRM module's queries) and work with AI off. The member's own Vy is given only those numbered facts (at most 23 short lines), once, under the meter key `meetings.brief`, with a 30 second limit; code keeps at most three sentences, each citing facts, and refuses one that writes a figure, a link or an address the cited facts do not. The answer goes back to the browser and is not stored: the run's result row holds counts only. No fact and no sentence is logged. With nothing to say there is no run and no call.
- **This week's decisions and open items (L9, #3949).** The facts are read by code and work with AI off: on the meetings page a "Tuần này" card lists, for the last 7, 14 or 30 days, the published decisions and the still-open action items of the meetings the member reads (row security decides; a draft result and a meeting past its text date show nothing; at most 30 decisions and 30 items, newest meeting first). The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets any member who may work with Vy click "Nhờ Vy viết vài câu tóm tắt" on it. The member's own Vy is given only those numbered facts (at most 60 short lines), once, under the meter key `meetings.digest`, with a 30 second limit; code keeps at most three sentences, each citing facts, drops one whose figure or date no cited fact writes or that holds a link, address or phone number, and the answer is shown on the page only and never stored (the run's result row holds counts only). With AI off the card is the lists alone.
- **Who an action item belongs to (#3921).** Code first (no AI): the name said in the meeting is matched with the attendees, then the other members, by display name (Vietnamese marks and case folded, titles such as anh, chị, bác and a description such as "bên kế toán" dropped), and a person is suggested only when exactly one matches; nothing is stored and a person still picks the seat. When code finds nobody, the same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets a member who may work with Vy click "Nhờ Vy xem đây là ai" on that item. The member's own Vy is given only the said name (at most 8 words) and the numbered display names of the people the member may give the work to (at most 100: the attendees, then the other members; no ids, emails, titles or seats), once, under the meter key `meetings.assignee`, with a 20 second limit, through the AI budget. It must answer `{"n": number}` or `{"n": null}`: a number off the list, or a person the said text does not resemble, is dropped, and a text that is a sentence rather than a name is never sent. The answer is shown as a suggestion with the person's seats (a seat is preselected only when they hold exactly one) and the run's result row holds counts only. With AI off the button is absent and step one still works.
- **Updating the deal after a meeting (#4766).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets the recorder of a meeting that is linked to an open deal and has published minutes click "Nhờ Vy đề xuất" on the card "Cập nhật cơ hội". The member's own Vy is given the deal's title and stage and the published summary, decisions and numbered action items (at most 40; a longer list is refused, never cut), once, under the meter key `meetings.deal_update`, with a 30 second limit, through the AI budget. It must answer `{"nextStep": {"text", "item"} | null, "note": string | null}`: the day of the next step is the parsed date of the action item numbered `item`, never the model's, and a text with a link, address, phone number or long digit run, or a figure, date or currency the minutes and the deal's title do not write, is dropped on its own. Nothing is written by the run (its result row holds counts only); the recorder's Save creates a tainted task (a note alone gets a task of fixed title), the note as a comment of that task, a line of fixed words on the contact's timeline, and closes the stale-deal reminder in one transaction. With AI off the button is absent and the card still saves what the recorder types. No migration, no new table.
- **Agenda adherence (#3959).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets the recorder of a meeting with a transcript click "Nhờ Vy kiểm tra" in the card "Cuộc họp đã bàn đủ chưa?" on the meeting's page. The agenda is read by code as the recorder (no AI, so it shows with AI off): up to 8 lines the recorder types in the box (used for that one check and not stored) and then the open items of the previous meeting of the series, 12 lines at most, each cut at 200 characters. Their own Vy is given only the numbered agenda lines and the numbered transcript lines the recorder reads (the draft, else the published ones; a line is read up to its first 600 characters; at most 700 lines and 120,000 bytes counted as they are sent: a longer transcript is refused with a sentence and never cut, because a cut transcript would make "not heard" false; no speaker, id or title goes), once, under the meter key `meetings.adherence`, with a 45 second limit, through the AI budget. It must answer `{"items":[{"n":1,"covered":true,"lines":[4,5]}]}`: a covered claim that cites none of the given lines, or more than three, is turned into "not heard" and counted, an unusable answer fails with a fixed code, and the page shows each agenda line as it was given with the transcript lines (time and words) a claim cites, never a sentence of the model's. There is no score, percentage or per-person figure anywhere. The answer is shown to the recorder only and is not stored: the run's result row holds counts only (agenda lines, transcript lines, covered, dropped). With AI off the card shows the agenda list and the box and no button.
- **Chapters by Vy (#3844).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets the recorder click "Gợi ý mục lục" in the chapters card of their meeting. Their own Vy reads the transcript (at most 60,000 bytes, longer is refused and never cut) as the recorder, once, under its own AI budget line (`meetings.chapters`, module `meetings`, 60 second limit), and proposes up to 20 chapters, each a short title and a second. Code keeps a chapter only if its second is the whole second of a real transcript line and its title, cleaned of control, bidi and zero-width characters, holds no link, email address, phone, run of 8 or more digits or figure the transcript does not say. The result is private to the recorder, tainted, and expires in 30 days; the recorder copies it into their draft (`meetings.chapters.copy_result`, which checks the anchors again), reads it, confirms it (`meetings.draft.review`, the only thing that clears the mark; a save keeps it) and publishes it with the minutes. A meeting's removal cancels a run in flight and deletes its result. With AI off the recorder types chapters by hand.
- **Title by Vy (#3947).** The same switch lets the recorder of a meeting that has a transcript click "Gợi ý tên" next to its title. Their own Vy reads the transcript (at most 60,000 bytes, longer is refused and never cut) as the recorder, once, under its own AI budget line (`meetings.title`, module `meetings`, 30 second limit); a spent budget or an engaged kill switch refuses before the call. It proposes up to 3 titles; code keeps one only if it is 3 to 80 characters on one line, holds none of the control, bidi, zero-width or separator characters a template title refuses, no email address, no run of 8 or more digits and no figure the transcript does not say. The titles go to the browser as plain text and are stored nowhere (the run's result holds counts only). A pick is `meetings.meeting.rename`, the recorder's own audited command (the audit row names the meeting, never the title); a rename after the minutes were published changes the title that readers and the MCP read see; with none picked nothing changes. With AI off there is no button.
- **Labels by Vy (#5010).** The same switch lets the recorder of a meeting that has fewer than 5 labels click "Gợi ý nhãn" next to the labels on its page. Their own Vy is given the meeting's title and the labels the recorder already reads (the ones on this meeting and the most used on the meetings they can read, at most 30 distinct; read through `meetings.meeting.list` as the recorder, so a label of a meeting they cannot read never reaches the model) and no transcript, summary, attendee, party or deal name; one call under its own AI budget line (`meetings.label_suggest`, module `meetings`, 30 second limit); a spent budget or an engaged kill switch refuses before the call. It proposes up to 3 labels; code keeps one only if it meets the rules of a typed label (1 to 40 characters, no line break or hidden character, no "@", no run of 7 or more digits), holds no link or phone number, prompt markup or instruction, repeats no label of the meeting or earlier suggestion, and keeps the meeting at 5 labels at most. The labels go to the browser as plain text chips and are stored nowhere (the run's result holds counts only, the start's audit row the ids of the meeting and the run). A click is `meetings.label.add`, the recorder's own audited command; with none clicked nothing changes. Deleting the meeting cancels a run in flight. With AI off there is no button.
- **Who committed to what, by Vy (#3954).** The same switch lets the recorder of a meeting that has a transcript click "Liệt kê cam kết" in the card "Ai cam kết làm gì". Their own Vy reads the transcript (at most 60,000 bytes, longer is refused and never cut) as the recorder, once, under its own AI budget line (`meetings.commitments`, module `meetings`, 60 second limit); a spent budget or an engaged kill switch refuses before the call. For each speaker it proposes up to 5 lines, each a commitment or a decision. Code keeps a line only if the speaker is exactly a label the transcript has (written as the transcript spells it, never as the model spelled it), the line cites the whole start second of a real line spoken by that same speaker, its kind is one of the two, and its text is 3 to 200 characters on one line with no hidden character, link, email address, phone, run of 8 or more digits or figure the cited line does not say; repeats are dropped, at most 20 speakers. The answer has no field for talk time, tone or a judgment of a person (the plan rules out manager analytics). A model that ignores the instruction and writes a judgment as a "commitment" is not caught by code: that is why every line carries the time of the line it rests on, the answer is private to the recorder, and nothing is saved, shared or made a task from the card (a person still takes the summary's action items as before). The lines go to the browser as plain text and are stored nowhere (the run's result holds counts only). With AI off there is no card.
- **Suggest the minutes template (#4772).** The card "Gợi ý loại cuộc họp" on a meeting page is the recorder's, for a meeting with a transcript that is still on the general summary. Code suggests first with no AI (the series' previous template, a linked deal, words in the title, two people as a corroboration of a 1:1 only); it needs no switch. When code cannot tell, the same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) shows "Nhờ Vy gợi ý": the recorder's own Vy reads the transcript once (at most 60,000 bytes; a longer one is refused, never cut) under its own meter line `meetings.template_suggest` (module `meetings`, 60 second limit) and answers one template with a one-line reason and the second of a real line; code drops a template that is not one of the four, a second that is no line, or a reason with a contact, a run of 8 digits or a figure the cited line does not say. Nothing is stored (the result row holds counts only, under a random subject); one click takes the suggestion through the existing set-template command. A spent budget refuses before the call; with the switch off the Vy button is absent and code's suggestion still shows.
- **What a meeting left open, by Vy (#4768).** The same switch lets the recorder of a meeting that has a transcript click "Liệt kê những gì còn bỏ ngỏ" in the card "Còn bỏ ngỏ". Their own Vy reads the transcript once (at most 60,000 bytes; a longer one is refused, never cut) under its own meter line `meetings.open_points`, and lists up to five questions it did not see answered and up to five topics where a decision was raised and not taken, each citing the whole second of a real transcript line. Code drops a point whose second is no line, or whose text carries a contact, a run of 8 digits or a figure the cited line does not say. Nothing is stored (the result row holds counts only, under a random subject, so a meeting's delete has no row of it to drop); the recorder may add a point to their own notes with a click. A spent budget refuses before the call; with the switch off there is no card.
- **Sales call insights for a meeting linked to an open deal (L26, #4920).** The same switch (`MEETINGS_SUMMARY=on`) lets the recorder of a meeting linked to an open CRM deal with a transcript click "Phân tích" in the card "Nhận định cuộc gọi bán hàng". Their own Vy runs once in the foreground under its own meter line `meetings.sales_insight` (admitted on projected cost, 60 second limit, 120,000 byte transcript cap), extracting up to five customer concerns/objections, up to five buying signals, and up to five agreed next steps with owners, while code computes customer talk-time share from L16 overlap-merged durations and mapped speaker labels (or null cleanly when mapping is absent/unmapped). Code rejects any item lacking at least one existing transcript line ID of that meeting, or containing any URL, bare domain, email, phone number, or figure not present verbatim in the cited transcript lines for that item (`#4789` / L15). Nothing is stored (the result row holds counts only under a random subject) and nothing writes to the deal; with AI off, a kill switch paused, or budget spent, the card and endpoint return a clear reason and make zero model calls. Non-recorders receive 404.
- **Morning notice of the day's meetings (L14, #3955 part B).** Not an AI feature and not behind `MEETINGS_SUMMARY`: it needs no model, and nothing in it runs Vy. A member who turns it on in Notifications (off by default, own setting, a time of day in their own time zone, default 07:00) gets one in-app notice and one mail on a day they have at least one event in their synced calendar (an active connection that reads events), sent by the `meetings-morning` sweep (every 5 minutes, egress `none`; the notice is due from the chosen time for up to 6 hours, once a day, never on a day with no event, never after the member muted the kind `meetings.morning`). The notice and the mail hold the number of meetings and a link to the meetings page: no title, no time, no name, no brief. An event that two of the member's connections both cache, with the same provider event id and start, counts once. A pass takes at most 200 members in no fixed order, so if many of them fail the same way (the log shows `[meetings-morning] ... failures`) the others are still reached within a few passes. A save of the setting locks the member's membership row until it commits, so it cannot outlive a removal and is refused (403) for someone who is no longer a member. The mail needs the deployment's mail to be on (otherwise the in-app notice alone). Rollback: turn nothing on; to stop the sweep for everyone, remove the `meetings-morning` queue's schedule or stop the worker; a member turns theirs off in the same place (their row is deleted), and a member who leaves or is removed from the org loses theirs at once, so a later return finds it off.
- **Weekly notice of the week's meetings (L28, #4967).** Not an AI feature and not behind `MEETINGS_SUMMARY`: it needs no model, and nothing in it runs Vy. A member who turns it on in Notifications (off by default, own setting, a Monday time in their own time zone, default 08:00) gets one in-app notice and one mail on Monday when something is there for the "this week" card (the meetings they read with a published result or action item in the last 7 days), sent by the `meetings-weekly` sweep (every 5 minutes, egress `none`; due from the chosen time for up to 6 hours, once a week, never after the member muted the kind `meetings.weekly`). The notice and the mail hold four numbers (meetings, decisions published, open action items, overdue ones) and a link that opens the card: no title, no name, no decision. The numbers follow the card's limits (50 meetings, 30 decisions, 30 open items); a cut is said as "at least". A member who left, a disabled account, an org that is closing and an org with Meetings off get nothing. A pass takes at most 200 members in no fixed order (the log shows `[meetings-weekly] ... failures`). The mail needs the deployment's mail to be on (otherwise the in-app notice alone). Rollback: turn nothing on; to stop the sweep for everyone, remove the `meetings-weekly` queue's schedule or stop the worker; a member turns theirs off in the same place.
- **Neither one changes data on its own.** Nothing is published, sent or turned into a task without a person's click.

| P3b | `crove-media` runs (it is in the default stack and in the pilot layout; the release publishes its image and `deploy.sh` pins it by digest like `crove-extract`, #2988; it needs up to 4 GB of memory while it measures a long video, and 1 vCPU, on the host) and the API and the worker reach it at `MEDIA_URL` (default `http://crove-media:8095`; both are on the `media` network). It measures the length of every meeting recording at upload, and the transcription meter charges that measured length, never the one the client sent (#2985). Without it, or when it is down, an upload is refused with `MEDIA_PROBE_UNAVAILABLE` (503) and nothing is stored, and a transcription run fails with `TRANSCRIBE_UNAVAILABLE`. A file it cannot read is refused with `MEDIA_PROBE_FAILED` (422), one over 2 hours with `MEDIA_TOO_LONG` (413). The time one call may take is set in three places that must agree: `MEDIA_BODY_TIMEOUT_MS` and `MEDIA_TIMEOUT_MS` on `crove-media` (compose: 300000 each) and `MEDIA_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS` on the kernel (default 315000); raise all three together for a 2 GB video. The sandbox runs one measure at a time (`MEDIA_CONCURRENCY`, queue of 4): a full queue (four waiting) refuses with `MEDIA_PROBE_UNAVAILABLE`. The time allowed for a measure (`MEDIA_TIMEOUT_MS`) starts when its turn comes, not when it arrives, so a recording that waited behind another is not cut off; the API itself already admits at most two meeting uploads at a time, one per member. Raising `MEDIA_CONCURRENCY` needs `MEDIA_SCRATCH_SIZE` and `MEDIA_MEM_LIMIT` raised in proportion (the concurrency times the largest recording), or two large videos at once fill the tmpfs or the memory limit. While a measure runs, the recording sits decrypted in the sandbox's scratch directory, which is a tmpfs (`MEDIA_SCRATCH_SIZE`, default 2304m, for a 2 GB video; it counts against `MEDIA_MEM_LIMIT`, default 4g): memory only, so a killed or removed container leaves no plaintext on the host. After upgrading from a release that used the `media-scratch` volume, remove that volume once (`docker volume rm <project>_media-scratch`): it may hold the leftovers of a killed measure. **The pilot layout (`compose.pilot.yml`) holds the sandbox to about 1 GB of video** because the host has 8 GB: scratch tmpfs 1g (`MEDIA_SCRATCH_SIZE`), memory 1.5g (`MEDIA_MEM_LIMIT`), cap `MEDIA_MAX_INPUT_BYTES=1000000000` (about an hour or so of screen recording; audio, 300 MB, always fits). The API gets the same cap: a recording over it is refused with 413 `FILE_TOO_LARGE` (the message names the cap) before it is read, and the grant of a staged upload is clamped to it, so a video over about 1 GB cannot be stored on the pilot; the default stack keeps 2 GB (2304m, 4g). The three values move together (cap < scratch, scratch plus a decode < memory), and the API's and `crove-media`'s `MEDIA_MAX_INPUT_BYTES` must be the same value (compose gives both one variable). **Audio from a video (#3337):** a member may upload a video file (a local Zoom or Teams MP4) as a meeting's audio; the API seals it under a throwaway in-memory key, sends it back to `crove-media` (`POST /extract-audio`, input cap `MEDIA_MAX_INPUT_BYTES`, so up to 2 GB) and keeps only the Opus it returns. It takes the same scratch space and memory as a measure of the same video and the same single slot, and the API's video-transfer slot, so size the container for a 2 GB input (scratch 2304m, memory 4g by default) exactly as for a 2 GB video. Extraction is about 0.6 s per minute of video; the call is cut at `MEDIA_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS` (answer `MEDIA_EXTRACT_TIMEOUT`, 503). That deadline starts when the API sends the call, so it also counts the time the call waits behind another one for the sandbox's single slot (the sandbox's own `MEDIA_TIMEOUT_MS` starts only when its turn comes): a member whose extraction queues behind a 2 GB one can get the timeout answer although the sandbox would have finished it, and a retry works. A large video must be sent as the raw request body or a staged upload with `?extract=audio`; a multipart form carrying `extract` only as a field is capped at the audio size by the gate. The edge proxy's response timeout must be longer than `MEDIA_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS` (Caddy's default for `reverse_proxy` is not verified: check it at the first pilot upload of a long video). A video with no sound is refused with `NO_AUDIO_TRACK` (422). If the API dies while the temporary sealed video exists, an unreadable ciphertext object stays under `tmp/<org>/<sha256>` (its key lived only in memory; since #3341 it has its own prefix, never `org/`). The worker's daily retention run deletes every `tmp/` object older than 6 hours and logs the count (`temporary video blobs`), and the bucket rule `ludy-tmp` ([provision-pilot.md](provision-pilot.md)) expires the rest after a day, so there is nothing to check by hand. Objects an older release left under `org/` have no row and cannot be told from a real recording by their key; that comparison of `org/` keys with the `kernel.file` rows (an object with no row older than a day is an orphan) stays manual for those | `docker compose ps crove-media` is healthy; a test upload of a short recording stores with its real length | coordinator |
| P6 (transcription only) | The lawyer's answer on recording consent and on sending voice to a provider abroad (#2585), or the owner's decision to run an internal test first | recorded on #2585 | owner, lawyer |

Where each setting must show in `docker compose config` (the pilot layout runs `api` and `worker` as two services that both extend the default stack's single `crove` service, and `tools/deploy/compose.pilot.yml` resets three settings on `api` on purpose):

| Setting | Default stack (`crove`) | Pilot layout, `api` | Pilot layout, `worker` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MEETINGS_TRANSCRIBE`, `TRANSCRIBE_BASE_URL`, `TRANSCRIBE_MODEL`, `TRANSCRIBE_ALLOW_INSECURE_HTTP` | set | set (they decide whether the meeting screen offers "Nhờ Vy ghi lời") | set |
| `TRANSCRIBE_API_KEY`, `TRANSCRIBE_API_MODE`, `AI_PRICE_AUDIO_PER_MIN` | set | **empty on purpose**: the API never calls the provider, never holds its key and never prices the run | set (the worker calls the provider, holds the key and prices each chunk) |
| `CREDENTIAL_KEY` or `CREDENTIAL_KEY_FILE` | the key, or the file on the `keys` volume | the same key as the worker (P4) | the same key as the API (P4) |

So on the pilot a key, mode or price that shows on `api` is a mistake to fix, and one that is missing on `worker` is why a run fails with `TRANSCRIBE_UNAVAILABLE` or counts no cost.

The provider must speak an OpenAI-compatible API (AGENTS.md: AI calls go through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint). `TRANSCRIBE_API_MODE` says which request the adapter makes:

| Mode | Request | For |
|---|---|---|
| `audio_transcriptions` (default) | the chunk as a file to `${TRANSCRIBE_BASE_URL}/audio/transcriptions`, `verbose_json` answer | a Whisper-class model, hosted or local |
| `chat_input_audio` | the chunk as an `input_audio` part to `${TRANSCRIBE_BASE_URL}/chat/completions`, with a fixed instruction asking for JSON segments (start, end, speaker, text) in the recorder's language | a provider with no transcription endpoint, such as Gemini's OpenAI-compatible one |

In both modes the answer is untrusted data: times are clamped to the chunk, text is cut to a stated length, a label is a short plain string, and a reply that is not usable JSON is refused. The audio and the key go only to `TRANSCRIBE_BASE_URL` over https, a redirect is never followed, and nothing of the audio or the text is logged.

Example values for Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (the key is set by a person as in T1, never written here):

```
TRANSCRIBE_BASE_URL=https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai
TRANSCRIBE_MODEL=gemini-3.5-transcribe
TRANSCRIBE_API_MODE=chat_input_audio
AI_PRICE_AUDIO_PER_MIN=<the price of one minute of audio in the unit of the other AI_PRICE_* settings>
```

The `input_audio` part names the audio's format by the extension of its type (`wav`, `mp3`, `ogg`, `m4a`, ...); a type with no such name (`audio/x-m4a`, `audio/aac`) is refused by Ludy before any audio is sent, and a type the provider does not accept is refused by the provider; either way the run ends with a `TRANSCRIBE_INVALID_REQUEST` code. T3 (the two-minute test recording) is what proves a given format works with the provider. The numbers (word error rate, time per hour of audio) come from the [transcription eval](meetings-transcribe-eval.md).

## Rollback at any time

- Set the flag back to `off` and redeploy, or turn on the AI kill switch for the org. Both stop new runs at once (the kill switch also stops a run between chunks; with only the flag off, a run already going may finish).
- Nothing is lost:
  - Drafts and published notes stay.
  - Each member's private Vy results expire on their own within 31 days and are never shared.

## Cost (fill in before the owner approves)

- **Summary:** not measured yet. The tokens a summary of an hour of meeting takes are read from the AI meter during the first test runs (S3, S4) and written here; until then the cap is the budget (`AI_DEFAULT_MONTHLY_BUDGET`, or the org's own), not an estimate.
- **Unit of every price.** A deployment has one unit for all of its AI prices and budgets: the currency `AI_PRICE_INPUT_PER_MTOK` and `AI_PRICE_OUTPUT_PER_MTOK` are written in (`.env.example` says "for example USD"). `AI_PRICE_AUDIO_PER_MIN`, `AI_DEFAULT_MONTHLY_BUDGET`, an org's own budget and `ORG_LIMIT_AI_COST_PER_DAY` all count in it. The coordinator reads which unit the host's current `AI_PRICE_*` use and the owner confirms it. In USD, the provider's price per minute below is the value to write as it stands (`AI_PRICE_AUDIO_PER_MIN=0.005`; the setting takes at most 6 decimals); in another currency, multiply by the rate of the day the owner approves and note the rate and the date here. A price of 0 counts the minutes but no cost, so a hard-stop budget never refuses (`pnpm pilot:check` fails that on a public deployment).
- **Transcription:** `AI_PRICE_AUDIO_PER_MIN` × 60 per hour of audio, at the chosen provider's price. For Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, Google's page (read 2026-11-10, via web sources) lists $2 per million audio input tokens plus $12 per million text output tokens, about $0.005 per audio minute, so about $0.30 per hour of meeting; confirm it on the provider's price page before the owner approves. A request is limited to 15 minutes of audio with timestamps and speakers, and Ludy's 10-minute chunks fit.
- **Limit:** the org's AI budget caps both; a spent budget refuses before the call.
