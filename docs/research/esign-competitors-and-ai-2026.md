Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is filtered against five fixed Ludy constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md), [esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md](esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or secures an agreement task an owner, salesperson, project lead, HR manager, or bookkeeper performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** works out of the box with zero external SaaS accounts, mandatory CAPTCHAs ([#203](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/203)), desktop Word COM add-ins, LibreOffice containers, or USB PKI drivers.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls run inside `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`) with `tools: []` ([ADR-0018](../adr/0018-untrusted-content.md)). Every AI output is tainted (`Đề xuất`), deterministically verified against extracted PDF pages/quotes/numbers, and backed by an identical deterministic manual workflow when AI is off or out of budget.
4. **Human approval on commitments:** a person approves every envelope send, recipient change, obligation confirmation, playbook override, KYC confirmation, VietQR payment verification, and accounting invoice/bank-match draft.
5. **One write path, strict confidentiality & Vietnam statutory honesty:** every write executes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-evidence-storage-and-integrity.md) SHA-256 evidence triples, strict `hr_contract` (`owner`/`admin`/`created_by`) and [ADR-0047](../adr/0047-meeting-notes-audio-and-transcripts.md) `meeting_minutes` reader isolation, and explicit compliance boundaries under Law `20/2023/QH15`, Decree `23/2025/ND-CP`, Decree `337/2025/ND-CP` + Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV`, and PDPL `Law 91/2025/QH15`.

How to read this document: Ludy capabilities reflect direct repository inspection of `origin/main` (`482d41edc`) on `2026-11-26` across `modules/sign/src/`, `apps/extract/src/`, `apps/kernel/src/`, `apps/app/src/`, `packages/sign/src/`, `packages/agent/src/meter.ts`, and `supabase/migrations/`. ## 2. Global & Open-Source eSign Leaders

### 2.1 DocuSign (Core eSignature + IAM / Iris AI / Agreement Manager / Maestro)

DocuSign packages **Core eSignature** alongside **Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM)** powered by **DocuSign Iris** (AI engine), **Agreement Manager** (formerly Navigator), and **Maestro** (no-code workflow orchestration) `[S-DS1, S-DS2, S-DS3]`.

- **2026 packaging & pricing (`search summary` `2026-11-24`):**
  - Classic eSignature annual plans: *Personal* (~$11/mo, 5 envelopes/mo), *Standard* (~$30/user/mo, ~100 envelopes/user/yr), and *Business Pro* (~$45/user/mo, adding web forms, bulk send, and **payment collection during signing**) `[S-DS1]`.
  - IAM annual tiers: **IAM Starter** (~$40-$45/user/mo, basic eSign + Agreement Manager AI search + 1 Maestro workflow w/ 100 automated sends/user/yr), **IAM Standard** (~$45-$50/user/mo, 3-user min, up to 3 Maestro workflows), **IAM Professional** (~$75-$80/user/mo, 3-user min, up to 10 workflows, Web Forms, PowerForms, SSO), and **IAM Enterprise** (custom quote adding **Agreement Desk** intake/redlining and **AI-Assisted Review** playbook risk flagging) `[S-DS2, S-DS3]`.
- **Capabilities vs Ludy (`main` after Slices 1-6d & Wave A1):**
  - *Matched or adapted in Ludy:* sequential/parallel routing, `Signer`/`Approver`/`CC`, in-flight recipient correction & audited delegation (`Slice 4c`), `dropdown`/`radio` + signer attachments (`Slice 4b`), corporate `stamp` (`Slice 4a`), pre-expiry reminders (`Slice 3c`), post-signature obligation & renewal alerts (`Slice 4e`), Contract Vault Q&A (`Slice 5a`), SME Playbook checks & cited redlines (`Slice 5d`), sender-approved static counterparty summary (`Slice 4f`, avoiding unauthenticated public LLM budget drain), QR scan-to-sign (`Slice 6a`), `.docx` ingestion (`Slice 6b`), multi-document packets (`Slice 6c`), and VietQR deposit collection (`Slice 6d`).
  - *Intentional differences / deferred:* DocuSign Enterprise offers cross-department Maestro conditional branching, bulk CSV send, and live SaaS Iris Q&A for external signers (`bulk send` and complex enterprise workflow builders stay in Ludy's Icebox per [scope.md](../product/scope.md)).

### 2.2 Documenso (Open-Source AGPL Reference)

Documenso remains the closest open-source architectural peer to Ludy eSign (TypeScript, PostgreSQL, AGPL-3.0) `[S-DM1, S-DM2]`.

- **2026 pricing & hosting (`search summary` `2026-11-24`):**
  - Managed Cloud: *Free* ($0, 5 docs/mo, up to 10 recipients), *Individual* ($25/mo annual), *Teams* ($40/mo annual for 5 users + $8/extra), *Platform* ($250/mo annual for embedded/API).
  - Self-Hosted: **Community Edition (`AGPL-3.0`, `$0` unlimited)** plus commercial Business Edition (fixed yearly license) and Enterprise Edition (AATL certificates, SLAs) `[S-DM1]`.
- **Capabilities vs Ludy (`main` after Slices 1-6d & Wave A1):**
  - *Matched by Ludy:* Direct Templates (`/-/sign/d#<token>`), Public Signing Profiles (`/-/sign/p/$orgSlug`), Saved Signatures (`Slice 4a`), X.509/CA architecture (`Slice 2c`), **`.docx` conversion** (`Slice 6b`, pure-JS zero-egress in `crove-extract` vs Documenso's LibreOffice server-side conversion), and **multi-document packets** (`Slice 6c`, 1-5 PDFs).
  - *Where Ludy goes further for Vietnam SMEs:* first-class 1-click `ký nháy` + red `stamp`, isolated In-Person kiosk mode, `KYC` MST/CCCD verification, QR mobile scan handoff + Zalo OA Engage insertion, VietQR deposit + `Ludy Kế toán` match bridge, and `10` metered `Vy` AI workflows with 100% AI-off fallback.

### 2.3 DocuSeal (Open-Core Form-First Reference)

DocuSeal targets fast form completion, embedded PDF text tags, and mobile/tablet execution `[S-DC1, S-DC2]`.

- **2026 pricing & hosting (`search summary` `2026-11-24`):**
  - *Cloud Basic* ($0, 10 signature request emails/mo), *Cloud Pro* ($20/user/mo or $200/yr + $0.20/completed API doc in production), *Open Source self-hosted* (`$0`, community/AGPL core), and *Pro On-Premises* ($20/user/mo annual for white-labeling, SSO, API). Add-ons: SMS verification ($0.20), ID verification ($2), EU QES ($2-$4) `[S-DC1]`.
- **Capabilities vs Ludy (`main` after Slices 1-6d & Wave A1):**
  - *Matched by Ludy:* structured `dropdown`/`radio`/`stamp`/`attachment` fields (`Slice 4a`-`4b`), deterministic `{{tag}}` parsing (`Slice 2a` & `4b`), In-Person Signing (`Slice 4d`), MCP read tools (`Slice 5c`), native `.docx` ingestion (`Slice 6b`), multi-document submission packets (`Slice 6c`), QR scan-to-sign mobile handoff (`Slice 6a`), and local payment collection adapted via **NAPAS VietQR + `Ludy Kế toán` bank matching** (`Slice 6d`) instead of Stripe card links.
  - *Where Ludy stays ahead / self-contained:* Ludy keeps Pro-equivalent features (white-label-free multi-member orgs, REST API, audit history, AI workflows) in pure `AGPL-3.0` with zero per-doc API fees (`$0.20/doc` on DocuSeal Cloud Pro), while DocuSeal's SMS OTP & EU QES add-ons correspond to email OTP + pluggable VN Public CAs (`#62`) in Ludy.

### 3.2 Critical 2026 Legal Shift: Decree `337/2025/ND-CP` & Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV` (`2026-07-01`)

Live verification on `2026-11-24` confirms major statutory enforcement changes in Vietnam beyond the baseline documented in [esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md](esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md) `[S-LEG1, S-LEG2]`:

1. **National Electronic Labor Contract Platform Live (`2026-07-01`):** Implementing **Decree `337/2025/ND-CP`** (`2025-12-24`), the Ministry of Home Affairs (`Bộ Nội vụ`) issued **Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV`** (`2026-05-15`, effective **`2026-07-01`**) operating the centralized **National Electronic Labor Contract Platform** `[S-LEG1, S-LEG2]`.
2. **Mandatory `VNeID` (`C06`) Portal Authentication:** Under Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV`, employees (personal `VNeID`) and employers (organizational `VNeID`) accessing, registering, executing, or querying statutory electronic labor contracts on the national platform authenticate via **`VNeID`** backed by the Ministry of Public Security (`C06`) national population database `[S-LEG1, S-LEG2]`.
3. **13-Character National eContract ID (`24-hour` Sync):** Every compliant statutory electronic labor contract submitted by a qualified `eContract` provider within **24 hours** of completion receives a permanent **13-character ID** (`1` letter prefix `A` = new post-`2026-07-01` e-contract, `B` = digitized from paper, `C` = pre-`2026-07-01` e-contract + `2`-digit year + `10`-digit unique number) that persists across addenda, suspensions, and terminations `[S-LEG2]`.
4. **Architecture decision (`Slice 5a` + deferred `Slice 6e`):**
   - `Slice 5a` (`#4439` / `#4456`) explicitly warns HR users on `hr_contract` envelopes that standalone drawn/OTP signing does **not** replace Ministry-registered statutory electronic labor contracts (while covering internal HR notices, offer letters, NDA/IP addenda, CTV service agreements, and B2B commercial contracts cleanly under `Law 20/2023/QH15` `Art. 22.4` / `Art. 23`).
   - Accessing `C06 / VNeID` and `Bộ Nội vụ` APIs requires formal corporate vetting, paid intermediary contracts, and `#62` counsel sign-off, failing `scope.md`'s zero-setup `docker compose up` gate. `Slice 6e` therefore remains deferred pending `#62`, partner credentials, and pilot demand.
