Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is filtered against five fixed Ludy constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md), [esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md](esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or secures an agreement task an owner, salesperson, project lead, HR manager, or bookkeeper performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** works out of the box with zero external SaaS accounts, mandatory CAPTCHAs ([#203](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/203)), desktop Word COM add-ins, or USB PKI drivers.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls run inside `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`) with `tools: []` ([ADR-0018](../adr/0018-untrusted-content.md)). Every AI output is tainted (`Đề xuất`), deterministically verified against extracted PDF pages/quotes/numbers, and backed by an identical deterministic manual workflow when AI is off or out of budget.
4. **Human approval on commitments:** a person approves every envelope send, recipient change, obligation confirmation, playbook override, KYC confirmation, and accounting invoice draft.
5. **One write path, strict confidentiality & Vietnam statutory honesty:** every write executes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-evidence-storage-and-integrity.md) SHA-256 evidence triples, strict `hr_contract` (`owner`/`admin`/`created_by`) and [ADR-0047](../adr/0047-meeting-notes-audio-and-transcripts.md) `meeting_minutes` reader isolation, and explicit compliance boundaries under Law `20/2023/QH15`, Decree `23/2025/ND-CP`, Decree `337/2025/ND-CP` + Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV`, and PDPL `Law 91/2025/QH15`.

## 2. Global & Open-Source eSign Leaders (2026 Verified)

### 2.1 DocuSign (Core eSignature + IAM / Iris AI / Agreement Manager / Maestro)

DocuSign packages **Core eSignature** alongside **Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM)** powered by **DocuSign Iris** (AI engine), **Agreement Manager** (formerly Navigator), and **Maestro** (no-code workflow orchestration) `[S-DS1, S-DS2, S-DS3]`.

- **2026 packaging & pricing (`search summary` `2026-11-24`):**
  - Classic eSignature annual plans: *Personal* (~$11/mo, 5 envelopes/mo), *Standard* (~$30/user/mo, ~100 envelopes/user/yr), and *Business Pro* (~$45/user/mo, adding web forms, bulk send, and **payment collection during signing**) `[S-DS1]`.
  - IAM annual tiers: **IAM Starter** (~$40-$45/user/mo, basic eSign + Agreement Manager AI search + 1 Maestro workflow w/ 100 automated sends/user/yr), **IAM Standard** (~$45-$50/user/mo, 3-user min, up to 3 Maestro workflows), **IAM Professional** (~$75-$80/user/mo, 3-user min, up to 10 workflows, Web Forms, PowerForms, SSO), and **IAM Enterprise** (custom quote adding **Agreement Desk** intake/redlining and **AI-Assisted Review** playbook risk flagging) `[S-DS2, S-DS3]`.
- **Capabilities vs Ludy after Slice 5:**
  - *Already matched or adapted in Ludy:* sequential/parallel routing, `Signer`/`Approver`/`CC`, in-flight recipient correction & audited delegation (`Slice 4c`), `dropdown`/`radio` + signer attachments (`Slice 4b`), corporate `stamp` (`Slice 4a`), pre-expiry reminders (`Slice 3c`), post-signature obligation & renewal alerts (`Slice 4e`), Contract Vault Q&A (`Slice 5a`), SME Playbook checks & cited redlines (`Slice 5d`), and sender-approved static counterparty summary (`Slice 4f`, avoiding unauthenticated public LLM budget drain).
  - *Genuine remaining gaps for SMEs:* **multi-document envelopes** (bundling contract + annex in one packet), **payment collection during/after signing**, and **QR/SMS desktop-to-mobile signing handoffs**.

### 2.2 Documenso (Open-Source AGPL Reference)

Documenso remains the closest open-source architectural peer to Ludy eSign (TypeScript, PostgreSQL, AGPL-3.0) `[S-DM1, S-DM2]`.

- **2026 pricing & hosting (`search summary` `2026-11-24`):**
  - Managed Cloud: *Free* ($0, 5 docs/mo, up to 10 recipients), *Individual* ($25/mo annual), *Teams* ($40/mo annual for 5 users + $8/extra), *Platform* ($250/mo annual for embedded/API).
  - Self-Hosted: **Community Edition (`AGPL-3.0`, `$0` unlimited)** plus commercial Business Edition (fixed yearly license) and Enterprise Edition (AATL certificates, SLAs) `[S-DM1]`.
- **Capabilities vs Ludy after Slice 5:**
  - *Matched by Ludy:* Direct Templates (`/-/sign/d#<token>`), Public Signing Profiles (`/-/sign/p/$orgSlug`), Saved Signatures (`Slice 4a`), X.509/CA architecture (`Slice 2c`), and far richer AI/SME lifecycle features inside Ludy (`Vy` field/clause/obligation/playbook/KYC/vault).
  - *Remaining Documenso advantages to evaluate for Slice 6:* **`.docx` upload conversion** (Documenso converts `.docx` server-side via LibreOffice, though official docs warn of occasional font/layout shifts vs direct PDF export) and **multi-document envelopes** `[S-DM1]`.

### 2.3 DocuSeal (Open-Core Form-First Reference)

DocuSeal targets fast form completion, embedded PDF text tags, and mobile/tablet execution `[S-DC1, S-DC2]`.

- **2026 pricing & hosting (`search summary` `2026-11-24`):**
  - *Cloud Basic* ($0, 10 signature request emails/mo), *Cloud Pro* ($20/user/mo or $200/yr + $0.20/completed API doc in production), *Open Source self-hosted* (`$0`, community/AGPL core), and *Pro On-Premises* ($20/user/mo annual for white-labeling, SSO, API). Add-ons: SMS verification ($0.20), ID verification ($2), EU QES ($2-$4) `[S-DC1]`.
- **Capabilities vs Ludy after Slice 5:**
  - *Matched by Ludy:* structured `dropdown`/`radio`/`stamp`/`attachment` fields (`Slice 4a`-`4b`), deterministic `{{tag}}` parsing (`Slice 2a` & `4b`), In-Person Signing (`Slice 4d`), and MCP read tools (`Slice 5c`, matching DocuSeal's AI agent integrations).
  - *Remaining DocuSeal advantages to evaluate for Slice 6:* **native `.docx` -> signing template conversion**, **multi-document submission packets (*Add Document*)**, **Stripe payment links inside signing forms**, and **QR scan-to-sign mobile/on-site handoffs** `[S-DC1, S-DC2]`.

### 3.2 Critical 2026 Legal Shift: Decree `337/2025/ND-CP` & Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV` (`2026-07-01`)

Live verification on `2026-11-24` confirms major statutory enforcement changes in Vietnam beyond the baseline documented in [esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md](esign-vietnam-legal-2026.md) `[S-LEG1, S-LEG2]`:

1. **National Electronic Labor Contract Platform Live (`2026-07-01`):** Implementing **Decree `337/2025/ND-CP`** (`2025-12-24`), the Ministry of Home Affairs (`Bộ Nội vụ`) issued **Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV`** (`2026-05-15`, effective **`2026-07-01`**) operating the centralized **National Electronic Labor Contract Platform** `[S-LEG1, S-LEG2]`.
2. **Mandatory `VNeID` (`C06`) Portal Authentication:** Under Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV`, employees (personal `VNeID`) and employers (organizational `VNeID`) accessing, registering, executing, or querying statutory electronic labor contracts on the national platform authenticate via **`VNeID`** backed by the Ministry of Public Security (`C06`) national population database `[S-LEG1, S-LEG2]`.
3. **13-Character National eContract ID (`24-hour` Sync):** Every compliant statutory electronic labor contract submitted by a qualified `eContract` provider within **24 hours** of completion receives a permanent **13-character ID** (`1` letter prefix `A` = new post-`2026-07-01` e-contract, `B` = digitized from paper, `C` = pre-`2026-07-01` e-contract + `2`-digit year + `10`-digit unique number) that persists across addenda, suspensions, and terminations `[S-LEG2]`.
4. **Architecture impact on Ludy Slice 6:**
   - **Why `Slice 5a` (`#4439` / `#4456`) was the right call:** Ludy honestly warns HR users on `hr_contract` envelopes that standalone drawn/OTP signing does **not** replace Ministry-registered statutory electronic labor contracts (while covering internal HR notices, offer letters, NDA/IP addenda, CTV service agreements, and B2B commercial contracts cleanly under `Law 20/2023/QH15` `Art. 22.4` / `Art. 23`).
   - **Why `VNeID` / `C06` / `Bộ Nội vụ` cannot be a mandatory Slice 6 default:** Accessing `C06 / VNeID` and `Bộ Nội vụ` APIs requires formal corporate vetting, paid intermediary contracts, and `#62` counsel sign-off, failing `scope.md`'s zero-setup `docker compose up` gate. Any technical adapter work must wait behind `#62`, partner credentials, and explicit pilot customer pull.

## 6. Genuine Remaining Gaps & Strict Scope-Gate Summary ([scope.md](../product/scope.md))

With Slices 1 through 5 complete on `main`, Ludy eSign already matches or surpasses most global and Vietnamese peers on core signing, Vietnamese `ký nháy`/`stamp`/`KYC`, AI contract review (`9` metered `Vy` features with strict `tools: []` containment), webhooks, MCP/REST APIs, and cross-suite handoffs. Four authentic SME friction points plus one regulatory gate remain:

1. **QR Scan-to-Sign Handoff & Zalo OA Engage Composer (`Slice 6a` candidate, Wave 1):** Counterparties opening `/-/sign#<token>` on a desktop struggle to draw signatures with a trackpad, and in-person counter signers prefer scanning a QR onto their own phone (`apps/app/src/lib/qr-svg.ts`) rather than touching a staff tablet. Meanwhile, sales staff can insert signing links directly into active `Ludy Engage` Zalo OA threads (`human sends`), avoiding mandatory paid Zalo ZNS accounts.
2. **Native `.docx` Upload -> Tagged A4 PDF Conversion (`Slice 6b` candidate, Wave 1):** Vietnamese counterparties still trade `.docx` Word drafts over Zalo and email. Extending `crove-extract` (`apps/extract/src/docx-text.ts` + `pdf-lib` + `Be Vietnam Pro`) to convert `.docx` to tagged A4 PDF inside the zero-egress sandbox removes manual Word-to-PDF export friction without bloated LibreOffice binaries or desktop Word COM plugins.
3. **Multi-Document Signing Packet (`Slice 6c` candidate, Wave 2):** Real SME engagements bundle `Hợp đồng chính` + `Phụ lục báo giá` + `NDA` into one transaction. Supporting 1 to 5 ordered documents inside a single bounded envelope (`<= 200` total pages) eliminates multiple signing links/OTPs and external PDF-merger tools.
4. **VietQR Deposit / Milestone Payment & `Ludy Kế toán` Bank-Match Bridge (`Slice 6d` candidate, Wave 2):** Localizing Agree.com's contract-to-cash insight for Vietnam via NAPAS VietQR deposit instructions (`CK-<code>`, `NUMERIC(20,4)`) and human-confirmed bank-feed matching in `Ludy Kế toán` (zero escrow/PSP custody).
5. **Conditional / Deferred Gate (`Slice 6e` candidate) & Explicit Icebox:** `VNeID`/C06 identity verification and Circular `08/2026/TT-BNV` 13-character Ministry of Home Affairs eContract ID submission require legal sign-off ([#62](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/62)), paid partner contracts, and real pilot pull; **bulk CSV spam campaigns, Word desktop COM add-ins, hardware USB PKI token drivers, multi-entity SSO/SCIM, white-label agency consoles, and regulated payment-intermediary custody stay strictly in the Icebox**.

Full candidate scorecards, risk/effort tables, Wave recommendations, and owner decision options are detailed in [esign-slice6-proposal.md](esign-slice6-proposal.md) and [esign-slice6-proposal.vi.md](esign-slice6-proposal.vi.md).
