Remaining HRM work is strictly tracked across 3 external `owner-action` gates ([#2535](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2535), [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536), [#2537](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2537)) and the 7 reopened P3 quality and database-hardening issues approved into `M3 Paid pilot` by owner JOY (`A1`, `2026-11-25`, [#1210](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/1210#issuecomment-7150912773)), while Section 4.3 non-SME enterprise/biometric items stay explicitly out of scope unless approved. Standalone Vietnamese summary: [hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md](hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md).

Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is weighed against Ludy's five fixed architecture constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or protects an HR, timekeeping, leave, payroll, compliance, or hiring task a Vietnamese small business performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** runs in one PostgreSQL database with zero external SaaS dependencies, hardware lock-in, or per-seat license floor.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls go through `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`), pseudonymize employee/candidate identifiers (`P1`, `C1` / `E1..En`), undergo deterministic verification (zero hallucinated numbers), and work cleanly with AI off.
4. **Human approval on money, hires, and statutory commitments:** AI never approves a payroll run, sends a bank payment, sends a candidate offer/rejection, issues a labor contract addendum, or files a statutory submission alone.
5. **One write path and strict Vietnam privacy/AI compliance:** every write routes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, `legal_entity_id` scoping, PDPL erasure (`Law 91/2025/QH15`, effective `2026-01-01`), and Ludy's human-approval rule aligned with human-oversight principles under `AI Law 134/2025/QH15` (`2026-03-01`; formal classification of HR tools remains pending lawyer review [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)).

## 3. Direct Matchup Against MISA AMIS HRM Screenshots & Market Reality (`v0.3.11`)

1. **Price & Integrated Stack Advantage (`0 VND` license vs `37.8M-54M+ VND/yr` before Accounting & Recruiting):**
   - MISA AMIS HRM bundles `Standard` (`3.15M/mo` = `37.8M/yr`) and `Professional` (`4.5M/mo` = `54M/yr`) for `<=200` staff (`S-MI4`) **and still prices `AMIS Tuyen dung` (ATS) and `AMIS Ke toan` (`TT133/TT99` GL) separately**; Base/1Office/Tanca/Terra charge recurring per-seat fees (often with `2M-30M+ VND` upfront setup fees); Zoho People/Lark Suite lack native Vietnam Payroll, `BHXH`, `TNCN`, `01/PLI`, and `TT133/99`.
   - Ludy `v0.3.11` delivers Core HRIS (`23` `ludy_hr.*` tables), Onboarding, Attendance (PWA/QR), 2-level Leave, OT & Business Trips, Chat AI Request Drafting & Cited Policy Q&A (`6A`), Salary Advances & Retro/Offset Payroll Adjustments (`6B`), Contract Addendums PDF -> eSign, Career Timeline & Asset Handover (`6C`), Lightweight Quarterly OKRs linked to `Ludy Cong tac` (`6D`), Payroll & diff preview, Bank files, Declarations, ATS Recruiting (`crove-extract` + Funnel BI + `Engage`), Probation Reviews, **native double-entry `TT133/TT99` Accounting (`334/338/3335/642` salary-isolated journals)**, and **`Ludy Ky ket` e-contracts** inside one `AGPL-3.0` `docker compose up` stack.
2. **PDPL `91/2025/QH15` + AI Law `134/2025/QH15` Architecture Advantage:**
   - Effective `2026-01-01` and `2026-03-01`, Vietnam's PDP Law (`91/2025/QH15`) mandates purpose minimization, erasing rejected candidates and post-retention ex-employees, biometric consent, and cross-border transfer controls; Vietnam's AI Law (`134/2025/QH15`) sets deployer accountability and human-oversight rules (`Decision 33/2026/QD-TTg` lists 46 initial high-risk AI systems omitting employment; formal HR legal classification pending [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)) `[S-VN1, S-VN2]`.
   - Ludy self-hosts in one PostgreSQL DB, pseudonymizes identifiers prior to AI calls, blocks AI-generated pay numbers or autonomous hiring/payroll/addendum/OKR commits, preserves `100%` AI-off parity across all **`12` `Ly` features**, avoids mandatory FaceID biometric storage (`Stage 5C`), writes `ludy:s1:` sealed secrets (PR [#4620](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4620)), and provides cryptographic per-subject PDPL erasure across employee, candidate, onboarding, advance, asset, and OKR tables.
3. **All Four Stage 6 SME Gaps Closed in `v0.3.11`; What Remains Open Now:**
   - Stage 6 (`Step 0` PR [#4560](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4560), `6A` PR [#4575](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4575), `6B` PR [#4590](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4590), `6C` PR [#4604](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4604), `6D` PR [#4673](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4673)) **closed all four SME gaps** identified against MISA AMIS HRM (`S-MI4`), ADP Assist, Rippling AI, Zoho Zia, and Lark OKR in v3 (Quick Comparison Matrix rows 10 to 13 are now shipped `Yes` in `v0.3.11`).
   - That leaves **no further feature-expansion wave queued before pilot validation**; remaining work consists of:
     1. **3 external `owner-action` compliance & ground-truth gates:** [#2535](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2535) (partner accountant `#61` confirming statutory payroll rules and worked examples), [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536) (lawyer PDPL/retention/AI Law HR classification & HTKK XSD review, which also gates HTKK XML and Icebox payslip email [#2539](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2539)), and [#2537](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2537) (anonymized real SME payroll month & filed documents).
     2. **7 reopened P3 quality & database-hardening follow-ups (`M3 Paid pilot`, owner approval `A1`, `2026-11-25`):** [#4486](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4486), [#4487](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4487), [#4488](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4488), [#4597](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4597), [#4654](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4654), [#4676](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4676), and [#4679](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4679) (detailed in Section 4.2).
     3. **Conscious SME scope exclusions (Section 4.3):** native mobile apps, biometric FaceID hardware, heavy factory split-shifts, global EOR, MDM/IT shipping, autonomous AI hiring/filing, and 360/9-box/LMS enterprise suites stay explicitly out of scope unless the owner approves.
