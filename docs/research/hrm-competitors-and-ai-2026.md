Standalone Vietnamese summary: [hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md](hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md).

Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is weighed against Ludy's five fixed architecture constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or protects an HR, timekeeping, leave, payroll, compliance, or hiring task a Vietnamese small business performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** runs in one PostgreSQL database with zero external SaaS dependencies, hardware lock-in, or per-seat license floor.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls go through `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`), pseudonymize employee/candidate identifiers (`P1`, `C1` / `E1..En`), undergo deterministic verification (zero hallucinated numbers), and work cleanly with AI off.
4. **Human approval on money, hires, and statutory commitments:** AI never approves a payroll run, sends a bank payment, sends a candidate offer/rejection, issues a labor contract addendum, or files a statutory submission alone.
5. **One write path and strict Vietnam privacy/AI compliance:** every write routes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, `legal_entity_id` scoping, PDPL erasure (`Law 91/2025/QH15`, effective `2026-01-01`), and Ludy's human-approval rule aligned with human-oversight principles under `AI Law 134/2025/QH15` (`2026-03-01`; formal classification of HR tools remains pending lawyer review [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)).

## 1. Where Ludy Nhan su Stands Today & "Are All AI Features Done?"

- **Short answer: YES, `100%` of the Stage 1-6 SME HR AI roadmap is built, live in `v0.3.11` (`pilot`), and hardened on `main`.** All **`13` metered `Ly`/payroll AI capabilities (`packages/agent/src/meter.ts` & `payroll-match.ts`)** plus **zero-LLM `Vy Today` compliance alerts (`hr.today`)** run with full AI-off deterministic fallbacks and strict human-approval gates. Only 2 tiny non-blocking P3 polish follow-ups stay in `Icebox` (`#4840`, `#4764`), alongside consciously excluded invasive/enterprise AI categories (autonomous video AI scoring, unconsented talent scraping, autonomous government filings, biometric cameras; Section 4.4).
- **Platform baseline (`v0.3.11` live vs `main`):**
  - **`23` `ludy_hr.*` tables live in `v0.3.11` -> `24` `ludy_hr.*` tables on `main`** (`+1` `ludy_hr.candidate_stage_transition` via PR [#4827](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4827); `0` `crove_hr.*` tables remain in `packages/db/src/generated/database.ts`, while `5` ADR-0054 Category B AAD strings `crove_hr.payroll_employee.*_sealed` stay intentionally preserved in `modules/hr/src/employees.ts` so pre-cutover encrypted PII decrypts cleanly and new writes use `ludy:s1:` from PR [#4620](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4620)).
  - **`7` HR routes** in `apps/app/src/router.tsx`: `/hr/employees`, `/hr/attendance`, `/hr/recruiting`, `/hr/payroll`, `/hr/declarations`, `/hr/bank-templates`, `/hr/my` (Stages 6A-6D integrated directly with zero route bloat).
  - **All `13` metered AI features (`8` `hr.*` + `5` payroll/labor `finance.*`) + `hr.today` (`0` LLM):** every metered call enforces `withAiScope` + `metered()`, randomizes or pseudonymizes subject aliases (`P1`/`C1` or `E1..En`), applies pre-call `PayrollPromptLeakError` guards and post-call grounding checks (zero hallucinated digits/links/autonomous directives), marks drafts tainted for explicit user confirmation, and degrades deterministically when AI is off or over budget.

## 3. Direct Matchup Against MISA AMIS HRM, Base.vn HRM+ & Market Reality

1. **Price & Integrated Stack Advantage (`0 VND` license vs `37.8M-54M+ VND/yr` before Accounting & Recruiting):**
   - **MISA AMIS HRM** bundles `Standard` (`3.15M/mo` = `37.8M/yr`) and `Professional` (`4.5M/mo` = `54M/yr`) for `<=200` staff (`S-MI4`) **and still prices `AMIS Tuyen dung` (ATS) and `AMIS Ke toan` (`TT133/TT99` GL) separately**. **Base.vn HRM+** bills per-seat/per-app across fragmented modules (`~450k VND/mo` base up to `~149k-169k VND/user/mo` for Agentic AI tiers) and lacks a complete native `TT133/99` ledger; 1Office/Tanca/Terra charge recurring PEPM fees (often with `2M-30M+ VND` upfront setup fees); Zoho People and Lark Suite lack native Vietnam Payroll, `BHXH`, `TNCN`, `01/PLI`, and `TT133/99`.

2. **PDPL `91/2025/QH15` + AI Law `134/2025/QH15` Architecture Advantage:**
   - Effective `2026-01-01` and `2026-03-01`, Vietnam's PDP Law (`91/2025/QH15`) mandates purpose minimization, erasing rejected candidates and post-retention ex-employees, biometric consent, and cross-border transfer controls; Vietnam's AI Law (`134/2025/QH15`) sets deployer accountability and human-oversight rules (`Decision 33/2026/QD-TTg` lists 46 initial high-risk AI systems omitting employment; formal HR legal classification pending [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)) `[S-VN1, S-VN2]`.
