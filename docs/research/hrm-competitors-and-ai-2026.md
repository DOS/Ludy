Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is weighed against Ludy's five fixed architecture constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or protects an HR, timekeeping, leave, payroll, compliance, or hiring task a Vietnamese small business performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** runs in one PostgreSQL database with zero external SaaS dependencies, hardware lock-in, or per-seat license floor.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls go through `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`), pseudonymize employee/candidate identifiers (`P1`, `C1` / `E1..En`), undergo deterministic verification (zero hallucinated numbers), and work cleanly with AI off.
4. **Human approval on money, hires, and statutory commitments:** AI never approves a payroll run, sends a bank payment, sends a candidate offer/rejection, or files a statutory submission alone.
5. **One write path and strict Vietnam privacy/AI compliance:** every write routes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, `legal_entity_id` scoping, PDPL erasure (`Law 91/2025/QH15`, effective `2026-01-01`), and Ludy's human-approval rule aligned with human-oversight principles under `AI Law 134/2025/QH15` (`2026-03-01`; formal classification of HR tools remains pending lawyer review [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)).

## 3. Direct Matchup Against MISA AMIS HRM Screenshots & Market Reality (`v0.3.8`)

1. **Price & Integrated Stack Advantage (`0 VND` license vs `37.8M-54M+ VND/yr` before Accounting & Recruiting):**
   - MISA AMIS HRM bundles `Standard` (`3.15M/mo` = `37.8M/yr`) and `Professional` (`4.5M/mo` = `54M/yr`) for `<=200` staff (`S-MI4`) **and still prices `AMIS Tuyen dung` (ATS) and `AMIS Ke toan` (`TT133/TT99` GL) separately**; Base/1Office/Tanca/Terra charge recurring per-seat fees (often with `2M-30M+ VND` upfront setup fees); Zoho People/Lark Suite lack native Vietnam Payroll, `BHXH`, `TNCN`, `01/PLI`, and `TT133/99`.
   - Ludy `v0.3.8` delivers Core HRIS, Onboarding, Attendance (PWA/QR), 2-level Leave & OT, Payroll & diff preview, Bank files, Declarations, ATS Recruiting (`crove-extract` + Funnel BI + `Engage`), Probation Reviews (`Ludy Cong tac`), **native double-entry `TT133/TT99` Accounting (`334/338/3335/642` salary-isolated journals)**, and **`Ludy Ky ket` e-contracts** inside one `AGPL-3.0` `docker compose up` stack.
2. **PDPL `91/2025/QH15` + AI Law `134/2025/QH15` Architecture Advantage:**
   - Effective `2026-01-01` and `2026-03-01`, Vietnam's PDP Law (`91/2025/QH15`) mandates purpose minimization, erasing rejected candidates and post-retention ex-employees, biometric consent, and cross-border transfer controls; Vietnam's AI Law (`134/2025/QH15`) sets deployer accountability and human-oversight rules (`Decision 33/2026/QD-TTg` lists 46 initial high-risk AI systems omitting employment; formal HR legal classification pending `#2536`) `[S-VN1, S-VN2]`.
   - Ludy self-hosts in one PostgreSQL DB, pseudonymizes identifiers prior to AI calls, blocks AI-generated pay numbers or autonomous hiring/payroll commits, preserves `100%` AI-off parity across all `8` `Ly` features, avoids mandatory FaceID biometric storage (`Stage 5C`), and provides cryptographic per-subject PDPL erasure.
3. **What Remains Open After Stage 5 Shipped in `v0.3.8` (4 Concrete Stage 6 Candidates):**
   - Stage 5 (`5A`-`5F`, **now live in `v0.3.8`**) closed **Vy `Hom nay` alerts** (`5A`), **Web/PWA + QR attendance** (`5C`), **CV PDF/DOCX parse + Funnel BI + Zalo/FB invite drafts** (`5D`), **payroll diff preview** (`5E`), **onboarding checklist + 2-level OT requests -> payroll + probation review w/ Projects tasks** (`5B`), and post-erasure polish (`5F`).

     1. **`AI nop don tu` & Cited HR Policy Q&A (`MISA AVA [Hot]`, `Rippling AI`, `ADP Assist`, `Zoho Zia`, `Bob AI`)**: employee types `"xin nghi phep chieu thu 6"` or asks `"phep nam con may ngay?"` inside `/hr/my` -> `Ly` drafts a structured request or answers from company handbook docs in `Ludy Ghi chu` with **clickable policy citations** (`human confirms submit`).
     2. **Salary Advance, Payroll Retro/Offset Lines & Business Trip Requests (`tam ung`, `truy thu`, `bu tru`, `don cong tac` in MISA `Tien luong`/`Nhan vien`)**: structured mid-month salary advance (`tam ung`) & retro adjustment (`truy thu`/`bu tru`) feeding `decimal.js` net pay + business trip request (`don cong tac`).
