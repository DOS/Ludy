# Global, Vietnam SME and AI-Native HRM Benchmark (2026-11-22, v2)

Status: updated research benchmark, `2026-11-22` (at owner JOY's request, integrating 7 live MISA AMIS HRM pricing/feature screenshots captured on `2026-11-22`, Lark Suite, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Gusto, and rising AI-native talent platforms Mercor, Juicebox/PeopleGPT, and micro1/Zara AI). Stage 5 candidates `5A`-`5F` below were approved on `2026-11-22` ([coordinator decision #1210](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/1210#issuecomment-7079235419)); Stage 6 candidates in section 4.2 remain **research only with no issues opened or code started until the owner decides**. Standalone Vietnamese summary: [hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md](hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md).

Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is weighed against Ludy's five fixed architecture constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or protects an HR, timekeeping, leave, payroll, compliance, or hiring task a Vietnamese small business performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** runs in one PostgreSQL database with zero external SaaS dependencies, hardware lock-in, or per-seat license floor.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls go through `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`), pseudonymize employee/candidate identifiers (`P1`, `C1`), undergo deterministic verification (zero hallucinated numbers), and work cleanly with AI off.
4. **Human approval on money, hires, and statutory commitments:** AI never approves a payroll run, sends a bank payment, sends a candidate offer/rejection, or files a statutory submission alone.
5. **One write path and strict Vietnam privacy/AI compliance:** every write routes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, `legal_entity_id` scoping, PDPL erasure (`Law 91/2025/QH15`, effective `2026-01-01`), and Ludy's human-approval rule aligned with human-oversight principles under `AI Law 134/2025/QH15` (`2026-03-01`; formal classification of HR tools remains pending lawyer review [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)).

How to read this document: Ludy baseline capabilities reflect `origin/main` / `v0.3.7` (Stages 1 to 4 shipped through PR [#4334](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4334) and PR [#4345](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4345)) plus approved Stage 5 issues `#4444` (5A), `#4448` (5B), `#4447` (5C), `#4446` (5D), `#4445` (5E), and `#4351` (5F). ## 1. Where Ludy Nhan su Stands Today (`v0.3.7` Shipped + Stage 5 Building)

Built in `v0.3.7` across 11 `crove_hr.*` tables, 6 workspace routes, sealed PDPL columns (`SEALED_COLUMNS` + `PERSONAL_DATA_COLUMNS`), and 5 metered `Ly` (`COWORKER_BY_MODULE.hr = 'finance'`) AI features:

- **Core HRIS, contracts, and terminations (`/hr/employees`):** employee & employment history, department structure with L1 manager assignment, dependent registry, deterministic Vietnamese Labor Contract PDF generation + `eSign` ceremony handoff, probation/contract timelines, Art. 46 severance and Art. 47 job-loss allowance, foreign worker tracking (resident 5-35% progressive vs non-resident 20% flat PIT, 12-month BHXH flag, masked work permit expiry alert), `So quan ly lao dong` + semi-annual `01/PLI` labor report, and retention-gated PDPL erasure (`hr.payroll.employee.erase`) that also wipes leave-request PII.
- **Leave & self-service portal (`/hr/my`):** annual leave ledger, CSV timekeeping import into payroll runs, `/o/$slug/hr/my` self-service portal for linked `member` roles (leave balance, submit/cancel with overlap warning, view approved/paid/closed payslips only + deterministic VN breakdown when AI is off, L1 manager approval -> final owner/admin approval atomically posting into `payroll_leave_entry` without allowing self-approval except audited sole-owner OPC; `viewer` blocked at UI/RPC/RLS).
- **Payroll calculation, bank files, and accounting handoff (`/hr/payroll`, `/hr/bank-templates`):** monthly, hourly, and piece-rate calculation; statutory BHXH/BHYT/BHTN/union caps and sickness/maternity exclusion; progressive PIT & 13th-month bonus (**new statutory rates marked `status: 'commentary'` until partner accountant `#61` confirms at [#2535](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2535)**); bank workbook export split by `VN_BANKS`; and **account-level (`334`, `338*`, `3335`, `642*`) Finance Inbox handoff** keeping individual pay hidden from Accounting.
- **Declarations & recruiting (`/hr/declarations`, `/hr/recruiting`):** `D02-LT`, `05KK-TNCN`, `05QTT-TNCN`, `03/TNCN` preview & Excel exports (marked draft pending `#2535`, HTKK XML pending lawyer [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)), job openings, staged candidates, interviewer busy-slot check, hire-to-employee transition, and candidate PDPL erasure.
- **AI Coworker `Ly` (`packages/agent/src/meter.ts`):** `finance.payroll_prepare`, `finance.payroll_flag`, `finance.payroll_explain`, `finance.labor_check`, and `hr.recruiting_brief` (pseudonymized `P1`/`C1`).
- **Actively Building Now (Stage 5 Wave 1 & Queue, `2026-11-22`):** `5A` ([#4444](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4444) `Vy Today` HR alerts), `5C` ([#4447](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4447) Web/PWA 1-click check-in/out + office QR + payroll rollup), `5D` ([#4446](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4446) `crove-extract` + `Ly` CV parsing + Funnel BI + `Engage Zalo/FB` invite drafts), `5E` ([#4445](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4445) Continuous payroll fluctuation diff), `5F` ([#4351](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4351) post-erasure guards & a11y polish), and Wave 2 queued `5B` ([#4448](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/4448) onboarding checklist + 2-level OT requests -> payroll + probation check-in w/ `Projects` tasks).

## 3. Direct Matchup Against JOY's 7 Live MISA AMIS HRM Screenshots (`2026-11-22`)

Why JOY's screenshots matter directly for Ludy positioning in Vietnam:

1. **Price & Stack Comparison (`0 VND` license vs `37.8M-54M+ VND/yr` before Accounting & Recruiting):**
   - MISA AMIS HRM bundles `Standard` (`3.15M/mo` = `37.8M/yr`) and `Professional` (`4.5M/mo` = `54M/yr`) for `<=200` staff **and still excludes `AMIS Tuyen dung` (ATS) and `AMIS Ke toan` (`TT133/TT99` GL)**.
   - Ludy delivers Core HRIS, Payroll, Bank files, Declarations, ATS Recruiting, **and native double-entry `TT133/TT99` Accounting (`334/338/3335/642` isolated journals) + `Ludy Ky ket` e-contracts + `Ludy Cong tac`** in one `AGPL-3.0` self-hostable stack.
2. **PDPL `91/2025/QH15` + AI Law `134/2025/QH15` Architecture Advantage:**
   - Effective `2026-01-01` and `2026-03-01`, Vietnam's PDP Law (`91/2025/QH15`) mandates purpose minimization, erasing rejected candidates and post-retention ex-employees, biometric consent, and cross-border transfer impact assessments; Vietnam's AI Law (`134/2025/QH15`) establishes deployer accountability and human-oversight principles while Prime Minister Decision `33/2026/QD-TTg` lists 46 high-risk AI systems across 6 sectors without employment systems in that initial schedule (formal HR legal classification pending `#2536`) `[S-VN1, S-VN2]`.
   - Ludy self-hosts on `docker compose up`, pseudonymizes `P1`/`C1` prior to AI calls, forbids AI-generated payroll numbers or autonomous hiring/payroll commits, preserves `100%` AI-off parity, avoids mandatory FaceID biometric storage (`Stage 5C`), and provides cryptographic per-subject PDPL erasure (`subject_key_shred`).
3. **Where MISA Screenshots Highlight Features Ludy Still Needs (Even After Stage 5 Lands):**
   - Stage 5 (`5A`-`5F`, **now in progress**) closes **Vy `Hom nay` alerts** (`5A`), **Web/PWA + QR attendance** (`5C`), **CV PDF parse + Funnel BI + Zalo/FB invite drafts** (`5D`), **payroll diff preview** (`5E`), **onboarding checklist + 2-level OT requests + probation review w/ Projects tasks** (`5B`), and `#4351` polish (`5F`).
   - That leaves **4 concrete gaps directly visible in MISA screenshots** plus global AI-native leaders (candidates for Stage 6, **strictly awaiting JOY approval**):
     1. **`AI nop don tu` & Policy Q&A by chat (`MISA AVA [Hot]`, `Rippling AI`, `Bob AI`, `Gusto Gus`)**: employee types `"xin nghi phep chieu thu 6"` or asks `"phep nam con may ngay / che do thai san tinh sao?"` inside `/hr/my` or `Ly`/`Vy` chat -> `Ly` fills a draft request or answers from company handbook in `Ludy Ghi chu` with **clickable policy citations** (`human confirms submit`).
     2. **Payroll Retro/Offset/Advance Lines & Business Trip Requests (`truy thu`, `bu tru`, `tam ung theo ky`, `don cong tac` in MISA `Tien luong`/`Nhan vien`)**: structured mid-month salary advance (`tam ung`) & retro adjustment (`truy thu`/`bu tru`) feeding `decimal.js` net pay + business trip request (`don cong tac`) alongside leave/OT.
     3. **Contract Addendums, Career Timeline & Asset Handover (`phu luc tu dong`, `qua trinh cong tac`, `tai san ban giao` in MISA `Thong tin nhan su`)**: deterministic contract addendum PDF (salary/position change -> `Ludy Ky ket`), structured employment history timeline, and company asset handover checklist integrated into onboarding & Stage 2 termination/erasure.
     4. **Lightweight Quarterly OKR / Goal Tracking Tied to `Ludy Cong tac` (`AMIS Muc tieu` / `Lark OKR` / `Lattice`)**: simple Company -> Team -> Member quarterly objectives linked directly to `modules/projects` tasks as factual evidence (skipping heavy 360-degree / 9-box corporate calibration suites).

### 4.3 Explicitly Keep Out of Scope / Icebox ([scope.md](../product/scope.md))

1. **Global EOR, multi-country payroll engines, and multi-entity consolidation** (Workday / SAP / Deel / Rippling).
2. **IT asset / laptop shipping & MDM device provisioning** (Rippling IT / Warp IT).
3. **Biometric FaceID/fingerprint hardware & heavy manufacturing/F&B split-shift engines** (MISA / Tanca / FastWork factory modules: high `91/2025/QH15` biometric liability + hardware lock-in).
4. **Autonomous "hands-off" AI video scoring/rejection, auto-hiring, or autonomous tax filing** (Mercor / micro1 Zara AI / Paradox autonomous routing / Warp US agentic filing).
5. **Heavy LMS, 9-box talent calibration, & 360-degree corporate review matrices** (Workday / SAP / MISA `AMIS Danh gia` enterprise features / Lattice Grow).
