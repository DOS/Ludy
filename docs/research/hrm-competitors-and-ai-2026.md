# Global, Vietnam SME and AI-Native HRM Benchmark (2026-11-22)

Status: research benchmark, `2026-11-22` (at owner JOY's request; informs future HRM Stage 5 planning after Stage 4 completion on `main`). **Research only: no code and no roadmap commitment until the owner decides.** Standalone Vietnamese summary for the owner: [hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md](hrm-competitors-and-ai-2026.vi.md).

Lens: one-person companies (OPC), household businesses, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in Vietnam first ([scope.md](../product/scope.md)). Every capability below is weighed against Ludy's five fixed architecture constraints ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) section 1.1, [ai-first-reference-study.md](ai-first-reference-study.md)):

1. **Monthly OPC/SME fit:** shortens or protects an HR, timekeeping, leave, payroll, compliance, or hiring task a Vietnamese small business performs at least monthly.
2. **Self-contained `docker compose up` (AGPL-3.0):** runs in one PostgreSQL database with zero external SaaS dependencies, hardware lock-in, or per-seat license floor.
3. **Metered, tainted AI with 100% AI-off parity:** all model calls go through `packages/agent/src/meter.ts` (`withAiScope` + `metered()`), pseudonymize employee/candidate identifiers (`P1`, `C1`), undergo deterministic verification (zero hallucinated numbers), and work cleanly with AI off.
4. **Human approval on money, hires, and statutory commitments:** AI never approves a payroll run, sends a bank payment, sends a candidate offer/rejection, or files a statutory submission alone.
5. **One write path and strict Vietnam privacy/AI compliance:** every write routes through `Runtime.execute()` with tenant RLS, `legal_entity_id` scoping, PDPL erasure (`Law 91/2025/QH15`, effective `2026-01-01`), and mandatory human oversight for employment evaluation (`AI Law 134/2025/QH15`, effective `2026-03-01`).

How to read this document: Ludy capabilities reflect direct inspection of `origin/main` (Stages 1 to 4 shipped through PR [#4334](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4334) and PR [#4345](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/pull/4345), live in release `v0.3.7`). Competitor facts come from official product documentation and web search summaries collected on `2026-11-22` (`[S-*]` in section 6); items known only from search summaries, third-party trackers, or unconfirmed marketing pages are explicitly labeled `search summary`, `third party`, or `Unverified`.

## 1. Where Ludy Nhan su Stands Today (`origin/main` / `v0.3.7`)

Built across 11 `crove_hr.*` tables, 6 workspace routes, sealed PDPL columns (`SEALED_COLUMNS` + `PERSONAL_DATA_COLUMNS`), and 5 metered `Ly` (`COWORKER_BY_MODULE.hr = 'finance'`) AI features:

- **Core HRIS, contracts, and terminations (`modules/hr/src/{employees,contract-pdf,departments,labor-register}.ts`, `/hr/employees`):** employee & employment history, department structure with L1 manager assignment, dependent registry, deterministic Vietnamese Labor Contract PDF generation + `eSign` ceremony handoff, probation/contract timelines, Art. 46 severance and Art. 47 job-loss allowance (`employer-funded`), foreign worker tracking (resident 5-35% progressive vs non-resident 20% flat PIT, 12-month BHXH flag, masked work permit expiry alert), `So quan ly lao dong` + semi-annual `01/PLI` labor report, and retention-gated PDPL erasure (`hr.payroll.employee.erase`) that also wipes leave-request PII.
- **Leave, timekeeping, and multi-level self-service (`/hr/my`, `/hr/employees`, `modules/hr/src/{leave,leave-requests}.ts`):** annual leave ledger, CSV timekeeping import into payroll runs, `/o/$slug/hr/my` employee self-service portal for linked `member` roles (leave balance, submit/cancel with department overlap warning, view approved/paid/closed payslips only + deterministic VN rule breakdown when AI is off, L1 department manager approval queue `pending_l1 -> pending_final`), and final owner/admin approval atomically posting into `crove_hr.payroll_leave_entry` without allowing self-approval (except audited sole-owner OPC). `viewer` roles are blocked at UI, RPC, and PostgreSQL RLS levels.
- **Payroll calculation, bank files, and accounting handoff (`modules/hr/src/{payroll-run,bank-file,bank-template,payroll-posting,payroll-payment}.ts`, `/hr/payroll`, `/hr/bank-templates`):** monthly, hourly (`luong gio`), and piece-rate (`luong san pham`) calculation; statutory BHXH/BHYT/BHTN/union caps and sickness/maternity exclusion; progressive PIT (up to 7 tiers per existing codebase rules) & 13th-month bonus; **all newly encoded statutory rates stay marked `status: 'commentary'` until partner accountant `#61` confirms ([issue #2535](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2535))**. Reuses saved per-bank Excel templates (`buildBankFileWorkbook`), splits bulk bank files by `VN_BANKS`, verifies line sums against frozen net pay, and generates **account-level** (`334`, `338*`, `3335`, `642*`) accounting posting & payment proposals into the Finance Inbox so `Ke toan truong` approves journal vouchers without ever seeing individual salaries.
- **Declarations & recruiting (`/hr/declarations`, `/hr/recruiting`):** `D02-LT`, `05KK-TNCN`, `05QTT-TNCN`, `03/TNCN` preview & Excel exports (marked draft pending `#2535`, no HTKK XML yet pending lawyer [#2536](https://github.com/DOS/Ludy/issues/2536)), job openings, staged candidates, interviewer busy-slot calendar conflict check, standalone interview cancel, hire-to-employee transition, and candidate erasure.
- **AI Coworker `Ly` (`packages/agent/src/{payroll,labor,recruiting}-coworker.ts`, `meter.ts`):** `finance.payroll_prepare` (draft sheet diff from previous month), `finance.payroll_flag` (minimum wage, deduction cap, anomaly flags), `finance.payroll_explain` (payslip plain-Vietnamese narration checked against stored figures), `finance.labor_check` (compliance readiness narrative on `01/PLI`/register), and `hr.recruiting_brief` (pseudonymized alias `C1` screening brief + interview focus questions over scrubbed untrusted text).
- **Important structural gap on `origin/main`:** `TODAY_APPS` (`packages/kernel-contract/src/today.ts`) does **not** include `'hr'` yet, so expiring probation, ending contracts, expiring work permits (`workPermitAlertsFor`), and waiting leave approvals do not automatically surface onto `Vy`'s `Hom nay` (`Today`) feed. Mobile hardware/camera/GPS timeclocks, HTKK tax XML filing, OKR/KPI appraisal cycles, and multi-entity/EOR global payroll are absent.

## 3. Architectural Comparison: AI Coworkers, Privacy & Lock-In

Why `Ludy Nhan su` has an uncrowded moat against both domestic VN SaaS vendors (`MISA`, `Base`, `1Office`, `Tanca`, `FastWork`) and global AI startups (`Rippling`, `Deel`, `Warp`, `Paradox`):

1. **Double-entry VN Accounting + HR in One Database without Re-Keying (`ADR-0046`) vs Separate SaaS Bills:**
   - Base.vn lacks native Vietnamese accounting; Tanca & FastWork lack full TT133/TT99 general ledgers and VAT tax suites; global tools (`Rippling`, `Deel`, `BambooHR`, `HiBob`, `Warp`) have zero VN statutory `TT133`/`TT99`, `D02-LT`, `05QTT-TNCN`, `01/PLI`, or Vietnamese bank file support.
   - Only MISA AMIS rivals Ludy's Finance + HR coupling in Vietnam, yet MISA charges separately per module (`AMIS Ke toan` + `AMIS Cham cong` + `AMIS Tien luong` + `AMIS Nhan su` + `meInvoice` + setup/training fees) and keeps proprietary cloud lock-in. Ludy bundles all modules in one `AGPL-3.0` stack and enforces **strict salary isolation (`2026-11-08`)**: Finance approves `334/338/3335/642` totals in the Inbox while individual compensation stays inside HR RLS.
2. **PDPL `91/2025/QH15` + AI Law `134/2025/QH15` Compliance by Design vs Over-Promised AI:**
   - Effective `2026-01-01` and `2026-03-01`, Vietnam's PDP Law (`91/2025/QH15`) mandates purpose limitation, erasing rejected candidates and post-retention ex-employees, biometric consent, and cross-border transfer impact assessments; Vietnam's AI Law (`134/2025/QH15`) classifies automated employee/candidate evaluation as high-risk/human-oversight-required `[S-VN1, S-VN2]`.
   - Domestic & AI-native vendors frequently ship unredacted prompts to external LLM APIs or market zero-human autonomous screening/approvals. Ludy self-hosts via `docker compose up` (optional local/chosen OpenAI-compatible endpoint), **pseudonymizes HR sheets (`P1`/`C1`) before LLM calls**, forbids AI from creating numbers or hiring/payroll commits, includes `100%` AI-off deterministic fallbacks, and enforces cryptographic per-subject erasure (`subject_key_shred`) + `leave_request` PII wiping.
3. **True Open-Source Self-Service (`v0.3.7`) vs Per-Seat Tax:**
   - Every competitor charges PEPM or seat-tiered fees once headcount scales past starter caps (`$5-$25/user/mo` globally, `40k-169k VND/user/mo` locally). Even `Tanca` gates its AI layer on ongoing monthly add-ons and keeps closed source code; `Odoo Community` strips payroll, appraisals, and AI into paid Enterprise + third-party `OPL-1` modules.

## 4. Honest Gaps Where Competitors Lead Today

To beat MISA AMIS HRM, Base HRM+, Tanca, and global AI-native tools in daily Vietnamese SME adoption, Ludy Nhan su currently trails in five concrete operational workflows:

1. **Zero proactive HR nudges on `Vy Hom nay` (`Today`) & missing operational alerts:** `TODAY_APPS` does not yet include `'hr'`. Owners and admins are not warned proactively on Home when probation (`thu viec`) is within 7 days of ending, a fixed-term contract expires within 30 days, a foreign worker's `work_permit` is expiring, `01/PLI` semiannual filing opens, or self-service leave approvals are waiting. Competitors (Rippling, Base, Tanca, HiBob) treat proactive HR lifecycle nudges as table stakes.
2. **Everyday Attendance / Timekeeping Friction (CSV-only vs Mobile/Quick Check-in + Shift Roster):** FastWork, Tanca, Base, and 1Office capture daily attendance on phones (`GPS`, `WiFi`/`QR`, `FaceID`) and sync leave + overtime automatically into timesheets. Ludy requires importing an offline CSV monthly, has no mobile quick check-in for small teams, and has no lightweight shift roster (`lich ca`) or overtime request (`xin tang ca`) self-service alongside leave requests (`/hr/my`). *(Note: dedicated FaceID camera hardware & heavy factory rosters remain disproportionate for 5-50 person offices, whereas lightweight phone/web check-in + OT request fits weekly SME work.)*
3. **Recruiting Inbound Velocity & Funnel Analytics (`Zalo` / `Facebook` intake, AI CV extract, Pipeline KPIs):** Paradox Olivia, MISA AVA Recruiting, and Ashby excel at parsing raw uploaded CVs (PDF/DOCX -> structured candidate fields), showing funnel conversion & time-to-hire metrics, and engaging applicants directly over chat (`SMS`/`WhatsApp`, or **`Zalo OA` / `Facebook Page`** in Vietnam) using existing scheduling slots rather than manual re-typing of every applicant into `/hr/recruiting`.
4. **Continuous "What Changed This Month" Payroll Diff & Payslip Delivery (`#2539`):** HiBob and Warp let owners preview pay changes continuously mid-month with plain diffs vs prior month before locking (`ai-first-reference-study.md` pattern: `reason + numbers`), and MISA/Base/1Office email or push PDF payslips natively (Ludy payslips require logging into `/hr/my`, while `#2539` email delivery awaits lawyer `#2536` + accountant `#2535`).
5. **Lightweight Probation Check-in & Performance Evidence (`OKR / Danh gia thu viec`) Linked to `Ludy Cong tac`:** MISA, Base, 1Office, FastWork, and Lattice connect goals/tasks to formal reviews. Small Vietnamese SMEs do not need heavy 360-degree corporate appraisals, **but almost every monthly-active SME conducts end-of-probation (`danh gia het thu viec`) and simple semi-annual salary-review check-ins**, ideally pulling completed tasks from `modules/projects` as factual evidence (zero AI scoring).

### 5.2 Explicitly Keep Out of Scope / Icebox ([scope.md](../product/scope.md))

1. **Global EOR, multi-country payroll engines, and multi-entity consolidation** (Deel / Rippling / HiBob multi-country footprints): enterprise scope outside Vietnam OPC/SME core.
2. **IT asset / laptop shipping & MDM device provisioning** (Rippling IT / Warp IT): unrelated to Ludy's Business OS core for small Vietnamese firms.
3. **Biometric FaceID camera hardware & heavy manufacturing/F&B split-shift engines** (MISA / Tanca / FastWork factory modules): hardware lock-in plus high `91/2025/QH15` sensitive biometric liability.
4. **Autonomous "hands-off" AI candidate auto-rejection, auto-hiring, or autonomous tax filing** (Paradox enterprise routing / Warp US agentic filing): conflicts directly with Ludy's human-approval invariant and Vietnam `AI Law 134/2025/QH15` oversight mandate.
5. **Heavy LMS / 360-degree corporate competency calibration suites** (Lattice enterprise Grow / MISA ASK enterprise): too heavy for 1-to-50-person SMEs compared to lightweight probation + task check-ins.
